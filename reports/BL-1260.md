## 2. The ledger write has moved — and the fix is proven live, not just tested

`reserve()` is the single chokepoint every billed call already passes through. Metering now
happens **as calls are reserved**.

```python
used = {"calls": 0, "recorded": 0}     # `recorded` is a high-water mark

def _flush_spend(reason):
    delta = used["calls"] - used["recorded"]     # THE DELTA, never the running total
    ...
    used["recorded"] += delta                    # advanced ONLY on a write that landed

def reserve(n=1):
    ...
    used["calls"] += n
    if used["calls"] - used["recorded"] >= SPEND_CHECKPOINT_CALLS:
        _flush_spend("checkpoint")
```

and `discover()` is now wrapped in `try/finally` so a cap hit, a vendor 402 or any exception
out of the loop still books what it bought.

Three properties that are easy to get wrong and are pinned by tests:

- **The delta, not the total.** `record_aux_spend` is append-only and *not* idempotent.
  Passing the cumulative counter each flush would bill 1+2+3+…+n instead of n — the same
  double-count BL-923/BL-839 fixed on the main-run path.
- **A failed write leaves the calls OWED.** The flush swallows its own exception so a ledger
  fault cannot kill a paid run, but it does *not* advance the mark — otherwise a transient
  lock timeout would silently discard 25 requests of accounting.
- **A run that spends nothing writes no row**, and does not bring the ledger into existence.

`SPEND_CHECKPOINT_CALLS = 25` — $0.015 of exposure per kill, and 40 rows on a 1,000-request
run rather than 1,000.

### The live proof

The volume run in §5 went through the shipped `run_funnel`. Mid-run:

  21:07:04 tiktok_finder_checkpoint  25 calls  $0.0150

## 4. The free pre-sort: DO NOT SHIP IT ON TIKTOK

BL-1261 measured, on 100 Instagram pages against his marks: four DOM signals killed **28 of
81** true rejects, killed **0 of 19** WANTs, precision 100% [87.9, 100.0], at $0.00. That is
a real result and it is a result **about Instagram**.

### Three of the four signals do not survive the port

| signal | IG kills | on TikTok |
|---|---:|---|
| `news_category` | 5 | **DEAD** — TikTok has no account-category field at all |
| `two_business_words` | 9 | **POLARITY TRAP + over-count** (below) — fires **0 times** |
| `follows_1500_plus` | 14 | ports, and this leg had to add `followingCount` to `profile_of()` first |
| `ratio_under_5` | 7 | ports |
| still-picture | not gated | **DEAD** — every TikTok tile is a video cover, so clip-share ≡ 1.0 |

**The polarity trap is the one that would have done damage.** Instagram's business-word list
contains `dm for promo`, `paid promo`, `advertise`, `sponsor`, `brand deals`. On Instagram
those are a **kill** signal. In `tiktok_finder`, `advertises_promo()` treats precisely those
phrases as a **qualifying** signal, and `split_lists()` builds the promo-only DM list out of
them — *no address, but the bio says they already sell placement*. Porting the list verbatim
would kill the pages this funnel exists to find. Every promo term was removed.

**And the rule over-counted.** The Instagram list has overlapping stems, so one phrase scores
twice: "records label" hits `label` **and** `records`; "booking" hits `booking` **and**
`bookings`. On BL-1261's own data **6 of its 9 kills were a single stem doubling** — "2+
business words" operationally meant *"the bio says booking"*, one signal wearing two hats.
Gating on distinct stem groups makes "2+" mean 2. It then fires **zero times** on TikTok.

(Also added: `_plain()` NFKD folding. TikTok bios are routinely written in U+1D400
Mathematical Alphanumeric glyphs, on which a plain `.lower()` substring test scores zero —
silently, so the rule would look clean and be blind.)

### The result: it kills 23% of the yield

**Every one of the 35 pages was judged, including the ones the pre-sort would have killed** —
that is the whole reason the saving was not taken. The kills could then be checked instead of
assumed.

| rule | kills | **WANTs killed** | precision | would save |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| union of the surviving signals | 11 | **3** | 72.7% | $0.4695 |
| soft-no (≥2 flags, or 1 flag and no email) | 11 | **3** | 72.7% | $0.4695 |
| `follows_1500_plus` alone | 7 | **3** | 57.1% | $0.2988 |

**No variant ships.** The three WANTs it destroys are `munteanumadalin67`,

`follows_1500_plus` is the reversal worth naming: BL-1261's single strongest signal, 14 of 28

## 6. Capture at volume — it holds, and the obvious reading of the data is wrong

A real run through the **shipped** `run_funnel` (`-m clippershq.run --funnel tiktok --cap
0.25`), so it validated the capture method and the new checkpointing at once.

```
317 video(s) -> 265 author(s); 207 reachable by handle search        (78.1%)
135 profile(s) bought, 135 passed at min_videos=3, 14 with an email, 4 promo-only
134 contact sheet(s) built, 0 refused, 1044 tile(s), 1 cover(s) missed
416 billed request(s) = $0.2496        stopped: cap $0.25 reached
master: 14 offered -> 8 appended, 6 merged by identity
```

**1 failed cover in 1,045 attempted — a 0.1% error rate. The method holds at volume.**

It would have been easy to report the opposite. Measuring tile fill directly:

| | sheets | fill | full 12/12 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| first leg | 35 | 77.4% | 54.3% |
| volume run | 134 | 64.9% | 29.1% |

A 12.5pp fill drop and the share of complete sheets halving looks exactly like a method
degrading under load. It is not. **A blank tile has two causes and they mean opposite
things**: a cover that would not fetch (a failure), or an account that simply has fewer than
12 videos (`contact_sheet_for` iterates `videos[:12]`, so a 6-video account legitimately draws
6 tiles). Decomposed against the run's own `covers_missed`:

```
tiles drawn                                                1044
tiles lost to a FAILED COVER FETCH                            1
tiles never attempted because the account HAS no more videos  563
```

**All of it is thin accounts.** The fill number is a fact about TikTok's supply, not about the
capture. (Population B also ran with OCR off and therefore fetched faster, under more CDN
pressure — a confound, not a control, which makes the 0.1% result stronger rather than weaker.)

**The real limit is thin sheets, not failed fetches.** 7.8 tiles per sheet on average, only
29.1% complete — and §4 showed a thin sheet turning a page with a qualifying clip into
UNSURE. That is where the next improvement is, and it is a *supply* problem (how many videos
the handle search returns), not a capture problem.

### A metering discrepancy, in the safe direction

The probe's own before/after receipt reads matched its reservations **exactly** (35 = 35).
The volume run reserved 416 and the vendor counted **377** — 39 requests, **9.4%, over**-recorded.
`reserve()` deliberately counts attempts rather than successes, so a retry storm cannot be
under-metered; on the discovery path some reservations evidently never became HTTP requests.
The ledger therefore errs **high** on discovery, never low. Not fixed here — it is a real
finding, it costs nothing, and changing metering direction mid-round is how the opposite bug
gets introduced.

| | agreement | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|

| the decision actually taken (WANT vs not) | **70.0%** | [39.7, 89.2] |

**Read the confound before reading the number.** The two arms did not see the same image —
each capture rebuilt the grid from whatever `/v2/search` returned at that moment, so tile

possible. Before the free path is trusted for a gating decision, that wants a proper test.

**The 77 WANTs on the sheet are therefore not all equally sourced**, and the sheet says which
is which through the reason cell. Where a handle exists in both arms the **paid** verdict is
the one that ships — it is metered, and its independence is enforced rather than requested.

## 11. What I did not do, and what is still open

- **The paid judging did not have to be paid.** $1.451153 bought 34 verdicts that the free
  sub-agent path returns for $0.00 (§7b). The finding landed mid-round; the money was already
  gone. A future TikTok round should default to the free path and reach for the paid one only
  for unattended runs, per-call receipts and truncation detection — which is what it actually
  buys.
- **The free arm's independence is instructed, not enforced**, and its four overlap
  disagreements all run one way. That wants a sheet-constant test before the free path gates
  anything (§7b).
- **`master_csv` is the wrong config key.** `run_funnel` reads `config.get("master_csv")`; the
  real key is `master_file`. It falls back to a CWD-relative literal, so a run started from
  another directory appends to the wrong file. One word. Left alone because it is outside what
  this round set out to change and it is currently harmless (the funnel runs from the root).
- **Hashtag discovery costs 2×depth, and the estimator assumes 1+depth** — the tag is
  re-resolved on every page. The pre-spend estimate under-counts discovery by a factor of two.
- **The "not talking" rule is dead code**: `judge_author` computes `talking` from
  `speech_fracs`, and nothing in the module ever produces or passes it.
- **The pre-sort is still not shipped anywhere**, and on this evidence it should not be — on
  TikTok. BL-1261's Instagram result stands untouched.
