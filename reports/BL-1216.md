Position five. Not pinned. 1,241,664 views. Others at 758, 680, 557, 521, 405, 397. Printed

**"The latest 10" is false on 68% of pages.** Across 628 stored verdicts: 427 counted fewer
than ten posts, 17 counted exactly **one**, and 78 counted **zero** — on those 78 the view
gate silently never runs and the card says "unknown views" beside a list of real numbers.

### Median or mean — decided on evidence, against his stated preference

`reports/BL-1206.md:26-34`: on the 18 pages where the two disagree at the 1,000 floor, **the
median agrees with him 13 times to the mean's 5.** My independent recomputation on the current

pages. The `18/13/5` split itself lives only in that report's prose; no per-page artifact was
published, so it is **plausible and unfalsified but not independently verifiable**.

**So the gate keeps the median and the label stops lying.** The rejection sentence now reads:

```
median 619 views over the 8 non-pinned post(s) older than 48h (mean 155730), below the 1000
floor; 3 pinned post(s) excluded, which alone read 30034
```

Both statistics, both named, the **real** sample size instead of the boilerplate "latest 10",
and the pinned inflation quoted beside it. `_mean_views_for_display` now takes the same
pinned-stripped, latest-N sample the median took, preferring the judge's own stored

**What I did not fix:** sites 7, 8 and 9 — the TikTok mean, the `quality_gate` dict key, and

## 2. PROMO — cut 5.6×, and the cost is stated

**The real bug was the surface, not the threshold.** `_post_text` (`meme_finder.py:941`)
returns the **caption** whenever a post has no drawn text, so a rule documented as
"drawn text only" silently judged captions on exactly those posts. **9 of his 93 false rejects
carry the reason "1 of 10 captions advertise something (10% >= the 10% veto)"** — the reason
string says *captions* because that is literally what was read. He has repudiated caption-promo
twice in writing:

> *"this is not promotion this is good if there is promotion in caption we dont care about
> caption pronotion only text on the video promotion so remeber that"*
> *"it is a bit promotional but its ok if there is at least 1 video not promotinal"*

`PROMO_DRAWN_ONLY = True` makes a caption-only post **abstain** instead of voting.

| | rejections on 610 marked pages |
|---|---:|
| before | **50** (1 in 12) |
| after | **9** (1 in 68) |
| his stated rate | ~1 in 600 |

**It does not reach 1-in-600 and I did not force it there.** The remaining 9 are pages whose
*drawn lines* advertise, which is the thing he says he does reject. Getting to 1-in-600 needs
the threshold raised as well, and switching promo off entirely measures **67.70% / 69.05%** —
worse than leaving it at 9. The correctness fix is defensible; the extra tuning is not.

## 3. DIALOGUE — the rule is CORRECT and the data is missing

RULE 8's logic **already implements the exact test he restated**, and has since BL-1193:

```python
measured = [p for p in posts if p.get("speech_frac") is not None]
clean    = [p for p in measured if not _speech_is_talking(p.get("speech_frac"))]
talky    = bool(measured) and not clean          # ONE clean video rescues the page
```

That is *"if there is at least one video where there is speech AND a song, that's a
lifesaver"*. Nothing needed rewriting.

**It fires zero times because `speech_frac` is never written.** Verified three ways:

| artifact | posts | carrying `speech_frac` |
|---|---:|---:|
| `scratch/bl1207/fresh50_pages.jsonl` | 563 | **0** |
| `scratch/bl1206_fresh_pages.jsonl` | 387 | **0** |
| `scratch/bl1196_pages.jsonl` | 4,825 | **0** |
| `scratch/bl1201_pages.jsonl` | 1,255 | **1,238** — but that file has **no verdicts** |

`speech_frac` is produced only by `clip_speech.py` inside the **clip** pipeline. The
**meme-page** pipeline (`posts_for` → `parallel_judge.judge_one` → `judge_page`) never calls
it. BL-1201 did run VAD over 1,238 posts and the enrichment was never wired back.

**And the rule is green in tests because the tests inject the field the runner never
produces** (`test_dialogue_state.py`, `test_rule_reachability.py` both synthesise
`speech_frac=0.9`). That is exactly how a dead rule survives a reachability suite.

**I did not ship a substitute, and here is why with numbers.** The energy read already failed
against his marks — 29.2% [14.9, 49.2] against a 30.9% base rate. The text heuristic
`looks_like_dialogue` fires on **20 of 3,956** judged lines in `bl1196` and 0 of 465 in
fresh50; it cannot carry the rule. The honest fix is a measurement pass: attach `speech_frac`
in `parallel_judge.judge_one` and re-judge. **Cost: ~6,000 posts × audio decode + Silero VAD.**
Until that runs, dialogue is unmeasurable, and *"unmeasured is never no-speech"* — the verdict
already says `dialogue_state: "unmeasured"` rather than 0%.

This is the single largest hole in the filter: **31 of 101 false accepts are dialogue.**

## 6. CREATORS — and @centregoals, which the brief and my own last report both got wrong

**@centregoals was rejected by `creator_page`, and by nothing else.** Its stored verdict is
`"fired": ["creator_page"]`, reason *"a person's page, not a repost page: no repost signal in
the bio or handle, and verified"*. `news_share 0.4 < 0.60` so `news_media` never fired;
`meme_share 0.30 >= 0.15` so `format_share` never fired.

The brief says *"No person rule was involved"* and **my own BL-1213 report said it died at
meme_share 0.3 to the NEWS and SYNOPSIS classifiers.** Both are wrong. The NEWS classifier
deflated its hook share; the **person rule killed it**, and `verified` was the only mark that
fired — bio *"The home of European football"* misses the repost pattern, handle `centregoals`
misses it, and 151,070 followers is below the 300,000 exemption.

`CREATOR_USE_VERIFIED = False` drops the blue tick from the person test. Measured:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| @centregoals | REJECTED, `fired: [creator_page]` | **PASSES**, `fired: []` |
| its NEWS verdicts | 4 of 10 | **1 of 10** |
| its hooks / share | 3 / 0.30 | **4 / 0.40** |
| `creator_page` rejections, 610 pages | 76 | **58** |

**The NEWS fix is what moved its hook count.** `_NEWS_RE` contains `\bvia @` and
`\bcredit\s*[:\-]\s*@` — **repost-credit markers, not reporting markers** — and the caption
branch has *no* person-anchor veto, so any caption ≥200 chars ending "Via @somebody" is
classified NEWS. Three of @centregoals' four NEWS verdicts came from captions; one came from
the word *"breaking"* inside *"breaking countless records"*. `NEWS_USE_CAPTION = False` removes
the caption arm. This is the **only change that improves agreement on its own**: 69.18 → 69.51
overall, 71.03 → **71.83** on held-out.

And the evidence says NEWS should never have been a negative: **a NEWS post appears on 48% of
the pages he WANTS against 20% of those he rejects.** He says so himself — *"yes its a bit a
report news page but its ok if there is a couple of meme clips"*.

**I did not rebuild the recurring-subject signal, and must not.** `tests/test_bl1200_creator.py`
enforces by AST that `creator_signals` is never imported, because it scores 29.0% [20.8, 38.9]
train / 27.6% [17.8, 40.2] held-out against a ~40% bar. A recurring subject appears on 34% of
