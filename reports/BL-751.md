## 1. The full path, and what each stage discards

```

        |     1. verdict in {EDITOR, LIKELY}     <-- HARD GATE
        |     2. exclude_business (acct type 2)
        |     3. quality_gate.is_curation_page(bio)
        |     4. _first_email() must be deliverable
        |     5. bio-link custom-domain email quarantine
        |     6. crossdedup.collapse_records
        |     7. sort ALIVE > UNKNOWN > DORMANT, personal > role, then reach  (cuts nothing)
        |
        +-- writer.dm_list_rows()            -> DM_LIST_*.csv      42,658
        |     1. verdict in {EDITOR, LIKELY}     <-- SAME HARD GATE
        |     2. has NO deliverable email (an emailable lead goes to BOT_READY instead)

        |     4. dedup by profile_link
        |
        +-- send_list.build()                -> output/<CAMPAIGN>_SEND.csv
              email required; bio-link quarantine; ALIVE+emailable only;
```

**Structurally unable to reach either list: 13,067 / 57,386 = 22.8%.**

That headline number needs splitting, because two very different things are inside it:

- **11,020 rows** are `UNSURE` / `CREATOR?` from the crawl funnels. Those are **deliberate quality decisions** — the pipeline looked and was not convinced. Working as designed.
- **2,047 rows** are the six non-crawl funnels: 2,005 with an **empty** verdict and 42 marked `MEME_PAGE`. **These were never judged at all.** No gate rejected them; no gate was ever asked.

`repost_finder` is the sharpest illustration. It writes a verdict — `MEME_PAGE` — on 100% of its rows. It is the *only* non-crawl funnel that populates the field, and it still exports zero, because `MEME_PAGE` is not in `BOT_READY_VERDICTS = {EDITOR, LIKELY}`. A meme page is exactly what that funnel is *supposed* to find. The vocabulary and the gate disagree.

## 6. What this means

The lead-*finding* work is in good shape — 57,386 rows, and the two crawl funnels produce a genuinely sendable list. **The last mile is where the loss is, and it is one field.**

Ranked by what it costs:

1. **Six funnels export zero** because they never write `verdict` (§2). 2,037 rows, every one of them paid for. `repost_finder` writes `MEME_PAGE`, which the gate does not recognise — decide whether meme pages should be sendable and make the vocabularies agree.
2. **9,873 rows have a profile URL and are blocked by verdict alone** (§3a) — the single largest recoverable pool in the audit.
3. **727 rows need only a derivable profile URL** (§3b), 492 of them the already-paid-for Spotify handles.
4. **`email_hint` is 0% everywhere** (§4) — the agency/verify warnings never reach master. Harmless today only because the affected funnels export nothing; fix it in the same change.
5. **27 agency domains would send as personal** (§5).

**Is the system ready to use?** For the TikTok crawl, yes — 1,463 ready-to-send leads with real reach and liveness signals, and nothing has been sent yet. For everything else, no, and the reason is a single unwritten column rather than anything about lead quality.

## Honest limits

- **No message was sent and no export was run to disk.** The counts come from calling the exporters in-process on the real master; the files on disk in `output/` were not regenerated or inspected.
- **`send_list.build()` was read, not executed.** Its campaign-attribution step drops AMBIGUOUS/UNATTRIBUTED rows, so the per-campaign SEND files will be *smaller* than the 1,576 figure. I did not quantify that additional loss — it needs a run with a config, which would have written files.
- **"Ready to send" is a structural judgement, not a deliverability one.** No address was verified as live; `_first_email` only checks format and known-bad patterns.
- Agency-domain detection in §5 is a keyword match on the domain, so it will both miss agencies with neutral names and flag the occasional legitimate personal domain.
- The spread sample is evenly spaced across the ranked list (deterministic, reproducible), not random.
