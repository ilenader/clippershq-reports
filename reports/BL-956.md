## 2. The merge fields: **there is no template, and the brief's premise is wrong**

The brief says the email side has its own equivalent of the 21 hand-marked hook windows. **It does not.** `grep -ril "template|subject|merge.?field|first_name"` across `clippershq/`, `docs/` and `dashboard/` returns nothing relevant. There is no subject line, no body, no greeting anywhere in this repository.

What exists is the merge-field **columns** — `display_name`, `fandom_display`, `followers`, `clip_views`, `clip_date`, `rank_median_views` — added to the send file by BL-788→790 and **never consumed by anything**. ## 5. BLOCKERS between here and one real send

1. **There is no message.** No template, subject or greeting exists. This is the whole gap; everything else is a detail beside it.
2. **Only 11% of rows merge cleanly**, and **all 1,402 client rows** carry a funnel name where the fandom should be. A client send today reads *"we cut short-form for Spotify_Finder."*
3. **The primary is not a superset** — 51 addresses in 5 other files, and ~20 subset files still on disk that would double-send.
4. **Two audiences, one file**, and nothing enforces branching on `lead_kind`.
5. **A second touch cannot be recorded** — already-sent rows are refused, so `touch_number=2` never lands.
6. **Expect ~1 bounce in 10.** Suppression removes only CERTAIN bounces: ~1.2% of the export against an observed ~10% rate. The MX table covers 141 probed domains; absent means UNKNOWN, never dead.
7. **No sent-log outside master.** An automation pointed at this file re-mails all 2,980 unless it dedupes on its own state.

### You cannot get 100 clean messages out of this file today. You can get 86.

Filtering to `clipper` + real name + present `followers` + present `clip_views` + a spaced `fandom_display` leaves **194 rows (6.5%)**, and **86 (2.9%)** that merge into something you could read aloud. That is the honest size of a first send.

## Honest limits

- **Nothing was sent, and nothing in this repo can send.** Step 7 is external.
- **The template is mine, written for this audit.** It is not a proposal for what to actually send; it exists so the fields appear in a sentence.
- **`_NUM_OK` and the name/fandom rules are heuristics I wrote**, refined twice against real output. They are a floor on the problem, not a ceiling — a merge fault I did not think to look for is not in the table.
- **The soft fandom rule over-fires** on genuinely one-word titles and is excluded from the headline count.
- **I did not fix any of it.** The brief asked for verification, rendering, a round trip, timings and blockers. Repairing `fandom` for client rows, writing a template, and deleting the decoy files are all real work and none of it is done.
