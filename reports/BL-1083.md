## 1. THE BAR, STATED BEFORE ANY CLIP WAS OPENED

> **Would a 100k repost page post this UNEDITED?**
> **`no`** — competitor watermark/handle/branding burned in; auto or karaoke captions;
> foreign-language text; severe dead canvas; unreadable or very low-resolution footage;
> landscape framing in a vertical feed; a dense unreadable text/table; a source that will not
> decode; brand-unsafe content.
> **`fix`** — footage is fine, framing or trim needs work.
> **`post`** — coherent subject, legible, no foreign text, no competitor mark, acceptable framing.

**The one judgement call that moves numbers, stated so a later judge can reproduce it:**
legible film **dialogue** subtitles are the format and **pass**; word-by-word **karaoke or
transcript** captions **fail**.

**Stratification:** accounts round-robin, fewest-watchable first, **zero-label accounts placed
first in every pass** — 30 accounts that had never been labelled are now covered. No account
supplies more than ~7% of my rows.
