## 3. What happens at exhaustion

All four contact funnels carry `source_drained` as an explicit stop reason, and `control.py:1401` renders it. **So none of them fails silently at the source level — but only after everything else has been tried.**

Spotify is the instructive case. `stop_reason` is initialised to `source_drained` *before the worklist loop* and only overwritten by `target_met` or `dollar_cap`. The worklist contains seeds **plus** related-artist expansion, so "drained" fires only once expansion is also exhausted. The run therefore degrades quietly through a worse population first and reports honestly second.

**The three behaviours, mapped:**

| behaviour | funnel | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| silently returns worse leads, then reports drained | **Spotify** | −17 pt handle rate, invisible in the summary |
| reports drained at the source | Twitch, YouTube, Google Play | honest, but the operator must read it |
| bounded by deadline, not supply | Twitch (`DEFAULT_MAX_RUN_SECONDS` 900) | a run can end without touching supply at all |

Nothing warns *before* a run that the target exceeds the pool. That single check — compare target against measured pool at ask-time — is the highest-value guard in this report and was already specified in BL-766.

## 4. Refresh rate

**Refresh is real and large, and I can prove that without being able to quantify it per week.**

Of today's 966-artist Spotify seed pool, only **389 (40.3%) have ever been delivered**; **577 (59.7%) are new to us.** Meanwhile only 389 of the 1,311 artists ever delivered are still in the pool. The playlists have rotated substantially across the nine days of operation.

But 59.7% fresh is a *stock* measurement, not a *rate*. It conflates genuine playlist rotation with artists the runs simply never reached. **Separating those needs two snapshots a week apart, and one exists.**

Estimates by funnel, honestly labelled:

| funnel | refresh mechanism | status |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | Fresh Finds rotate weekly; RADAR slower | **measurable next week** — snapshot below |
| Twitch | different streamers live at different hours | **plausibly the largest**, entirely unmeasured |
| IG/TikTok | new posts continuously | unmeasured; the 2,519-tag pool is re-walkable |
| Google Play | new app releases | unmeasured |
| YouTube | continuous | irrelevant at zero output |

**The measurement to start today** — free, one file, no code change: store today's 966 seed artist IDs (already captured in `scratch/bl769_full_pool.json` and the overlap set) with a date. Re-fetch in seven days. New IDs ÷ 7 is the daily seed refresh, and that number *is* the sustainable Spotify rate. The same pattern applied to Twitch — snapshot live channel IDs at two times of day — would bound the largest unmeasured supply in the system.

## 5. The honest answer

**Run everything to exhaustion today and you add roughly 1,200–2,000 email-bearing leads**, dominated by Twitch if you widen its categories (~736) and Spotify's remaining seeds (~464), plus whatever the IG/TikTok crawl yields at its measured 5.2% email rate. You would roughly **double** the 4,208 addresses you hold.

**It would cost about $2 in cash and, on the only funnel where time is measured, something like 6–10 hours of wall clock.** The cash is irrelevant; the wall clock is the constraint.

**Afterwards, the sustainable weekly rate is unknown, and that is the single most important gap in this report.** It is also the cheapest to close: two snapshots, seven days apart, no code.

**Three things I would do before generating another lead**, in order:
1. **Widen `twitch_finder.categories` from 2 to the measured 60.** Cheapest supply in the system, currently throttled by a config list.
2. **Warn at ask-time when the target exceeds the measured pool.** Spotify has already run 1.69× past its seeds with no warning.
3. **Take the refresh snapshot.** Free, and without it "sustainable rate" stays a guess.

**And find out why YouTube has produced zero.** A funnel that has been built, tested and shipped and has never delivered a lead is either misconfigured or should be retired — either answer is worth having.
