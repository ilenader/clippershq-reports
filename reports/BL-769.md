## 3. Pool size — and a correction to my own BL-766

**I measured the pool at the wrong depth in BL-766.** `SpotifyScraper.get_playlist` takes `max_tracks`, which **defaults to 100**. Three of the seven seeds are longer than that, so the default silently truncates:

| seed | fetched at default | actual length |
|---|---|---|
| RADAR UK & IE | 100 | **291** |
| Fresh Finds Electronic | 100 | **272** |
| Fresh Finds R&B | 100 | 141 |

At the default, 533 of 937 tracks — **57% of the catalogue**. That is where BL-766's 599 unique artists came from, and this round reproduces it exactly at the same depth.

**But a real run does not use the default.** `control.py:2081` sets `per_seed = max(50, pool_cap)` where `pool_cap = target × run_over_provision_factor` (3). A 1,000-lead target therefore passes `max_tracks=3000` and fetches **whole playlists**. Verified live: asking for 300 on RADAR UK & IE returns all 291.

Measured at the depth a real run actually uses:

| depth | unique artists | in-band (80.4%) |
|---|---|---|
| 100-track default (BL-766's figure) | 599 | ~481 |
| **full playlists (what a 1,000-target run fetches)** | **967** | **~777** |

**Your safe run_target is ~777, not ~452.** With the pooled rate's 95% CI [68.2%, 88.7%], the honest range is **659 – 857**.

### What this does to BL-766's conclusion

BL-766 argued the 30 July run's 1,000-lead target was 2.2× the seed pool and the overflow had to come from related-artist expansion. **The direction survives; the magnitude does not.** The real ratio is 1,000 / 777 = **1.29×**, so expansion was still required — but for roughly 223 leads, not 548.

That leaves a genuine tension I am not going to paper over: BL-766's mixing model implied only ~267 of 1,000 leads came from seeds, yet ~777 in-band seed artists were available. Both cannot be right. The likely reconciler is dedup against master plus the country/language gates thinning the pool further at scale — but **the honest position is that the two estimates disagree and the per-lead `source` field BL-761 added is what settles it on the next run.** The 17-point handle-rate penalty for expansion (93.5% vs 76.6%) is unaffected either way; it was measured directly.

## Limits

- **n = 8 per seed.** The confidence intervals are wide: 8/8 is [68, 100], 6/8 is [41, 93], 3/8 is [14, 69]. **Individual seed scores are directional, not precise** — a one-seed swing of ±2 is inside noise. What carries weight is that Fresh Finds Country landed 3/8 on two independent runs and that its five below-floor artists are named and verifiable.
- **The per-seed pool shares multiply a full artist count by an n=8 rate**, so they inherit that imprecision. Treat 34.1% vs 27.9% as "these two dominate", not as a ranking.
- **Artist counts sum to 1,002 across seeds but 967 unique** — 6 artists appear on more than one seed at default depth; the per-seed shares double-count them slightly.
- **The 80.4% pooled rate includes the country and language gates**, which happened to reject nobody in this sample. On a seed set that surfaced non-Anglophone artists the in-band pool would be smaller than 777.
- **Playlists refresh continuously.** This is a 2026-07-31 snapshot; Fresh Finds rotates weekly.
- **I did not change config, retire any seed, search for replacements, or touch `spotify_finder.py` / `control.py`.** The only files written are `scratch/bl769_*` and the harness's own `scratch/spotify_audit/seeds.json`.
