## 2. FORTY MATCHES, HAND-AUDITED — AND THE BASELINE IS NOT COMPARABLE

**The definition, stated, because MEMEBOT-093's lesson is that an unmethodded number cannot be
checked:**

- **RIGHT** — the mood is a defensible fit for what is on screen; a viewer would not be surprised.
- **WEAK** — the mood is not contradicted, but it rests on a thin or incidental signal (a title,
  one word in a narration) and another mood would fit as well or better.
- **WRONG** — the mood contradicts the clip; a viewer would notice.

Stratified across tiers and songs, judged from the stored `vision_scene` and `track_title`:

| | RIGHT | WEAK | WRONG |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| VISION_RULE (24) | **11** | 9 | 4 |
| TRACK_TITLE_MOOD (16) | **3** | 10 | 3 |
| **all 40** | **14 (35.0%)** | 19 | 7 (17.5%) |

**Against the 56.1% / 68.8% baseline I cannot claim to beat or miss it, because the bar is not
the same — and saying so is the finding.** Under a *lenient* bar (count anything not
contradicted) this batch is **33/40 = 82.5%** overall and **13/16 = 81.3%** on track-title. The
published 68.8% sits between my strict 18.8% and my lenient 81.3%, which means the two numbers
came from different definitions and comparing them would be arithmetic dressed as evidence.

**On its own terms the audit says the track-title tier is the weak one.** Three of sixteen are
RIGHT under a strict bar; ten are title-driven matches over clips whose content is unrelated —
*"It Snows Silently"* over Young Sheldon banter, *"Jolly Morning"* over Bender. Three are
outright WRONG, including *"In Another Lifetime"* over an explicitly humorous Homer kidnapping
and *"Me and the Devil (slowed)"* over a **static ratings graphic**.

**The VISION tier's failure mode is different and worth naming separately: commentary.** Four of
its cases are talking-head or interview clips — a lore explainer about Valyrian steel, a prophecy
narration, a Ryan Reynolds interview, and a montage of *game logos* that matched on `katana`. The
vision rules fire on words in a **description of speech**, not on action.

**One clip in the sample matched on `track_title` with an EMPTY `vision_scene`** — nothing has
ever looked at it and a song was still chosen. `require_vision` guards `pick_song`; it does not
guard the title tier.

## Method / limits

**Judged from stored text, not from frames.** `vision_scene` and `track_title` are what the
matcher itself reads, so this audits its inputs faithfully — but a description can be wrong about
its own clip, and some of my 40 calls would flip if it is. A frame-level pass is a different and
more expensive audit.

**n = 40, stratified, largest cells first.** The two smallest cells (`sng_0002`,
`FRANCHISE_MOOD`) are represented by 2 clips each, so nothing here separates them from noise.

**"Reachable" is supply, not rotation.** It says a clip exists that would select each song; it
does not say rotation will reach every window before the operator posts, and no render tested it.
