## 2. Phase 1: research (written before any spend)

  - Neither has a sort, region or language parameter.
  - Search, followers, comments, likes, following and suggested were all measured dead in BL-1614.
- **Sort order, measured free on BL-1625's 156,103 saved items: not newest-first.**
  - Page 0's median post is 556 days old; 8.6% are ≤ 14 days.

## 6. The plan, with numbers

1. **Every new tag: v1 first.** That is 6 pages for ~$0.0036, against 0.15 new emails per page on name tags. Walk v2 deep only if v1 found a new email: D measured 3× the yield on such tags.
2. **Name lists, not captions.** Asian actor / idol and title-edit name lists ran $16.6–27 per 1,000. Caption tags ran $53–152.
3. **Expected:** about **$17–25 per 1,000**, for as long as fresh name lists last. Each 100 productive name tags gives ~25 editors (C3: 12 from 51).
4. **$15 needs about 4,000 productive name tags per 1,000 editors.** No such stock exists in the ledger. Of 17,383 v2 tags, 1,650 ever showed an email, and those are now walked on v1 too.
5. **Cheaper judging** (decision C) cuts feed and judge cost, about 18% of the money (940 feeds + $0.105 judge). It cannot move pages and lookups, which are about 80% (4,633 pages + 302+ lookups of 6,043 requests).

## 7. What I got wrong

- **I trusted the router's 80% precision prior for Phase 3's first $0.84.**
  - The pools' real precision was 6–45%.
  - Eye checks of the first sheets caught it. I stopped p3 by its STOP file, then made the router use eye precision from 5 checks.
  - The bad pools (capids, names, kpop captions) still cost ~$0.44 before they were cut.
- **The router then cut two good pools on lagging eye data** (older_rich at "$30.4", asia on the prior). Re-measured with all their sheets they were $21–26. I restarted on them (p3c) until they ran out.
- **The v1 loader skipped every v2-walked tag** (it checked both ledger keys). The first c3 launch walked nothing and cost $0. Fixed: a v1 arm only checks the v1 key.
- **`gen3.py` overwrote its own Phase-3a tag list** when I added the 3b mode (a path substitution went wrong). The walked 3a tags were rebuilt from `pages.jsonl` (154 tags); nothing was paid twice.
- **My per-arm script first double-counted Phase 3 pool dollars**, because p3b/p3c carry p3's counters. Fixed (max, not sum) before any number here was written.
- **D scanned 206 of 300 tags,** not 300. Its $0.24 budget went to 115 paid lookups (the edit-app terms had no free ids).
- **E was not "fresh":** most of those names were walked in BL-1625.
