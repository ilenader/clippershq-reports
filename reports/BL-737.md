**`GET /api/v1/credits`** → `total_credits: 0`, `total_usage: 0.016335`
**`GET /api/v1/key`** → HTTP 200, `is_free_tier: True`, `limit: 30`, `limit_remaining: 30`

So the key is **valid and authenticates fine** — it is a free-tier key with a 30-request monthly allowance and **no purchased credit**. On an audio request OpenRouter returns:

> `402 — "This request requires at least $0.50 in balance for audio"`

**That floor is flat and cannot be engineered around.** The first attempt failed on a different 402 — a `max_tokens` reserve ("you requested up to 65535 tokens, but can only afford 24488") — which *is* avoidable. The remedy hint even suggests lowering max_tokens; it does not help, because the audio floor is a fixed minimum balance, not a function of request size.

The 30 free requests are also irrelevant here twice over: the audio floor is checked independently of them, and 30 is fewer than the 42 calls the benchmark needs.
