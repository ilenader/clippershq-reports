### (b) CROP IS DISQUALIFIED, and the frames say why

On the 4:5 source the crop rendered the headline as **"EP 1 IS THE HIGHEST RAT…"** and
**"…EP IN ENTIRE SERIES !"** — cut at *both* edges. On the landscape source,
**"…hen bro's tox…"** and **"…o get back wit…"**. That is the hard constraint in this round's
brief and the same failure that got the pillarbox trim **built, wired, rendered and reverted**
after 6 of 6 renders sliced the headline: **the caption is wider than the picture.**

### What shipped: (a) + (c), and they only work together

- **(a)** the sharp layer is fitted to the **full canvas** (`scale_width` 864 → **1080**), so
  a 9:16 source — 92.6% of the library — fills it exactly;
- **(c)** whatever the sharp layer does not cover is filled with a **blurred, cover-cropped
  copy of the source** instead of white.

The sharp layer uses `force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease` and is therefore **never
cropped**, so the caption survives at all four edges **by construction** rather than by
margin — no reliance on the 70 px the transform budget leaves.

**Yes — and for the 92.6% majority it is not a judgement call.** Those frames are the source

## 5. THE COSTS, STATED

1. **The zoom no longer varies the rendered width.** `zoom_w = min(canvas_w, scale_width × z)`,
   so with the fit box at 1080 it clamps to 1080 on every roll — the brief's warning, and it
   is real. I did **not** raise `scale_width` *on its own*; it is paired with the blurred fill,
   which is what actually removes the white. The per-render fingerprint still varies on
   brightness, saturation, gamma, hue, speed, trim, rotation, shift, sharpening, noise,
   vignette, colorbalance, pitch and volume — 14 parameters, minus one.
2. **The blurred backdrop reproduces the caption as ghost text** in the bands on non-9:16
   sources — visible on the 4:5 clips as a soft repeat of the headline above the crisp one.
   It affects **7.4%** of the library and never the 9:16 majority. An edge-stretch or mirror
   fill would avoid it; not attempted here.
3. **`gblur=sigma=40` adds encode work** on the 7.4% where the backdrop is visible, and is
   computed-then-hidden on the rest.

**`y_offset` was left alone** — it is inert at HEAD (read only on the `vertical_align: top`
branch, and this template is `center`), so touching it would have been theatre.
