## 4. THE DEFERRAL MECHANISM — THE LATCH IS REAL, AND IT IS 4,888 PAGES, NOT 3

This is the one place the brief *understates* the problem.

**The filter, shipped, identical on both brains:**


### ⚠️ MY OWN FINDING ON RECENCY: THE FREE DATE DOES NOT EXIST ON INSTAGRAM

The brief says `taken_at_timestamp` gives the last-post date free "where the code says there is no
free source." **On the shipped Instagram funnel it does not.** Measured across all 46,899 banded

```
    last_post_at AND media_count   1,523
    last_post_at, NO media_count       0      <-- not once in 46,899 rows

```

**Control passes** — 1,523 co-occurrences prove the detector fires. Per band, `last_post_at` is on
**0 of 5,874** rejects and **0.0% of 36,496** unjudged, against **28.7%** of uncertain and
**99.7%** of keeps: **it appears exactly where the profile was bought, and nowhere else.**

⚠️ **So a recency gate cannot be used to AVOID the paid Instagram call. It requires it.** The field
map at `ig_discovery.py:77` does list `taken_at_timestamp`, and `:132` records it arriving on 90 of

fact: the last-post date is the **only one of the second call's five jobs a free field can do at
all; the other four are 0/680 across every payload family on disk.** In the master store the
asymmetry is stark: **TikTok carries `last_post_date` on 99.0% of 55,615 rows, Instagram on 5.1% of
17,007.**

And the threshold is where every previous gate went wrong: **TikTok's 62-day gate destroyed 40.5%
of wanted pages**; 30 days kills **20.4% [11.5, 33.6]** of his 8-10s for +2.0 pp; and ⚠️ **the

describes ZERO pages he has ever graded.** A 730-day rule was **discarded — its control failed, it
