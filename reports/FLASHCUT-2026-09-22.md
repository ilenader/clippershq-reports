## 4. Degenerate refusals

`overlay.render` raises `DegenerateOverlay` (an `AssertionError`) when: the head box is over **0.45** of the window; `converge` has under **8** keypoints or their spread is under **2.0 head widths** on the smaller axis; `skeleton` lacks both shoulders and a hip, or has under 10 keypoints. Measured on the delivered solves: real full bodies span 2.3–5 hw; the Haaland cheek triangle was 4 keypoints at 1.6; close celebrations 1.3–2.2. Six controls in `tests/test_overlay_transform.py` (the face-filling box refused, a normal box drawn, the 3-point triangle refused, a full-body converge drawn, a head-only skeleton refused, a full-body skeleton drawn): **40 passed, 0 failed**. `overlay_gate` applies the same limits to each segment's median geometry with a margin before the render, so nothing is drawn and then refused frame by frame.
