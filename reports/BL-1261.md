## 5. HIS SOFT-NO RULE — implemented, correct, and untestable on this population

*"if it's a soft no AND it has an email, let it through. If it's a soft no and there is NO
email, reject it."* Implemented as: flagged by exactly one signal = soft no; two or more =
hard no.

- hard no (2+ signals): **6**
- soft no (exactly 1 signal): **22** — of which **22 have an address and 0 do not**

**Every page in this sample carries an address (100 of 100)**, because the pool was drawn
from the send list, which requires one. So the rule reduces to *"keep every soft no"* and
**cannot be falsified here.** Applied literally it takes the pre-sort from 28 kills to **6**,
and the pass rate from 26.4% back to **20.2%** — it costs 22 of the 28 kills and buys, on this
evidence, nothing, because all 28 kills were correct.

The asymmetry is still sound; it is simply aimed at a risk this population does not contain.
It will start paying the moment the pre-sort runs on freshly discovered pages, where email
coverage is partial and a soft no without a reachable address is genuinely worthless. **My
recommendation is to keep the rule in the code and run the union un-softened on
already-harvested pools**, and to re-measure it on the first batch with partial coverage.

## 7. THE ECONOMICS, AND THE CEILING HE IS ASKING ABOVE

Judging at **$0.033/page** (BL-1258, prefix cached, 93.8% of input from cache).

| config | fed | killed | judged | WANTs | pass rate | $/WANT |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| today, no pre-sort | 100 | 0 | 100 | 19 | 19.0% | $0.1737 |
| **union of the 4 free signals** | 100 | **28** | 72 | **19** | **26.4%** | **$0.1251** |
| his soft-no rule applied literally | 100 | 6 | 94 | 19 | 20.2% | $0.1633 |
| union + still gate <50% clips | 100 | 49 | 51 | 17 | 33.3% | $0.0990 |

The recall-safe pre-sort is **1.39x on pass rate and −28% on cost per WANT, for zero pages
lost and zero dollars spent.** The pre-sort itself costs one page load: **100 pages probed in
111.4 s at 6 lanes = 53.88 pages/min**, nearly five times the capture rate, because it takes
no screenshot.

**He asked for "three times better", 50–70%. That is above the ceiling.** If a *perfect* free
text/DOM pre-sort killed all 57 text-reachable rejects and kept all 19 WANTs, 43 pages would
survive and the pass rate would be **44.2%**. A 50% pass rate needs 62 of the 81 rejects
killed — 77% — which means killing `not_english` (8), `dialogue` (6), `edit_page` (4),
`topical_aggregator` (3) and `promo` (1). Every one of those is defined by the text drawn on
the video. **They cannot be reached without looking at pixels.**

The honest route to 50%+ is therefore not a better text pre-sort. It is a **cheap vision
triage** — a small capture judged against a two-line question instead of the full brief — and
BL-1258 measured what that would cost: with the prefix cached, input is $0.008/page and the
long written reason is 75% of the bill, so a triage pass that returns *one word* instead of a
paragraph would run at a small fraction of $0.033. That is the next measurement, and it is
cheap to make.
