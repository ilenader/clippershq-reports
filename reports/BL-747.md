# BL-747 — The Spotify no-contact pool is 18.9% and genuinely dead. The real leak is next door: 52% of delivered leads never reach master.

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Type:** Investigation, READ-ONLY · **Spend:** **$0.0000** — no paid
call, no write to master or spend.json. SpotifyScraper is free web scraping.

---

## Pool size first

| | last run (1,000 delivered) |
|---|---|
| **no handle AND no email** | **189 = 18.9%** |
| handle only (DM-able) | 335 = 33.5% |
| email of any kind | 476 = 47.6% |

**BL-744's 32% was a small-sample artefact.** It came from 8 of 25; the true figure across the
full run is **18.9%**. That correction matters — the pool is a little over half the size the
last report implied.

Across master, `spotify_finder` rows with neither a handle nor an email: **0 of 1,233.**

That zero is not good news, and it is the finding this investigation actually turned up.

---

## The real leak — 52% of delivered leads never enter master

`spotify_finder.append_found_to_master` (line ~1111):

```python
if not r.get("email") or r.get("already_in_db") == "yes":
    continue
```

**Any row without an email is skipped.** Not just the 189 no-contact leads — the **335
handle-only leads too.** Together that is **524 of 1,000 delivered leads (52.4%) that exist
only in the per-run xlsx and never reach the master database.**

Consequences, none of them intended as far as the code says:

- **They are invisible to `crossdedup`.** A handle-only artist found today is not in master, so
  the next run has no record of them.
- **They can be re-discovered and re-paid for on every run.** The paid IG resolve that produced
  the handle is not remembered anywhere master-side. `resolved_recent` guards within a run and
  a cache window; master does not.
- **They cannot be DM'd from the send lists**, which build from master. The Twitch and IG
  funnels route DM-only leads through master; Spotify drops them at the door.

This is a bigger, cheaper win than anything in the no-contact pool itself, and it was found by
asking why master had zero of them.

## What surfaces are actually checked

Traced through `find_contact`:

| order | surface | cost | line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `external_links` from the artist payload → `parse_external_links` | free | 120–136 |
| 2 | aggregator / bio-link scrape (Linktree etc.) | free | 383 |
| 3 | **paid IG/TikTok resolve** — only runs if step 1 produced a handle | **paid** | 444 |
| 4 | external-link website scrape | free | 457 |
| 5 | country backfill from the already-bought profile | $0 | 472 |

**Step 3 is gated on step 1.** No handle from `external_links` means no paid resolve, which
means no profile, which means no email and no bio-link. The entire contact chain hangs off one
free field.

## Is anything present but unread? Yes — but it does not rescue this pool

**`parse_external_links` reads only two hosts.** Its own comment says so:

```python
if "instagram.com" not in low and "tiktok.com" not in low:
    continue        # skip FB/Twitter/Wikipedia/website
```

Real payloads confirm what is being discarded:

```
JEDSOUL       instagram.com/jedsoul1 · tiktok.com/@jedsoul · twitter.com/jedsoul1
Taylor Swift  facebook.com/TaylorSwift · instagram.com/taylorswift · twitter.com/taylorswift13
Eminem        facebook.com/Eminem · instagram.com/Eminem · twitter.com/Eminem · wikipedia
```

**Twitter/X is thrown away — and a working X-bio email scraper already exists in this
codebase.** `twitch_finder.x_bio_email()` fetches an X profile's `og:description` and pulls an
email from it, at ~2 s and $0. It is wired to Twitch and not to Spotify. That is precisely the
Twitch shape: a field that has always been there and a reader that already exists.

Facebook pages often expose a contact tab; Wikipedia is not contact but would fill country,
which is unknown on 41.5% of delivered leads.

## Are these leads worth recovering? No

The pool is **genuinely dead at the Spotify surface**, not neglected. Spotify holds no links for
these artists, so there is no unread field to read. This is the opposite of the Twitch case,
where `socialMedias` was populated and ignored.

Two further signals say the same thing:

| | no-contact pool | all delivered |
|---|---|---|
| median monthly listeners | **142,928** | 211,576 |
| country unknown | **119/189 = 63%** | 41.5% |

They are **smaller and less documented than average** — consistent with newer or more obscure
artists who have not filled in their Spotify profile. An artist who has not linked an Instagram
to their own Spotify page is exactly the artist least likely to have a findable one.

**Recommendation: report the pool as dead and stop chasing it.** Any recovery would require
leaving Spotify entirely — a name search on Instagram or a web search — which is a namesake-risk
problem (BL-702's `ig_artist` was the uploader, not a musician; the same class of error), not a
field-reading problem.

## If there is a recovery path, priced

**For the 189: none that is safe or cheap.** No free surface carries their contact. A
name-based IG search would cost a paid resolve per attempt *and* carry namesake risk with no
corroborating signal, which is how you get wrong contacts into a database.

**For the wider funnel, two cheap paths that are real:**

| path | population | cost | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Append handle-only rows to master** | **335/run** | **$0** | pure code change; stops re-discovery and re-payment, and makes them DM-able |
| **Read twitter/X from `external_links` + reuse `x_bio_email`** | unknown, needs measuring | **$0** | scraper already exists in `twitch_finder`; ~2 s per artist. Would NOT have helped 14 of the 15 sampled here, since they have no links at all — its value is on artists who *do* have links but no IG. |

The second needs a measurement before it is worth building: what fraction of artists have a
twitter link but no instagram link? That was not measured here and is the one number that
would price it.

## Limits

- **n=15 hand-checked**, all from one run. 14/15 with empty `external_links` has a 95% CI of
  roughly 68–99% — the direction is solid, the exact rate is not.
- **Artist IDs are not in the output sheet**, so the 15 were re-found by name search. A name
  collision would show up as a false "has links" (which is exactly the caveat on "Smiley") or,
  worse, a false "no links" if the search matched the wrong artist. This is the weakest link in
  the method.
- **I did not verify externally** whether these artists have an Instagram that exists but is not
  linked from Spotify. Section 4 asked for that; doing it properly means a web search per
  artist, and a match without corroboration is the namesake trap. What is established is
  narrower and still decisive: **Spotify itself holds nothing for them.**
- The 52% master-exclusion finding is read from the code path and the master count. Whether
  handle-only exclusion is deliberate is not documented anywhere I found.

**Nothing was implemented.**
