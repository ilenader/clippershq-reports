## 3. B — dialogue, the rule he dictated twice

> *"If from ten videos ALL of them are talking, scrap that page. If there is AT LEAST ONE
> video that isn't talking — just a sound — that's good, because that means that guy can post
> not-talking videos with our sound."*

Not a percentage. One clean video rescues the page, however talky the rest. The share rule
wrongly rejected rows 21 and 39; row 18 he approved *because* it had a mix.

**And he named the reason nothing was catching it:** *"moviezar's 'a lot of dialogue' is
SPOKEN AUDIO and nothing currently reads it."* The old signal read the drawn text, which
cannot hear. **Silero VAD is already in this repo** — `clippershq/clip_speech.py` runs it via
`faster_whisper.vad`, the ONNX assets ship in the venv, `available()` returns True. The rule

rather than growing a second copy — which is also how his finer line survives: *"a little
dialogue is fine when the song is the main focus"*.

**Honest limit: this rule has no field measurement.** The 500 stored clips carry no
`speech_frac` — VAD was never run on them — so `dialogue` fires 0 times in the scoring below.
It is unit-tested end to end, and measuring it needs a re-download of the clips, which is
spend this round did not have a mandate for.
