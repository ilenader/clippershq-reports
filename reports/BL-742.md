# BL-742 — FALSE-NEGATIVE RATE 63.3%. The music detector discards 31 of 49 music clips. Do not ship this gate.

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Type:** Measurement, read-only · **Spend:** $0.00

---

## The number that decides it

**At the shipped threshold (`speech_frac < 0.30`), the detector discards 63.3% of music clips
— 31 of 49.** Every one of those is a lead silently deleted.

The false-positive side is fine (1 of 35 dialogue clips kept, 2.9%). The gate is
catastrophically wrong in exactly the direction that costs you leads and leaves no trace.

**There is no threshold that fixes it.** The full curve, 84 hand-labelled clips:

| threshold | TP | **FN** | FP | TN | **FN-rate** | recall | precision | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.30** *(shipped)* | 18 | **31** | 1 | 34 | **63.3%** | 36.7% | 94.7% | 61.9% |
| 0.40 | 19 | 30 | 3 | 32 | 61.2% | 38.8% | 86.4% | 60.7% |
| 0.50 | 21 | 28 | 4 | 31 | 57.1% | 42.9% | 84.0% | 61.9% |
| 0.60 | 23 | 26 | 6 | 29 | 53.1% | 46.9% | 79.3% | 61.9% |
| 0.70 | 29 | 20 | 7 | 28 | 40.8% | 59.2% | 80.6% | **67.9%** |
| 0.80 | 32 | 17 | 12 | 23 | 34.7% | 65.3% | 72.7% | 65.5% |
| 0.90 | 34 | 15 | 17 | 18 | 30.6% | 69.4% | 66.7% | 61.9% |
| 0.95 | 36 | 13 | 26 | 9 | 26.5% | 73.5% | 58.1% | 53.6% |
| keep all | 49 | **0** | 35 | 0 | 0.0% | 100% | 58.3% | 58.3% |

Peak accuracy anywhere on the curve is **67.9%**, and it still throws away 40.8% of music.
Even at 0.95 — nearly "keep everything" — you lose 26.5% and precision has collapsed to the
base rate. **You were right to ask for the false-negative number first; it is the only one
that matters here, and it never gets acceptable.**

---

## Why it fails, in one line

`speech_frac` measures **speech**, and it is being asked about **music**. Those are not
complements. A clip with dialogue over a score has both.

The two distributions are not separable:

| truth | n | min | p25 | median | p75 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MUSIC** | 49 | 0.00 | 0.18 | **0.66** | 0.96 | 1.00 |
| **NO MUSIC** | 35 | 0.15 | 0.76 | **0.91** | 0.97 | 1.00 |

They overlap across essentially the entire range. There is a weak central tendency — music
clips do skew lower — but nothing a threshold can exploit.

## Item 5: the music-under-dialogue stratum is the whole problem

BL-700 flagged this as the case its tools were blind to. It is not an edge case here — **it is
the majority of the music.**

- **26 of 49 music clips (53%) have `speech_frac ≥ 0.60`** — music with speech over it.
- At the shipped threshold the detector keeps **0 of 26**. It discards **100%** of them.
- Only 18 of 49 music clips are "pure" music (`speech_frac < 0.30`).

Spread across formats: anime-edit 8, fan-edit 6, meme 5, movie-clip 4, franchise-clip 3.
**Every format has this problem**, so it cannot be routed around by page type.

This is the known boundary BL-700 named, now measured: the detector sees speech and infers
absence of music, which is wrong for over half of all music clips.

## Ground truth

84 clips, blind-labelled from spectrograms. **The spectrograms carried clip id and stratum
only — `speech_frac` was withheld**, so the ground truth could not anchor on the detector's
answer.

Rule applied: music = sustained horizontal harmonics, a visible beat/pulse grid, **or** a
tonal bed persisting through speech gaps. A diffuse **broadband haze with no tonal structure
was scored as ambience, not music** — that distinction is what BL-700's "diffuse haze" note
left unresolved, and it is applied consistently here.

| stratum | n | music | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| anime-edit | 12 | 12 | **100%** |
| fan-edit | 12 | 12 | **100%** |
| franchise-clip | 12 | 12 | **100%** |
| meme | 6 | 5 | 83% |
| **movie-clip** | 42 | 8 | **19%** |
| **total** | **84** | **49** | **58.3%** |

Movie-clip pages are the only stratum where music is the exception — consistent with BL-700
(8.5% music-dominant on those same three accounts) and BL-702 (movie-clip pages are
dialogue-first). The fandom formats are effectively all music, which is why a gate that
discards 63% of music is so expensive: it is discarding the formats you built the pipeline for.

## Item 4: `audio_type` cannot help, and the reason is structural

**Zero variance. All 84 clips are `original_sounds`.**

Not a measurement failure — a consequence of BL-702: `licensed_music` clips carry no
`original_sound_info`, so they expose **no downloadable audio at all**. The clips you can
analyse and the clips that declare licensed music are disjoint sets. `audio_type` is free and
present on every clip, but on every clip you can actually run a detector against it reads the
same value. It cannot discriminate here.

BL-705 found it useless at the account level; this is the clip-level counterpart, and the
cause is different — there it was uninformative, here it is constant.

## The ambiguous band is not where the problem lives

BL-700 predicted ~14% ambiguous. Measured: **10 of 84 = 12%** sit in `0.30 ≤ speech_frac <
0.60`, and they are **50/50 music** — no signal.

But fixing the middle would barely help. The 31 false negatives are overwhelmingly **not** in
the middle: 26 of them sit at `speech_frac ≥ 0.60`, well inside what the detector confidently
calls "speech". **This is not a boundary-tuning problem; the detector is confidently wrong.**

Per your instruction I did not reuse chroma, HPSS or peak-persistence — BL-700 invalidated
them and I found no cheap second signal that survives.

---

## Recommendation

**Do not gate on `speech_frac`.** Biasing toward keeping music, as you asked, the honest
conclusion is that the bias has to go all the way to "no gate":

| option | music kept | cost per 84 clips |
|---|---|---|
| gate at 0.30 *(shipped)* | 18 / 49 | **31 leads silently deleted** |
| gate at 0.70 *(best accuracy)* | 29 / 49 | 20 leads deleted + 7 wasted calls |
| **no gate** | **49 / 49** | **35 wasted audio-model calls (42%)** |

A wasted model call is a metered, visible, bounded cost. A false negative is an invisible one
you will never audit. At a 58.3% base rate you are paying for 42% waste to keep 100% of the
leads — and that is the correct trade for a first gate.

**If you need a gate later, it has to detect music directly**, not infer it from the absence
of speech. That means either a music/speech discriminator trained for the job, or accepting
the audio model's own verdict as the filter.

## Limits

- **84 clips, and the strata are unbalanced** — half are movie-clip, because that is where the
  on-disk audio is. The pooled 58.3% base rate reflects this corpus, not the live pipeline mix.
- **Adjudication was by machine vision on spectrograms, not by listening.** Sustained
  harmonics and beat grids read reliably; a *quiet* bed under continuous speech is the case
  BL-700 called invisible, and my rule scores broadband haze as ambience. If some of those
  hazes are in fact quiet music, **the true false-negative rate is higher than 63.3%, not
  lower** — the error direction is against the gate, not for it.
- The 26 music-under-dialogue clips were labelled from visible tonal structure; a human
  listener would likely find more, again pushing FN up.
- `speech_frac` values were reused from BL-694/BL-721 rather than recomputed — same detector,
  same files, deterministic. BL-721's 2.70s/clip timing stands; nothing here re-measures it.
