## 3. Three defects that are still live, and they are not the same defect

### 3a. The readback guard is BLIND on 9 of 9

The old guard compared `src_line.text` to `src_line.text` — the same variable — so it could
never fail. It has been replaced. It is no longer a tautology, and it is currently producing
**no measurement at all**: `our_line` in `_batch.json` reads

```
readback: OCR found no text inside the label band
```

for **all nine finished items**. It never read a single line back. Likely cause, from
`paste_batch.py:1845`: `label_box` is measured on the SOURCE frame and `blocks_in_band`
requires the band be in the same pixel space as the frame it reads, while our renders are
re-scaled — clip 12 is 720x720 against a differently shaped source.

**That blindness is why the two word-drops below shipped.** Whose: mine.

An independent readback pass, run separately over the same nine pairs, puts the real number
at **7 of 9 character-exact**. The two exceptions are exactly the two word-drops below. So
the guard is recoverable — the evidence it needs is there to be read, the production path
simply is not reading it.

### 3b. Clip 02 draws a row 92 px BELOW the strip, out on the video

Words correct, layout broken. The strip runs y=200..305; the text runs y=195..397. The last
row, *"years ago"*, is drawn on the picture and cut through its baseline. **This is a new
finding and it is not the swap** — fixing the reading order fixed the words and exposed that
the strip is not sized to the text it will hold. Whose: mine.

### 3c. Two clips lose words, at two different stages

| clip | recorded source line | what happened |
|---|---|---|
| **05_theclipbuzzz** | **already truncated** | His card genuinely reads "…he didn't react **to the reels I sent**". **Our strip covers that fourth row and never re-types it** — so we hide his words and put nothing back, which is worse than leaving them. The render was faithful to a string that was already wrong when it arrived. |
| **10_wasted** | **complete** — contains "Witness my aura and glory" | the **renderer** dropped it. The region below the strip was contrast-stretched in case black type had been drawn on black video: **nothing is there.** |

Same symptom, opposite causes. A single fix would have addressed at most one.

### 3d. And clip 08's "sending g me" is an OCR phantom in HIS frame

His original reads **"Real reason why bro is not sending me reels"** — there is no `g`.
RapidOCR split his row into two boxes whose x-ranges overlap (`is not sending` x178-623,
`g me reels` x592-900), so the `g` was read twice. That corrupted string became `his_line`
and the renderer drew it verbatim.

**The important part for the guard:** our pixels and the recorded source line agree at
**1.0000**. A readback that compares our render to the recorded line **passes this clip**. It
is only wrong against *his frame*. So the readback, once it works, must compare against HIS
frame, not against what we recorded — or this class stays invisible.
