# BL-1189 — the 31 studied, LamaTok mapped, and the funnel his spec asks for cannot be built as written

**LamaTok has no endpoint that lists a named account's own videos.** Its OpenAPI spec is 23
paths and not one of them returns a user's posts; the client's shipped default,
`/v1/user/videos`, 404s. So the operator's central gate — *"~3,000 average views across the
latest ten videos"* — **is not computable for a named account with this vendor**, and neither
is "text on screen" for an account you have merely been handed.

That is the finding this round exists to deliver, and it lands **before** any filter was
written, which is what he asked for.

**And the number that sets everything downstream: 7 of his 31 accounts publish an email —
22.6%.** Higher than Instagram's 17%. But 0 of 48 accounts found by discovery published one,
and the reason is size, not luck.

Every figure here came from **`.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, Python 3.11.9**. Bare `python` is
3.13.14 and lacks the packages; it was used only for the stdlib-only claim readers.

## 2. What LamaTok actually exposes — all 23 paths

Read from its own `/openapi.json`, which is free and definitive.

| need | endpoint | result |
|---|---|---|
| profile, bio, followers, videoCount | `/v1/user/by/username` | ✓ — bio is `signature`; profile nests at **`.users.<username>`**, stats at `.stats.<username>` |
| **an account's own videos** | — | **✗ DOES NOT EXIST.** `/v1/user/videos`, `/user/medias`, `/user/posts`, `/user/feed` all 404 |
| following | `/v1/user/following/by/**secUid**` | ✓ `.response.userList` — **`by/username` returns 403; by/secUid returns 200** |
| followers | `/v1/user/followers/by/secUid` | ✓ 20/call |
| suggested | `/v1/user/suggested/by/secUid` | ✓ 20/call |
| hashtag videos | `/v1/hashtag/medias`, `/v2/hashtag/medias` | ✓ `itemList`, 20/call |
| keyword search | `/v2/search` | ✓ `search_item_list`, authors + `play_count` + `desc` |
| **accounts using a SOUND** | — | **✗ DOES NOT EXIST.** Only `/v1/media/music/download/by/{id,url}` |
| download a video for OCR | `/v1/media/video/download/by/{id,url}` | ✓ |
| one video by id/url | `/v1/media/by/{id,url}` | ✓ |

**Two of the levers the brief asked for are simply absent**: sounds-as-a-cluster, and
per-account video listing. The wrapper discipline paid off immediately — every discovery
payload nests under `.response.*`, and `envelope.items_of` unwrapped all of them unchanged.

> **I nearly mis-reported `following` as unavailable.** `by/username` returns **403**; the
> `by/secUid` variant returns **200** with a populated `userList`. One probe of one variant
> would have retired TikTok's equivalent of Instagram's best discovery path on a false
> negative.

### Cost per page, measured — not assumed

`/sys/balance` returns `{"amount": 271.4572, "requests": 452421}` and the `requests` field is
**remaining quota, not a counter**: after 31 profile calls it had fallen by exactly 31.

**$271.4572 ÷ 452,369 = $0.000600 per request, and a profile is one request.** That is the
same unit price as the Instagram vendor, established here from the vendor's own balance/quota
pair rather than a config assumption. The USD figure did not move at four decimal places
across 31 calls, which is why the quota field is the better instrument.

## 3. Discovery yield — and the ceiling that follows

10 of his own accounts as seeds:

| path | calls | returned | NEW | new/call | profiled | **with email** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| followers | 10 | 200 | 200 | 20.0 | 16 | **0 — 0%** |
| search | 2 | 34 | 34 | 17.0 | 16 | **0 — 0%** |
| suggested | 10 | 197 | 37 | 3.7 | 16 | **0 — 0%** |
| following | 1 | — | — | — | — | HTTP 200, works |

**0 of 48 discovered accounts published an email**, against 22.6% of his hand-picked 31.

That is not a broken instrument — it was checked. The `followers` path returns
`monggocious` (91 followers), `hunkifli93` (6), `pitchpeaches1` (64), `kiyo8334` (28).
**On TikTok, an account's followers are its FANS, not its peers.** 20 accounts per call of
essentially worthless leads, and the highest raw yield of any path — volume and value point in
opposite directions again.

`suggested` returns 20 but only 3.7 *new* per call: it clusters tightly, which is good for
relevance and converges fast. `search` returns actual content authors and is the most
promising, at 17 new/call.

**So the chain, measured end to end: 268 accounts discovered → 48 profiled → 0 emails.**
Discovery is cheap and contactability is the wall, exactly as it turned out on Instagram —
where 230 of 300 pages published no contact at all.

## 4. What I did NOT build, and why

The brief asked for the filter, VAD dialogue gating, the send file with MX refreshed first,
and a full chain run. **None of those were built.** The reason is section 2: his format spec
is defined on an account's latest ten videos, and this vendor cannot return them. Building a
filter against a proxy I chose — "the videos we happened to see in a hashtag feed" — would be
substituting a definition he did not give, which is precisely the failure that sank three
Instagram filters in a row.

**The design the vendor actually permits is video-first, not account-first:** discover videos
from `search`/`hashtag_medias` (each carries `author`, `play_count`, `desc`), group by author,
then buy one profile call per author for the bio and the email. On-screen text is reachable
via `/v1/media/video/download/by/id` → `frame_text`, and speech via Silero VAD, both per video
rather than per account. That is a coherent funnel and it is a different shape from the
Instagram one; it should be built deliberately, not improvised at the end of a study round.
