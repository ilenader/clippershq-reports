## 2. The regression: depth is refuted

### A method correction first

The brief proposed comparing fill rate **by position within the 30 Jul run**. The output sheet is **sorted descending by monthly listeners** — 996 of 999 adjacent pairs descend — so position is reach rank, **not run depth**. That test would have measured fill-versus-size and been read as fill-versus-depth.

The decisive test is whether the fill-versus-size *curve* shifted, or only the size mix.

### The size mix barely moved

| | 27 Jul | 30 Jul |
|---|---|---|
| leads | 304 | 1,000 |
| median listeners | 247,554 | 211,576 |
| **share under 100k** | **24.0%** | **24.9%** |

### The curve moved — most where depth cannot explain it

| listener band | 27 Jul | 30 Jul | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50k–100k | 57.5% (n=73) | 45.8% (n=249) | −11.8 pt |
| 100k–250k | 50.0% (n=80) | 51.0% (n=300) | +1.0 pt |
| 250k–500k | 63.6% (n=55) | 48.0% (n=196) | −15.7 pt |
| 500k–1M | 62.0% (n=50) | 46.9% (n=130) | −15.1 pt |
| **1M–3.1M** | **67.4% (n=46)** | **43.2% (n=125)** | **−24.2 pt** |

A depth effect leaves large artists untouched. This one hit them hardest.

**Verdict: not expected decline. The audit should alarm, not excuse.**

### Where it actually broke

| run | handle rate | email given a handle |
|---|---|---|
| 27 Jul | **92.8%** | 63.5% |
| 30 Jul | **81.1%** | 58.7% |

`0.928 × 0.635 = 0.589` · `0.811 × 0.587 = 0.476` — reproducing both published headline numbers exactly. **~70% of the loss is free handle discovery, upstream of any payment.**

**It is not a fetch failure.** On the run day `spotify.com` returned **6,457 of 6,699 responses as 200 (96.4%)**, 219 `401`s (token refresh), and **zero 403s**. Artist pages loaded fine — they carried fewer IG/TikTok links.

**Why cannot be answered from any artifact**, because **no per-lead discovery source is recorded**. That is exactly the gap BL-744 ranked #1. Two independent investigations now converge on the same missing column.

**What section 6 should therefore print** is not `email fill −19.2%` but *"handle discovery 92.8% → 81.1% — upstream of spend, not a fetch failure"*. That is the difference between an alarm and an action, and it is a change to the spec's section 6 that this investigation earned.
