## 1. The four edits, applied

### 1. `captioned` is gone

> ~~"we send back cut, **captioned**, ready-to-post clips"~~

It had **zero implementation anywhere**. `subtitles_uri_of` reads a URI Instagram attached to a *scraped* clip; client footage never has one. Nothing in the system has ever produced a caption for a client, so the word was a promise with no mechanism behind it at any point.

### 2. All five outcome claims are now intents

| segment | was — a claim about **results** | now — a claim about the **edit** |
|---|---|---|
| creator | shorts **that feed** the main channel | **cut to** work as channel feeders |
| musician | clips **that send** people to the track | **cut to** send people to the track |
| streamer | clips **that pull** viewers back to the live channel | **cut to** pull viewers back to the live channel |
| developer | clips **that show** the app doing the thing, not a feature list | **cut to** show the app doing the thing, not to list features |
| generic | clips built to travel | **cut to** travel |

This is the worst of the four and the reason is arithmetic: **no client has ever been delivered to**, so there is no clip that fed any channel, sent anyone to a track, or pulled a viewer back. The sentence was stated as fact to 1,392 strangers.

It is the same defect BL-992 fixed on the clipper branch — the false median, corrected on 751 messages for exactly this reason. A number or an outcome the data does not carry must not be asserted.

### 3. The mechanism sentence is gone

> ~~"**you send the footage**, **we send back** cut, ready-to-post clips"~~
> now: "We turn *{material}* into short-form for TikTok, Reels and Shorts — *{intent}*."

Both halves promised a mechanism that does not exist: there is no ingest path and no delivery path. BL-1010 proposed making it *"concrete or conditional"* once ingest is decided; ingest is **not** decided, so the truthful move today is to remove it rather than invent it. **A narrower offer beats a promise with no backing.**

### 4. The trial sentence is untouched

> "If you want to see it on your own material rather than a showreel, reply and I'll cut three clips from your footage first."

Per BL-1010 edit 4. It is conditional on a reply, so it **arranges** rather than promises, and it becomes fully concrete the moment ingest and delivery are answered.

## 3. The clipper sweep — clean, and here is why

The clipper message carries the only sentence in the system that asserts a consistency, and it is **data-routed**, not asserted:

- `reach_shape()` compares the featured clip against the median. **Spike** → *"that one went well past your usual"*; **consistent** → *"a real audience, not one lucky post"*; **unknown** → **empty string, no tail at all**.
- Both branches fired in the sample: **16 spike, 12 consistent**. BL-986 measured the un-routed version false on 47.6% of the block; BL-992's fix is live and observed working.

Arm B carries **no numeric claim whatsoever** — *"I run ClippersHQ — we pay editors… I came across your {fandom} edits and thought of you. Are you taking paid work at the moment?"* It cannot exhibit the defect.

The remaining clipper sentences are offer statements (*"ClippersHQ pays editors to cut short-form…"*, *"you keep your own account"*) and a reply instruction. None asserts a result, a consistency or a track record.

## 6. Ingest and delivery are gated by nothing

`strict=True` refuses exactly these seven when unfilled:

```
SENDER_NAME  REPLY_TO  RATE  TURNAROUND  PAYMENT_TERMS  PRICE  PORTFOLIO_LINK
```

**Neither ingest nor delivery is among them, and no placeholder for either exists in the module at all** — `INGEST`, `DELIVERY`, `FOLDER`, `UPLOAD`, `HANDOFF` appear nowhere. So price, turnaround and portfolio cannot be forgotten silently, and the two decisions the offer actually depends on can.

Removing the mechanism sentence (§1.3) defuses the *copy* half of this: the message no longer promises something the operator has not decided. The decision itself is still ungated, and BL-1010's page is right to tell the operator to settle ingest and delivery first.

## 7. What I got wrong, and the suite

**I nearly published my own harness bug as a template defect.** My first render pass set `ab_arm` on each row. The arm column is **`message_variant`**. Nothing reads `ab_arm`, so both arms rendered identically and I measured *"arms DIFFER: 0, identical: 400"* — which, given BL-991 exists specifically because an earlier pilot's arms sent identical copy, looked exactly like a regression of a known defect. With the right column: **arms differ on 400 of 400**, and the diff is a genuinely different opening and a shorter body.

I also passed a plain `dict` where `render(operator=…)` expects an `mt.Operator`, got `AttributeError: 'dict' object has no attribute 'value'`, and briefly recorded strict-accept as failing.

**Three existing tests used the deleted copy as their marker** (`"we send back"`, `"they send the footage"`). Rather than delete the assertions — they exist to prove the two audiences never cross — I re-anchored them on the **contract**: the direction of payment (`Price:` for the client, `Rate:` for the clipper) and the agency pronouns that survive. A test about audience separation should not redden because a sentence was corrected.

It reports the same 7 at HEAD** — measured by rendering HEAD's `message_template.py` against a full copy of the package — so they are pre-existing and untouched by this round.
