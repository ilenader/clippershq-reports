## The headline: your rule was right, and the margin is large

You instructed me to treat HikerAPI as a raw mirror — assert presence on the schema set, non-null on the canary set, and never the full schema. Measured:

| provider class | endpoint | total paths | schema | **optional** | canary |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **raw mirror** | `/v2/user/clips` | 556 | 417 | **139 (25.0%)** | 314 |
| **raw mirror** | `/v2/hashtag/medias/clips` | 719 | 481 | **238 (33.1%)** | 349 |
| normalised | Spotify `get_artist` | 45 | 45 | **0** | 34 |
| normalised | Spotify `get_playlist` | 179 | 179 | **0** | 166 |
| normalised | Twitch panels | 14 | 14 | **0** | 10 |
| normalised | Google Play `app()` | 47 | 47 | **0** | 39 |
| normalised | Google Play `search()` | 15 | 15 | **0** | 13 |
| normalised | MusicBrainz | 23 | 23 | **0** | 15 |

**A canary asserting the full schema on `/v2/hashtag/medias/clips` would fire on a third of its assertions on the first run.** It would be switched off within a week, exactly as BL-772 predicted — and then the endpoint that matters most would have no canary at all.

The optional paths are recognisable Instagram optionality, not noise:

```
response.items.media.coauthor_producers.*                      (12 paths, 1 of 3 accounts)
response.items.media.clips_metadata.music_info.music_asset_info.*   (1 of 3)
response.items.media.caption.text_translation                  (1 of 3)
response.items.media.collab_follow_button_info.*               (1 of 3)
response.items.media.clips_metadata.content_appreciation_info.* (2 of 3)
```

A collab post has `coauthor_producers`; a solo post does not. A post with licensed audio has `music_asset_info`; an original-audio post does not. **None of these is a schema change — and all of them would look like one to a naive canary.**

### The rule, as it now stands in the manifest

```
RAW MIRROR  -> assert PRESENCE on the schema set only
            -> assert NON-NULL on the canary set
            -> assert NOTHING on optional paths
NORMALISED  -> the full schema may be asserted
```

`_probe_samples/README.md` now carries both rules side by side with the measured percentages, so the next person does not have to rediscover why the two classes are treated differently.

My first shape script capped traversal at depth 4 and reported **19 total paths and 0 optional** for `/v2/hashtag/medias/clips` — impossible for a 1.4 MB body — and extracted **0 media ids**, which made its "PAGINATION OK" verdict vacuous. The `/v2/user/clips` figures from the first pass were not depth-limited in the same way, but they were still understated (287 → 556 paths) because of the single-row list walk. **Both numbers in this report are from the corrected pass.** Had I published the first, the report would have claimed the raw mirror behaves like a normalised provider — the exact opposite of the truth, and it would have justified the canary design that fails.

## Honest limits

- **Three samples remains a floor, not a proof.** 139 and 238 optional paths is a lower bound on optionality — a fourth account would likely add more. For a raw mirror the practical rule is that the *schema* set shrinks as samples grow, so a canary built on three samples should be re-derived when the baseline is refreshed, not frozen.
- The three `/v2/user/clips` accounts are all repost/meme pages from the master's `user_pk` column, because those were the ids available for free. They are the funnel's real population, but they are not a random sample of Instagram.
- The p1↔p2 overlap check uses any `pk`/`id`/`code` value found in the document, not a media-id whitelist, so the absolute counts (49, 209…) mix media ids with user and audio ids. The **overlap ratio** is the meaningful figure and is robust to that; the raw counts are not a media count.
- **`/v2/hashtag/medias/clips` is deprecated and was still answering normally today.** This baseline captures a surface that is scheduled to disappear — its value is detecting the shutoff, not preventing it.
- **A concurrent agent was editing `clippershq/` during this round** — `send_suppress.py` appeared (new, untracked, 13:22) and `writer.py` was modified (13:23), implementing BL-770's suppression recommendations. Those are not my changes; I verified my own writes were confined to `scratch/`, `_probe_samples/` and `spend.json`. The shape analysis reads only saved JSON, so it is unaffected.
