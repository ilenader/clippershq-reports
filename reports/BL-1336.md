## 2. The other two defects

### The date field: correct, and verified live

From the shipped source, `clippershq/meme_finder.py::_last_post_epoch`:

> *"The newest `taken_at` among a page's posts, or None. **None, never 0.** `posted_at is EPOCH`
> is already a trap this project has paid for, and a zero here would render as 1970 in the review
> sheet — a date that looks like data."*

It is `max(taken_at)` — the newest post, not the first element, not the account age — and the
author had already refused the epoch-zero trap by name.

**Source is a claim, so I re-fetched live.** Three pages spanning the stored range — the oldest,
the median and the newest:

| handle | stored | live newest | == newest? | == oldest? | == first element? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wesoccermemes` | 1,696 days ago | **identical** | ✅ | ✗ | ✅ |
| `drunkcigsdontcount` | 3.0 days ago | **newer** (posted since) | moved forward | ✗ | ✗ |
| `topgreatsongs` | 0.1 days ago | **identical** | ✅ | ✗ | ✅ |

`wesoccermemes` is the decisive one: it is the **oldest** stored date, which is exactly where
"account creation" or "oldest post" would show up. Twelve posts came back and the stored value
matched the **newest** of them exactly. **stored == oldest: 0 of 3.**

Sanity across all 100 profiled pages: **0 epoch-zero**, **0 in the future**, 2 honest `None`, range
2021-12-24 to today, median 3.2 days since last post.

**There is no Instagram date defect.** The dormancy rule that could fire on it
(`cut_garbage_dormant_days`, default **730 days**) is not set in `config.json`, and it carries its
own backstop — `cut_garbage_max_fraction`, abort if more than 40% would be cut. Only 3 of 98 dated
pages are older than 730 days.

The cost is nonetheless severe: **judge-first rests entirely on free capture, and free capture
