## PART 2 — the trace, file:line

### The capture is not at submit at all

There is no cover capture on the submit path. `captureClipThumbnail` has exactly two callers in the repo: `src/app/api/clips/[id]/thumbnail/route.ts:84` and `scripts/backfill-clip-thumbnails.ts:153`. The card fires the POST lazily on render when a TikTok/Instagram clip has no stored cover (`ClipCardNew.tsx:157-188`). So the first capture attempt happens the first time a clipper looks at their clips page, not at submit.

### The break, per platform

| platform | stage | verdict | location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Instagram** | **CAPTURE** | **BROKEN, 100%** | `src/lib/clip-thumbnail.ts:289` |
| TikTok | capture | healthy | `clip-thumbnail.ts:279` (free oEmbed, BL-373) |
| YouTube | capture | not applicable | `ClipCardNew.tsx:132` (URL-derived) |
| all | storage | healthy | `clip-thumbnail.ts:202-208` |
| all | retrieval | healthy | 60/60 HTTP 200 |
| all | render | healthy | `ClipCardNew.tsx:258-269` |

**`src/lib/clip-thumbnail.ts:289` is the single line that kills it:**

```ts
const { stats } = await fetchClipStats(clip.clipUrl, { skipHikerOverlay: true });
```

`skipHikerOverlay: true` is a deliberate choice, documented at `clip-thumbnail.ts:285-287`: *"skipHikerOverlay: true routes IG to Apify (whose raw carries displayUrl); HikerAPI's raw strips image blobs."* That flag forces `fetchClipStats` past the HikerAPI branch (`apify.ts:2284`) and straight into `fetchInstagramStats` (`apify.ts:2332`), whose three tiers are **all Apify**: `apify/instagram-api-scraper` (`apify.ts:722`), Apidojo (`apify.ts:685`), and the legacy chain (`apify.ts:695`, itself skipped for `/reel/` URLs by BL-543, and 96% of Instagram clips are reels). With `APIFY_API_KEY=DISABLED` in prod every tier 401s and returns null, so `stats` is null, `stats?.raw` is undefined, and `clip-thumbnail.ts:290-293` returns **`reason: "scrape-empty"`** with no cover, forever.

The design intentionally pinned Instagram cover capture to Apify. When BL-613/BL-614 removed Apify, view tracking, verification and submit all had non-Apify replacements. **Cover capture did not, and it failed silently because it is best-effort by design and reports only into a browser console line** (`ClipCardNew.tsx:181`).

**Why TikTok survived.** BL-373 added a free TikTok oEmbed call at `clip-thumbnail.ts:279`, tried FIRST and before any scrape. It needs no key and no Apify credit. Probed live: `tiktok.com/oembed` returned a `thumbnail_url` on `p16-common-sign.tiktokcdn.com`. That is why TikTok is 54/54 after the cutover.

### CRITICAL SUSPECT (expiring signed CDN URL): DISPROVEN

Every stored cover is on Supabase. Grouped by host over all 1,280 rows with a `thumbnailUrl`:

| platform | host | rows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | `<project>.supabase.co` | 692 |
| TikTok | `<project>.supabase.co` | 588 |

**Zero rows point at an Instagram or TikTok CDN.** The re-host into the `uploads` bucket under `clip-thumbnails/<clipId>.<ext>` (`clip-thumbnail.ts:202-208`) has always worked as designed, the bucket is public, and 60/60 sampled objects still return 200. This hypothesis is dead. As a second nail: the CSP `img-src` allow-list does not include `cdninstagram.com` or `fbcdn.net`, so a raw Instagram CDN URL could never have rendered even for a minute.

### SECOND SUSPECT (the Apify cutover): CONFIRMED

The daily table above is the proof. 100% on 2026-07-21, 0% from 2026-07-23. Weekly, the same signal: week of 2026-07-13 Instagram captured 143 of 147 (97.3%); week of 2026-07-27, 0 of 104. TikTok held 100% across the same boundary. Nothing else changed on that day but the Apify key.

## PART 3 — what each provider actually returns for a cover image

Probed live this round (costs in the disclosure section).

| provider | platform | field | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HikerAPI** `/v2/media/info/by/code` | Instagram | `image_versions2.candidates[0].url` | **PRESENT and EXPIRING** |
| HikerAPI (as parsed today) | Instagram | `HikerResult.raw` | **ABSENT** (stripped, see below) |
| **LamaTok** `/v1/media/by/url` | TikTok | `video.cover` (also `originCover`, `dynamicCover`) | **PRESENT and EXPIRING** |
| **TikTok oEmbed** (free, no key) | TikTok | `thumbnail_url` | **PRESENT and EXPIRING** |
| **YouTube** `i.ytimg.com` (free, no key, no API) | YouTube | `maxresdefault.jpg` etc. | **PRESENT, does not expire** |
| YouTube Data API | YouTube | `snippet.thumbnails` | PRESENT in the API, NOT fetched (no key locally; not needed) |

**HikerAPI gives us the Instagram cover today, and it is downloadable right now.** Two live probes on real pending Instagram reels: HTTP 200, `media_type: 2`, `image_versions2.candidates` length 1, dimensions 640×1136. Fetching that candidate URL with the browser-like headers `rehostImage` already sends returned **HTTP 200, `image/jpeg`, 26,832 and 31,622 bytes, JPEG magic bytes `ffd8ffe0`**. It re-hosts cleanly.

**EXPIRING, so it must be copied, never stored raw.** Both candidate URLs carry `oh` and `oe` signature parameters. Decoding `oe`: `6A6FA110` = 2026-08-02T19:57:04Z and `6A6FB401` = 2026-08-02T21:17:53Z, roughly 4 days out. The TikTok cover URL carries `x-expires` and `x-signature`. **The existing re-host into Supabase is exactly the right architecture and must be kept.**

**Why HikerAPI's cover never reaches the extractor.** `classifyV2Media` deliberately reduces the response to `raw: { media_type: mediaType }` (`hikerapi.ts:595`, also `:546`, `:575`, `:610`), with the reason stated at `hikerapi.ts:506-507`: emit key names only, never the nested values, because `video_versions[]` alone can run to 100+ KB per row on the tracking batch. That is a sound decision for the cron. It also means the image URL is thrown away before anyone can use it. And note the near miss: `extractCoverUrl` **already probes exactly the right path** at `clip-thumbnail.ts:113`, `at(raw, "image_versions2.candidates.0.url")`. The extractor was written for this shape. It is never handed the object.

### Render is fine, and here is the placeholder the owner is seeing

`ClipCardNew.tsx:258` renders the image when a source exists; otherwise `ClipCardNew.tsx:275-277` renders a gradient box plus a play glyph, tinted by platform from `ClipCardNew.tsx:50` (`from-[#833ab4]/25 via-[#fd1d1d]/15 to-[#fcb045]/15`) with the Instagram corner mark at `:292-296`. **That is the "generic Instagram placeholder". It is the correct fallback firing on a genuinely absent cover, not a load failure.** `/api/clips/mine` uses `include:` not a top-level `select:` (`mine/route.ts:60`), so `thumbnailUrl` does reach the card, and the CSP allows `*.supabase.co`.

## PART 4 — the fix spec, ranked. Build nothing yet.

**RANK 1 — Instagram cover from HikerAPI. This is the whole problem.** Two shapes, same result:

* **1a (recommended, smallest blast radius):** give `clip-thumbnail.ts` its own direct HikerAPI `/v2/media/info/by/code` call for Instagram, read `image_versions2.candidates[0].url`, hand it to the existing `rehostImage`. Replaces the `fetchClipStats(..., { skipHikerOverlay: true })` call at **`clip-thumbnail.ts:289`** for the Instagram branch only. Contained entirely inside one non-money display module. `apify.ts`, `hikerapi.ts` and `tracking.ts` are untouched. Needs a vendor call: yes, one HikerAPI media call per clip, **$0.001** at the repo's own ledger rate (`apify-ledger.ts`).
* **1b (free at the margin, larger blast radius):** carry the full v2 media object through on `HikerResult.raw` behind an **opt-in flag**, exactly the pattern BL-668 used for LamaTok, then drop `skipHikerOverlay` at `clip-thumbnail.ts:289`. Edits **`hikerapi.ts:595`** (and `:546/:575/:610`). Marginal vendor cost **$0** if the capture piggybacks a Hiker call the cron already makes. **The opt-in is mandatory:** an unconditional attach pushes 100+KB payloads through the tracking batch, which is precisely what `hikerapi.ts:506-507` was written to prevent.
* Either way the re-host into Supabase **must stay** (PART 3: the source URL expires in ~4 days).

**RANK 2 — backfill the 1,138 Instagram clips with no cover.** `scripts/backfill-clip-thumbnails.ts` already exists and reuses the same `captureClipThumbnail` path, so once rank 1 lands this is a run, not a build. Cost at $0.001 per HikerAPI call: **≈$1.14 one time**, less whatever share of those posts are deleted or private (a definitive 404 costs the same but yields nothing). **Recommendation: backfill.** It is one dollar and it repairs 1,138 cards.

**RANK 3 — TikTok: change nothing.** 100% since BL-373 and free. The 521 TikTok clips with no cover are pre-BL-373 leftovers and would backfill at **$0** through the free oEmbed path. Optional, cosmetic.

**RANK 4 — YouTube: change nothing.** Working, free, no storage.

**Storage cost at 2,300 clips a month.** Measured cover sizes this round: 26.8KB and 31.6KB (Instagram, 640×1136), 52.5KB (TikTok). At a 40KB average, 2,300 clips a month is **≈92 MB/month, ≈1.1 GB/year**. At Supabase's published $0.021 per GB-month storage rate that is **≈$0.002 the first month, ≈$0.023/month after a year, ≈$0.12/month after five years.** Existing usage is 1,280 objects, well inside the free allowance. **Storage cost is not a consideration at this volume.** The variable to re-check before committing is Supabase's current per-GB rate and egress allowance, not our volume.

**One thing to fix alongside rank 1, or it becomes a real bill.** The card's session dedup marker is set only on a SUCCESSFUL capture (`ClipCardNew.tsx:175`, deliberate per BL-359). Today a permanently uncapturable clip re-fires its POST on every visit; that is free right now because Apify 401s cost nothing. **After rank 1 lands, every such retry becomes a paid HikerAPI call.** A negative-result marker or a `thumbnailCaptureFailedAt` stamp should ship with the fix. Flagged, not fixed, per this round's scope.

## PART 5 — the verdict

**Instagram thumbnails fail because `clip-thumbnail.ts:289` passes `skipHikerOverlay: true`, deliberately routing Instagram cover capture into the Apify tier chain, which has 401'd on every call since the key was disabled at 2026-07-22T11:12Z, giving 0 of 169 captures since (100% failure, exactly as reported) while TikTok is unaffected at 54 of 54 via free oEmbed and YouTube is unaffected via URL-derived `i.ytimg.com` covers; the single fix that resolves the most is to source the Instagram cover from HikerAPI's `image_versions2.candidates[0].url`, which is PRESENT and downloadable today for $0.001 a clip, and keep re-hosting it into Supabase because that URL expires in about four days.**
