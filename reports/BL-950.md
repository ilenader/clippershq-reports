It wraps to three lines and then **stops**. This is not a wrap failure — it is a **hard

### The text is the wrong text even when it fits

unrelated sentences with no separator. v24's is a plot synopsis. **Nobody stops scrolling
for an encyclopedia entry.** These are scraped reference text, not hooks.

## Limits

- **I read 7 of 25 sheets in full, plus 4 full-resolution frames.** The caption, layout and
  double-caption findings come from those. Duration, dead-black, audio presence and the song
  test are measured on **all 25**. A per-video *content* judgement beyond the first
  disqualifier is not something I can honestly give for the 18 I sampled less closely.
- **I cannot hear them.** "Silent" and "source only" are stream and level facts; whether the
  kept source audio is any good is unmeasured.
- **Items 3 and 4 are unanswered** — hook placement and song fit need a batch where the bed
  actually lands. That is a finding, not a gap I chose.
- **The batch ledger covers only the 14 in `bl940_work`**, so the `mb044_work` twelve have no
  `song_tier`/`bed_enabled` record here; their silence is measured directly instead.
- **"Would a page post this" is judgement**, not measurement. The 12 silent files and the
  79-second one need none; the rest is taste, informed by the format.
- **A near-miss worth recording:** `scratch/bl950_report.md`, a path I claimed, already
  contained **BL-951's** report. I did not overwrite it and wrote under a different name.

## Method

Six frames per video at even intervals, tiled into one contact sheet each, and read as
images — plus four full-resolution single frames, because a tiled sheet understates how much
of the canvas is empty. Duration, size, dimensions and near-black fraction from
`ffprobe`/`blackframe` on all 25. Audio presence per file, then envelope correlation of every
audible output against the configured track to test whether the music had landed. Song, bed
and class facts read from the batch ledger and render logs rather than inferred. Nothing was
modified.
