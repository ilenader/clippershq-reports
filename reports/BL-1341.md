## §4 — JUDGE-FIRST, AND ORDERING KEPT SEPARATE

**The mode that gives judge-first is `want_only`, and the config does not ship it.**
`config.json` currently carries `page_verdicts_mode: "reject_only"`, whose own documentation
says it *"buys everything else INCLUDING a page with no verdict"* — the mode that bought 567
profiles for 100 wanted at **$7.23**. The branch is `meme_finder.py:3175`:

```python
_skip = (_v == "REJECT") or (_mode == "want_only" and _v != "WANT")
```

**No ordering was used in this round and none was added on top of judge-first.** They are
different things and are stated separately: **ordering saves JUDGE CALLS, not dollars** — it
changes which pages an agent reads first, and buys nothing from the vendor.

**Neither funnel's `--target` counts wanted pages.** Instagram stops on `stats["resolved"]`
(emails, `meme_finder.py:3139`); TikTok on `result["with_email"]` (`tiktok_finder.py:1512`).
This run's own numbers show the gap: **145 passing against 5 with an email.** "Run to 100
wanted" has to be counted by the caller, and that is why the TikTok run stopped at 74.
