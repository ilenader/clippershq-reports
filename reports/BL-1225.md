## 1. The mechanism, proven by construction

`hook_chain` ends with `adelay=<place_at>`, and `edit.py` appends its bed bound
`atrim=end=<video duration + 0.25>` **after** it. So `end=` is measured on a timeline the
delay has already pushed forward, and cutting at the video's duration on that clock removes
exactly the delay from the tail.

Measured on his batch by **AAC frame count** (`nb_frames x 1024 / 44100`) — never the
container's declared duration, which reads full length on every one of these files and is
wrong:

**`audio_seconds == video_seconds - adelay_seconds` on 14 of 14 clips carrying a delay**,
to within 0.35 s.

| clip | video | adelay | video − adelay | actual audio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 21_wasted | 13.97 | 8.77 | 5.20 | **5.25** |
| 16_movies.h1story | 18.43 | 9.77 | 8.66 | **8.73** |
| 11_men | 14.47 | 6.77 | 7.70 | **7.76** |
| 13_memeequest | 11.50 | 5.77 | 5.73 | **5.78** |

**And the control is inside the same batch.** Clips 10, 20 and 22 carry **no adelay at all**,
and their audio is full length — 100.1%, 100.2%, 100.1%. The defect scales one-for-one with
the placement and vanishes when the placement is zero. That is construction, not symptom.

**Both candidates I was handed are ruled out.** The `aloop` is correct — `loop=-1` with
`size` in samples, verified against `hook_chain` directly. `-shortest` is doing its
documented job; the audio leg really is short, and it is short because of the bound above.

## 3. The fades are removed, and they were never the cause

`fade_in_sec` 0.4 and `fade_out_sec` 0.8 are now **0.0**, in both places that set them:
`edit.py`'s defaults and `memebot/scraper/config.yaml`, which sets them explicitly. Changing
only the default would have left every render he actually receives still fading, because an
explicit value beats a default.

A template may still ask for a fade — the key is read, not ignored. Removing the capability
would have been a different change from removing the default.

**This is cosmetic next to the 47.5%, and it is not what stopped the music.** Said plainly so
the fix is not credited to the wrong change.
