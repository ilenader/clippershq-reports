## 6. THE ONE SIGNAL THAT SURVIVES — and it is pixels, not a model

`video_strip._pad_frac_one` is deterministic. It has no self-agreement problem because it
cannot disagree with itself. Asked of the same question, at the **page** level — nine videos
from one page are not nine independent observations of that page's taste — with a 20,000-draw
bootstrap and a permutation test:

**tiktok / memes — 93 of his 9s-and-10s against 58 of his 6s-and-7s, 9,024 tiles**

| statistic | good | less-good | difference | 95% CI | p | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| page **median** pad_frac | 0.3833 | 0.1656 | **+0.2177** | [+0.0989, +0.3812] | **0.0034** | **SEPARATED** |
| page **p90** pad_frac (the tail) | 0.5292 | 0.5615 | −0.0323 | [−0.0739, +0.0855] | 0.3075 | not separated |
| share of tiles with any bar | 0.8333 | 0.6562 | **+0.1771** | [+0.0333, +0.3549] | **0.0131** | **SEPARATED** |

**instagram / memes — 38 against 29, 4,790 tiles: nothing separates**, and the reason is that
the signal is *saturated* — **share of tiles with any bar is 1.0000 in both groups.** Every
Instagram tile in this corpus has a bar. A feature that is always on cannot discriminate.

Three things follow, and each is a separate claim:

1. **The bars rule is real, TikTok-only, and lives in the MEDIAN, not the tail.** His good
   TikTok pages letterbox their typical post; the extreme tile is identical in both groups.
   Anything reading the p90, the max, or a whole-sheet `content_box` reading would find
   nothing.
2. **On Instagram the rule is inert, not absent.** 100% against 100%.
3. **The pixel instrument sees it and the model does not.** Same corpus, same pages: the
   deterministic reading separates at p=0.0034 while the model's reading of the same property
   sits 14 points inside a 32-point noise room. **This is free and it is already written** —
   `_pad_frac_one` runs on a frame we already hold. Asking a model that is 68% self-consistent
   for a number a function can compute exactly is paying for noise.

**Re-derived a second way**, as required: the model's own reading points the same direction on
TikTok (letterbox 35.0% good against 23.7% less-good) — it simply cannot clear its own floor.
Direction agrees; only the pixel version is measurable.
