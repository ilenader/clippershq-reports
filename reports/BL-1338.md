**32 lanes is WORSE than 24.** The ceiling on this stage is around 24 lanes.

**And here is what actually breaks first — it breaks silently.** At 32 lanes the vendor began
returning **HTTP 427**, and `api_client.py:522` handles any unexpected status by logging a
warning and **returning `None`**:

```python
# Any other unexpected status: log and skip (don't burn retries).
log.warning("unexpected status %s on %s: %s", status, what, _short(resp.text))
return None
```

`videos_from_handle` turns that `None` into an empty list with **no error**, which becomes *"this
account has 0 videos"* — **a rate-limit signal converted into evidence about the page.** That is
this round's bug in a third place, and it is why the errors column above reads 0 while 427s were
firing. My own lane table is contaminated by it at the 32 arm.

**On the 100 pages/min target:** the bottleneck stage sustains ~140 calls/min at 24 lanes. Whether
that yields 100 pages/min end-to-end depends on the 45.9% share holding, which I did not
re-measure — so I am not claiming the target is reached. **The measured facts are: 24 lanes is the
ceiling, 32 regresses, and the failure at 32 is invisible to the caller.**
