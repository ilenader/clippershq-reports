# BL-852: the threshold that matters is not the one that was inherited. `SPEECH_MUSIC_MAX = 0.30` is roughly right; `SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN = 0.05` is four times too low, and it is the singing bug

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Type:** Measurement, read-only · **Spend:** **$0.00 · 0 paid calls**
Claim filed via `tools/claim.py` (`.claims/BL-852.json`, 8 other rounds in flight). **No pipeline code changed** — output is `scratch/` only, as instructed. All audio was already on disk; all compute was local.

Acts on [BL-848](BL-848.md). Ground truth, spectrograms and labels are saved in `scratch/bl852_*`.

## The headline

The round was asked to re-derive one threshold. There are **two** in play, and they had been conflated:

| threshold | where | decides | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SPEECH_MUSIC_MAX = 0.30` | `clip_library` | `format`: music-first vs dialogue-first | **roughly right** — inside the derived band |
| **`SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN = 0.05`** | `clip_speech` | the four-way `audio_class` | **four times too low — this is the singing bug** |

The inherited 0.30 survives scrutiny. The number that is actually wrong is the one BL-848 introduced itself, and it is wrong in the exact way the singing question predicted.

**Recommended: set both to 0.20.** Evidence below. **Not changed in code**, as instructed.

## 1. Breaking the circle — ground truth that owes nothing to `speech_frac`

BL-848 could not adjudicate because the only labels available were a threshold on `speech_frac` itself (their ranges were perfectly disjoint, which is how the circularity was detected).

So I built labels by **looking at spectrograms**. 60 clips, stratified across the `speech_frac` range so the decision region was actually covered — a random 60 would be nearly all extremes and would pin a threshold nowhere. Each clip was rendered 0–5 kHz, where formants live, and read using BL-700's method:

- **spoken** — stacked formant bars that move fast, syllable-rate striation, discrete utterance bursts
- **sung** — formants held far longer, sinusoidal vibrato, smooth glides, notes on a musical grid
- **instrumental** — steady harmonic stacks, percussive stripes, no formant motion
- **unclear** — recorded and **excluded from the derivation**

Labels and images are saved (`scratch/bl852_labels.py`, `scratch/bl852_sheets/`) so the judgement can be checked rather than taken on trust.

| label | n | high-confidence |
|---|---:|---:|
| spoken | 22 | 11 |
| sung | 12 | 3 |
| instrumental | 15 | 6 |
| unclear | 11 | 0 |

**These labels are wrong in their own ways. What matters is that they are not wrong in a way correlated with `speech_frac`.**

### The derived threshold

**High-confidence labels only (n = 20):**

```
    thresh    TP    FN    FP    TN    recall     prec      acc   bal-acc
      0.10    11     0     1     8    100.0%    91.7%    95.0%     94.4%
      0.15    11     0     0     9    100.0%   100.0%   100.0%    100.0%
      0.20    11     0     0     9    100.0%   100.0%   100.0%    100.0%
      0.30    10     1     0     9     90.9%   100.0%    95.0%     95.5%
      0.50     8     3     0     9     72.7%   100.0%    85.0%     86.4%
```

**All confident-enough labels, high + med (n = 49):**

```
      0.10    21     1     9    18     95.5%    70.0%    79.6%     81.1%
      0.20    18     4     4    23     81.8%    81.8%    83.7%     83.5%
      0.30    16     6     1    26     72.7%    94.1%    85.7%     84.5%
      0.35    16     6     0    27     72.7%   100.0%    87.8%     86.4%
```

Perfect separation at 0.15–0.20 on n = 20 is certainly optimistic. The wider set peaks at 0.35. **The honest reading is that the boundary lies somewhere in 0.15–0.35, and 0.30 sits inside it.**

**What breaks the tie is the error asymmetry, and it is not symmetric at all:**

- a **false negative** — spoken dialogue scored as music → **muted and replaced → the dialogue is destroyed**
- a **false positive** — music scored as dialogue → **ducked → the song plays slightly quieter. Harmless.**

At 0.30 the wider set loses **6 of 22** spoken clips (27%) to muting. At 0.20 it loses 4; at 0.10, one. Since the costs differ by that much, **the threshold belongs at the low end of the defensible band: 0.20.**

## 2. The singing case — measured, and it is real but not where it was expected

`sung` clips versus the others, on the hand-labelled set:

| label | n | min | p25 | **median** | p75 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| instrumental | 15 | 0.000 | 0.000 | **0.005** | 0.129 | 0.217 |
| **sung** | 12 | 0.000 | 0.004 | **0.085** | 0.244 | **0.344** |
| spoken | 22 | 0.000 | 0.266 | **0.629** | 0.804 | 0.984 |

**Silero does not mistake a chorus for dialogue wholesale.** Sung clips sit at a median of 0.085 against spoken at 0.629 — a 7× separation, and no sung clip in the sample exceeded 0.344.

| sung clips scoring ≥ | n | share | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 0.05 | 8/12 | **66.7%** | 39.1–86.2% |
| 0.10 | 4/12 | 33.3% | 13.8–60.9% |
| 0.30 | 1/12 | **8.3%** | 1.5–35.4% |

So singing is **harmless at 0.30 and severe at 0.05.** And `SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN` — the gate that assigns the four-way class — **is 0.05.**

### The damage, quantified on declared songs

`licensed_music` clips carry a declared track title: they are songs by declaration. Of 70 in the pool, **35 (50%) were classified `dialogue-over-music`.** That should be near zero.

**Their `speech_frac` median is 0.098, and 74.3% of them sit in the narrow band [0.05, 0.10)** — barely over the line. This is not dialogue. It is sung vocal producing a small non-zero score, and a 0.05 gate converting it into "someone is talking".

Raising `SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN` fixes it almost completely, and — the important part — **without disturbing the clips that really do carry dialogue**:

| `SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN` | `licensed_music` → music-only | `original_sounds` → music-only |
|---|---:|---:|
| **0.05 (current)** | **50.0%** | 14.3% |
| 0.10 | 87.1% | 17.1% |
| **0.15** | **91.4%** | 18.6% |
| **0.20** | **94.3%** | 22.1% |
| 0.30 | 97.1% | 29.3% |

Songs move 50% → 94%; dialogue clips move 14% → 22%. **That is the asymmetry you want, and it is why this is the single highest-value change available.**

**Consequence today:** BL-848's own "`licensed_music` is classified free from the declaration" shortcut was the right instinct — but the measurement path contradicts it on half of those clips, so which answer a clip gets depends on whether it happened to carry a URL. Raising the gate makes the two paths agree.

## 3. The `dialogue-only` boundary is reachable — BL-848's limitation was too pessimistic

BL-848 recorded that `BED_PRESENT_DBFS = −45 dBFS` sat "well below any observed bed level" and was untested where it decides. On 60 clips that was true. **On 572 it is not.**

```
  bed_dbfs over 572 clips: min -180.0  p1 -180.0  p5 -46.6  p10 -38.8  median -18.3  max -0.8
  clips below the boundary        : 33/572 (5.8%)
  clips within +/-10 dB of it     : 64   (11.2%)   <- the region it actually decides
  dialogue-only clips' beds       : min -180.0  median -52.2  max -45.1
```

The boundary sits **between p5 and p10** of the real distribution — sparse, but populated — and the closest `dialogue-only` clip sits at **−45.1 dBFS, one tenth of a decibel from the line.** The class is genuinely reachable and genuinely decided at that value. **I am correcting BL-848's stated limit rather than repeating it.**

The one caution that survives: the distribution is strongly **bimodal** — a long tail at −180 dBFS (digital silence) and a bulk around −18 dBFS. 11.2% of clips land in the ambiguous middle, and the boundary's exact placement decides those.

## 4. The projection, widened from 20 clips to 210

BL-848's 52/38/9 came from 20 measured clips. Re-run on **210 clips with a declared `audio_type`** (140 `original_sounds`, 70 `licensed_music`), projected onto the live library's own composition (895 clips: 528 / 357):

| class | projected n | share | **95% CI** |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **music-only** | 432 | **48.3%** | **45.5 – 52.3%** |
| **dialogue-over-music** | 385 | **43.0%** | **38.3 – 46.9%** |
| **dialogue-only** | 68 | 7.6% | 4.9 – 11.4% |

BL-848's ~52% music-only had a ±10-point interval; this has **±2–3 points** and lands slightly lower. The conclusion is unchanged and now safe to plan from: **roughly half the library is mute-and-replace, and roughly half needs ducking.**

**Caveat that matters:** these rates are measured at the *current* `SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN = 0.05`. At the recommended 0.20 the music-only share **rises** — the `licensed_music` reclassification alone moves ~44 points of that stratum. The projection above is therefore a **lower bound on music-only**, and mute-and-replace will cover more than 48% once the gate is corrected.

## Recommended change — evidence complete, not applied

| constant | now | recommend | why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `clip_speech.SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN` | 0.05 | **0.20** | songs 50% → 94.3% correctly music-only; dialogue clips barely move |
| `clip_library.SPEECH_MUSIC_MAX` | 0.30 | **0.20** | inside the derived band; recall 72.7% → 81.8%, and the FN cost is destroyed dialogue |

Setting both to the same value also removes a genuine hazard: two different constants currently answer the same question ("is anyone talking?") with different numbers.

## Honest limits

- **The ground-truth labels are mine, from images, and are not calibrated.** I could not listen to the audio. `high` confidence means the signature was unambiguous; 11 of 60 clips I could not read at all and excluded rather than guess. A second labeller would disagree on some of the `med` set, and the two derivations (0.15–0.20 vs 0.35) differ by exactly that.
- **Perfect separation at n = 20 is not evidence of a perfect threshold.** It is a small sample and the wider set does not reproduce it. The recommendation rests on the *band* plus the error asymmetry, not on the 100%.
- **The singing measurement rests on 12 sung clips.** The confidence intervals are wide (39–86% at the 0.05 gate). What is not in doubt is the *direction* and the mechanism — 74.3% of misclassified songs sitting in [0.05, 0.10) is a much larger and much clearer sample than the hand labels.
- **`sung` vs `spoken` is my visual judgement**, and rap, spoken-word intros and heavily processed vocals sit genuinely between the two. Those are the cases where a threshold cannot help.
- **The 210-clip projection pool is not the library.** It is the offline audio that happens to exist, drawn mostly from BL-715's undeclared-clip corpus, which was itself selected. The library's own `audio_type` composition is applied on top, which corrects the biggest skew but not all of it.
- **Nothing was changed in code and nothing was re-run through the pipeline**, so these numbers describe what the current build *would* do, not what it has done.
