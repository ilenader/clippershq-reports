## 3. The franchise allowlist — shipped, and BL-1005's stub was matching a third of it

BL-1005 refused a `^\w+:\s` pattern. **That refusal is confirmed by measurement**, not just
respected: over all **947** non-empty franchise values, **111 carry a colon and only 28 are
a label.** The other **83 are real titles containing one** — `Avengers:` alone is 14 rows.
A pattern would have deleted three real titles for every label it caught.

### What BL-1005's stub actually matched

The stub listed six canonical names — `fact / about / credit / source / judul / rare` — and
matched **9 of the 28 label rows**. It had the *names* and not the *spellings*:

| canonical label | spellings in the data | rows |
|---|---|---:|
| movie name | `Movie Name:` · `Movie Title:` · `Movie name:` · `Movie ':` · `Movie Name :-` | 13 |
| fact | `Fact:` · `Fun Fact:` · `Fun fact:` · `Cool fact:` · `Interesting fact:` · **`Intresting Facts:`** | 9 |
| judul | `Judul:` · **`​Judul:`** (leading zero-width space) | 5 |
| about | `About:` | 1 |
| credit | `Credit:` | 1 |
| rare | `RARE:` | 1 |

Two of those are the reason a name-list was not enough: the **typo `Intresting Facts:` is in
the data**, and one `Judul:` carries a **leading zero-width space** so `startswith` never saw
it. `source:` occurs **zero** times; it is kept because it costs nothing, and it is not
evidence of anything.

### Every change, listed — so a stripped title would be visible

**25 distinct values changed, and I read all 25.** `Movie Title: Spider-Man: Homecoming` →
`Spider-Man: Homecoming` is the one that matters: the label goes, the *title's own colon*
stays. A separator fix came out of reading them — `Movie Name :- Enchanted` left `- Enchanted`,
which reads as a bullet, so the separator glyph is stripped whichever was typed.

**83 colon-bearing values were left untouched.** `test_NO_REAL_TITLE_IS_STRIPPED` pins 22 of
them by name — Avengers, Spider-Man, Batman v Superman, Captain America, Thor, Avatar, Venom,
Pirates, SAS, Furiosa, X-Men, Madagascar, Minions, Dune, Mad Max, The Hobbit, Robin Hood,
Austin Powers, Léon, `Re:Born`, Dungeons & Dragons.

A label with nothing after it is **not** stripped: `Fact:` stays `Fact:`, because returning
`""` would render as a blank and that is exactly what `val()` exists to prevent.
