**Why UNBOUNDED means unbounded.** The labellers select on `not is_labelled(r) and attempts_of(r) < MAX_ATTEMPTS`, and `vision_attempts` is bumped by **`record_failure` alone** (`clip_vision.py:497`). ## 4. The guard

`tools/paid_write_guard.py`. **AST, because grep cannot answer the question** — `grep continue` returns hundreds of lines and none of them says whether money was spent first.

A branch is reported only when **all three** hold:

1. **the function persists somewhere** — a pure fetcher's `return None` loses nothing, its caller owns the record;
2. **the exit is `continue`** — `break` ends the loop and the function records below it;
3. **the paid call binds to the SAME innermost loop** as the branch.

**Precision 154 → 0, and every step was driven by hand-verifying a false positive, not by tuning to a number:**

| | found | what was wrong |
|---|--:|---|
| first draft | 154 | `retrieve()` returning to a caller that records perfectly well |
| + persists & loop-exit | 32 | — |
| + same-innermost-loop | 15 | `discover_pages` skipping ad placeholders in a nested loop over an **already-paid** response |
| + `continue` only | 3 | `clip_media.retrieve` breaks 4× then falls into `if media is None: res["stopped_early"]=…` |
| + local-recorder resolution | **0** | `reject()` persists via `append_rejection` but matches no word in `WRITES` |

Two further precision defects were mine and are fixed: `_get(` matched `_cfg_get(cfg, "motion_frac_times")` — a dict lookup in a frame loop, reported in **six copies** of `band.py` — now `._get`; and `vision` matched `vision_text(clip)` and `parse_vision_json(raw)`, free accessors over data already bought, now excluded by `NOT_PAID`.

**A coverage gap the guard was hiding from itself:** `scratch/infra002_mem.py` was reported "unparseable — invalid non-printable character U+FEFF". Valid Python; I was reading it as `utf-8` instead of `utf-8-sig`, so a BOM made a whole file scan for nothing. Now `0` unparseable across all roots.

The one that matters: **`original_lang_for_translations` is populated on 42.9% of paid media, `ig_discovery.py:364` reads it into `post_lang` for leads, and the clip library has no language field fed by it at all.** The library's `language` column is **1.0% populated** (21 of 2,003) and comes from a caption-derived source. Of 224 payload keys: **15 kept**, **16 read-then-dropped**, **106 never read by any line of `clippershq/`**, **87 present but never populated** on any of the 170.

That last group is the trap worth naming: `music_metadata`, `clips_captions`, `accessibility_caption`, `title`, `usertags` and 82 others read as tempting recoveries and are **empty on every single media**. ## Honest limits

- **I did not fix the largest open instance.** `bl895_finish.py:195` is the still-silent `HTTPError` branch, UNBOUNDED, 5 passes run. BL-951 holds it and its claim says it is sweeping exactly this. Reporting beats racing another round's edit — but the leak is open as I write this, and if BL-951 misses that branch the way BL-949 did, it stays open.
- **`bl842_gemini.py` has 5 branches and I left them.** RERUN class, unclaimed, and I could have taken it. I stopped at the two UNBOUNDED-adjacent files to keep the round's scope defensible against a shared index; that was a judgement call and a reader may reasonably think it was the wrong one.
- **I classified `bl896_tokens.py` as UNBOUNDED and was wrong.** I read its selector (`V.select`, the shipped unlabelled filter) and stopped one question short: it never *marks* a clip even on success, so a re-run re-pays the whole sample either way. Corrected to RERUN in the table, with the reasoning left in the source.
- **`$/1000` is the loss per item that HITS the branch, not per item processed.** The true bill is that number times the failure rate, and I did not measure the failure rate — it varies by vendor health and would need a live run.
- **Recurrence is hand-read, so it can be wrong.** Fifteen runners, fifteen judgements, each recorded with its deciding line so it can be checked. The guard's three structural conditions are testable; this column is not.
- **One-level recorder resolution, not transitive.** A recorder that only calls another recorder still reads as silent. Stated in the source rather than hidden; the fix if it bites is a fixpoint pass.
- **`NOT_PAID` is a name list and will need extending.** It is a heuristic over identifiers, same as `PAID` — it will misjudge something eventually. Extending the tuple is the intended repair, not special-casing a call site.
- **The 106 never-read keys are unexamined individually.** I ranked them by fill and read the top of the list; there could be something valuable at 30% fill that I passed over.
