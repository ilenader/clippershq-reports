## §1 — THE FIX, VERIFIED BEFORE HE MARKS ANYTHING

BL-1309 said plainly what it could not do: *"I could not reproduce it in a live browser. The
browser tool refuses `file://` URLs."* So the fix had never been driven by a keyboard.

**The `file://` block is solved by not using `file://`.** The sheet is served over
`http://127.0.0.1:<port>` by a stdlib `http.server` in a thread, which gives it a real origin
and therefore a real `localStorage`. Playwright drives it with a **persistent** profile, and the
strongest proof is taken by **closing the browser process entirely and reopening it** — a value
that survives a process exit reached the disk, whatever the in-memory API claimed.

It was verified **twice**, because proving the code is not proving the artefact.

**Pass 1 — the shipped JS** (`scratch/bl1315_verify_reason.py`, imports `bl1296_sheet.JS`
unmodified, JS sha256 `95a54f25e65d6030`):

| check | result |
|---|---|
| A survives a browser restart, read back off disk | **pass** |
| B **the counter advances by two per page** | **pass** — seq 0 → 2 → 6 |
| C comma + apostrophe + newline round-trip | **pass** — 55 chars in, 55 out, exact |
| D edited after saving, overwrites cleanly | **pass** |
| E a reason typed with **no verdict** | **pass** — persists |
| F a save with the **server down** | **pass** — localStorage is client-side |
| G receipt reads `reason saved (N chars)`, green | **pass** |

On B: `fill` fires both `input` and `change`, so a verdict plus a reason moves `seq` by 4.
Typing key by key fires strictly more. The failure being excluded is a delta of **1** — the
verdict landing and the reason never arriving, which is exactly the arithmetic that gave 101
for 100 pages.

**Pass 2 — the 9.3 MB file he will actually open**
(`scratch/bl1315_verify_live_sheet.py`, driving `output/bl1315_fresh100/mark/index.html`):

100 textareas found · storage key `bl1315_marks_v1` with **BL-1296's key untouched** ·
seq delta **4** · receipt `reason saved (55 chars)` green · exact round-trip ·
**exact after a browser restart** · textarea repopulated from the store on load ·
fail banner empty · **test marks deleted and the deletion verified, so the sheet arrives
unmarked.**

The two sibling sheets he was asked about, `bl1298_instagram_marks_v1` and its TikTok twin, do
**not** carry the fix — but they are **spent**, not reachable work: their marks were downloaded
on 2026-08-15 into `ground_truth/`, which is where the 200 empty `why` fields above come from.
Nothing is waiting to be typed into them.

## §4 — WHAT THE ANSWER WILL MEAN, WRITTEN BEFORE HE MARKS

**This draw is the NEW DISCOVERY pool.** Every page is a suggestion-crawl candidate that has
never been judged, never been shown on a sheet and never been marked. BL-1312 measured that
pool at **9.0% [4.8, 16.2]**. The other rate — **28.0% [20.1, 37.5]** — is *enriched stock*
already inside the funnel. Both are correct and they are different populations.

**So the expectation is about 9 of 100, and anything from 5 to 16 is the same finding.**
Comparing this draw against 28% would read a different population as a collapse in supply.

**The judge, blind, said WANT on 11 of 100 — 11.0% [6.3, 18.6].** That sits on top of the 9%
prior with heavily overlapping intervals. Its rejections: `creator` 40, `still_pictures` 17,
`business` 10, `not_english` 8, `no_qualifying_tiles` 6, `topical_aggregator` 4,
`lyrics_ambient` 3, `news` 1. Confidence high on 84.

### The reject-everything floor for THIS draw

**At a 9% want rate the floor is 91%. If he wants 11, it is 89%.** The floor is `1 − his want
rate on this draw`, so it cannot be finalised until he has marked.

**The uncomfortable consequence, stated before the data.** The judge's headline is 92 on
Instagram. A 92 at n=100 carries **[85.0, 95.9]**. The floor is **91**. The interval contains
the floor.

> **Agreement on this draw cannot separate this judge from one that rejects every page.**
> That is a property of a low base rate, not a failure of the judge. On BL-1298's stock, a 28%
> want rate left 20 points of headroom and the judge was worth +20. Here there is roughly one
> point of headroom.

### So what to read instead

**Confirms it holds:** recall on his WANT pages stays at or near 1.0 — the judge misses none of
the pages he wants — *and* agreement is at or above the floor computed from his actual rate.

**Means it has drifted:** the judge misses a page he marks WANT. **One miss is worth more than
five points of agreement**, because 100% recall on wanted pages is the claim that has held
across every scored set so far.

**Cannot be concluded either way:** that the judge is better or worse than 92. This draw has no
power to move that number.

**And recall's own denominator is tiny.** At 11 wanted pages, recovering all 11 gives a Wilson
interval of **[74.1%, 100%]** — perfect recall here is consistent with a true recall of three
in four. The interval gets reported; a bare "100%" would overstate it.
