## CONFIRMED ON THE MERGED RESULT, BY READING THE MERGED CODE

### 1. The protection is scoped to the identified clippers and the measured window

`OUTAGE_FORGIVEN_LOCAL_DAYS` is a single literal, `["2026-09-08"]`, with **exactly three references in the
whole repository**: the declaration at `gamification.ts:263`, the walk branch at `:270`, and the progress
grid branch at `:545`. Every other date string falls straight through to the original path at `:276-277`.

The scoping to *people* is structural rather than a list. The outage day **inherits the verdict of the day
before it**, so a clipper whose 7 September was a passing day keeps their streak, and a clipper who had
already stopped inherits a failure and gains nothing. **There is no allowlist and no hardcoded user id**,
which is why it cannot drift onto anyone it was not measured for.

### 2. The streak rule itself is unchanged

**The entire merge removes exactly TWO lines from the file**, and both are assignments being replaced:

```
REMOVED:   const { start: oldestStart } = dayBoundsFromStr(oldestStr, tz);
REMOVED:     const dayClips = byDate.get(dayStr) || [];
```

**Neither is part of the pass, pending or fail decision.** `evaluateDayByBounds`, which is the function that
actually decides a day's verdict from the clips on it, is **untouched**. So are the backwards walk and its
break conditions, the today-credit branch, the no-active-campaign freeze, and the `streakRestoredAt` skip.
**From 9 September every date takes the original path and normal behaviour resumes**, so a clipper who does
not post loses their streak exactly as before.

### 3. Nothing is altered retroactively

The streak bonus on a clip is frozen at approval in `Clip.streakBonusPercentAtApproval`. The recalculation
path reads that snapshot at `gamification.ts:948` and passes it as an override at `:984`
(`streakBonusPercentOverride: lockedStreakPct ?? 0`), and because `?? 0` makes the override always non-null,
the live-streak branch in `earnings-calc.ts` is unreachable from a recalculation.

**This merge touches none of those lines.** Filtering the diff for `lockedStreakPct`,
`streakBonusPercentOverride` and `recalculateUnpaidEarnings` returns **one match, and it is inside a comment**.

**BL-824's paid-is-final and BL-538's never-decrease are both untouched**, because `balance.ts` was not
edited and no earnings write path changed.
