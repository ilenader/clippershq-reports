# BL-744 — Spotify funnel audit: the filter order is already correct, the seeds have drifted, and 41.5% of delivered leads have no country

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Type:** Audit, READ-ONLY · **Spend:** **$0.0000** — no paid call, no
write to master or spend.json. SpotifyScraper and MusicBrainz are free.

---

## Headline

**The thing most likely to be wrong is right.** All five gates fire *before* the paid resolve;
nothing that costs money is spent on a lead a later filter throws away. The only step after
payment is the country backfill, which is $0 and *recovers* leads rather than dropping them.

The real weaknesses are elsewhere: one seed has decayed badly, the trial playlist everyone
expected to die is now the best performer, and **41.5% of delivered leads carry no country at
all** — the single largest group in the output.

## 2. What the filters are actually throwing away

Re-measured live and free: 56 artists, 8 per seed, across all seven default seeds.

| reason | n | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `low_listeners` | 11 | **all correct** — 4,367 to 48,241 listeners, genuinely below a 50k floor |
| `too_many_listeners` | 1 | **marginal, see below** |
| `non_target_country` | **0** | never fired |
| `non_target_language` | **0** | never fired |

Real names behind `low_listeners`: Cam Keddy (4,367), Moriah Mensah (7,383), Dan Alley
(12,337), Vuat (13,848), Alyssa Flaherty (13,960), Ava Claire (14,295), CHITAA (21,391),
X & Ivy (33,954), McCall Chapin (35,878), Lakelin Lemmings (42,321), Miss Kaninna (48,241).
No false positives — these are small artists, correctly dropped by a has-money gate.

**The one drop worth arguing with:** `Barry Can't Swim` at **3,015,808** listeners, dropped by
a **3,000,000** ceiling. That is **0.53% over the line.** A ceiling exists to skip artists with
a label and a marketing team, and half a percent is not a meaningful difference in that
property — it is a rounding error deciding a lead.

**Country and language did not fire once in 44 checks.** That is not proof they are harmless —
it is a much stronger statement about the *seeds*: with US/UK/CA/AU editorial playlists as the
source, the geo and language gates have almost nothing to reject. They are near-inert on the
current seed set, which means they are also not the thing limiting volume.

> The three named historical failures (Latin-script Serbian, a 53%-wrong MusicBrainz name
> match, an NSFW keyword firing on chat rules) do not reproduce here — but on a sample where
> those two gates never fired at all, that is weak evidence. **A country/language false
> positive would only appear on a seed set that surfaces non-Anglophone artists, and the
> current one does not.**

## 4. Seed drift — one decayed, one surprised

All seven playlists **resolve**, 8 artists sampled each, in-band = clears both listener gates.

| seed | in-band now | prior | drift | median listeners |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RADAR Canada | **8/8** | 5/8 | **+3** | 319,073 |
| Fresh Finds R&B | **8/8** | 7/8 | +1 | 216,110 |
| **Fresh Finds Electronic (TRIAL, non-37i9)** | **7/8** | 4/8 | **+3** | 796,960 |
| RADAR Early Noise | 6/8 | 6/8 | flat | 366,697 |
| RADAR UK & IE | 6/8 | 5/8 | +1 | 179,360 |
| RADAR AU & NZ | 6/8 | 5/8 | +1 | 290,920 |
| **Fresh Finds Country** | **3/8** | 5/8 | **−2** | 390,169 |

Overall **44/56 = 79% in-band**, up from a prior average of ~5.3/8 (66%).

**Fresh Finds Country has decayed.** 5 of its 8 sampled artists are under the 50k floor
(Alyssa Flaherty 13,960, Dan Alley 12,337, Ava Claire 14,295, McCall Chapin 35,878, Lakelin
Lemmings 42,321). The playlist has rotated toward much smaller artists than when it was
measured.

**The trial playlist did not die — it is now the best seed by in-band rate.** The non-37i9
community id `7Ge1SJnlEQ08lgpAGS7xX2` still resolves with 100 tracks and scores 7/8, up from
4/8. Its median of 796,960 is the highest of any seed, which also means it sits closest to the
ceiling. It should be promoted out of "trial", **with the caveat that a community playlist can
still vanish without notice** — the reason it was flagged has not gone away, only the evidence
of instability.

Caveat: n=8 per seed. A one-seed swing of ±2 is inside noise; the +3 and −2 moves are the ones
worth acting on.

## 5. ListenBrainz — on and wired, but its contribution is unmeasurable

`spotify_use_listenbrainz` is **true**, `spotify_listenbrainz_days` is **1**, and it is wired
at `control.py:2139`. So it is on and it runs.

**How many of the last run's 1,000 leads came from it cannot be answered.** No per-lead source
field is recorded — the output sheet has no discovery-source column and the master does not
carry one for Spotify rows. The 100%-net-new claim from the original measurement therefore
**cannot be re-verified from any artifact this funnel produces.**

That is itself the finding: a discovery source whose whole justification is "100% net-new"
ships with no way to confirm it is still true. Adding a one-word `source` field would make
every future seed decision measurable.

## 6. What the funnel is not doing

**Fields fetched and discarded.** The artist payload is pulled for every candidate and carries
43 fields. Three useful ones are never referenced anywhere in `spotify_finder.py`:

| field | why it matters |
|---|---|
| **`followers`** | a *committed audience* number alongside *reach*. `followers / monthly_listeners` is a free loyalty ratio — a 200k-listener artist with 50k followers is a far better clipper client than one with 2k. |
| **`world_rank`** | a free global ranking, already computed by Spotify |
| **`top_tracks[].play_count`** | per-track traction, free |

All three arrive in the same request that is already being made. **Zero extra cost.**

**The output sheet hides the flags it was designed to carry.** Columns are Artist, Monthly
Listeners, Country, IG/TikTok, Email, Email Source, Email Quality, Already in DB, Status.
There is **no language column** — so the keep-and-flag policy for 306 language-unclear leads
produces a flag nobody can see. The country flag survives (`US (via IG)`, `country unknown`);
the language one does not.

**The no-contact pool is visible and unexplained.** In the 25-artist sample, **8 had no contact
at all** (32%) — no handle, no email. Same shape as the Twitch pool that turned out to be a
single unread field. Not investigated here.

## 7. Top five improvements, ranked by (leads x quality) / effort

| # | change | why | effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Record a per-lead `source` field** (playlist name / listenbrainz / related-expansion) | Unlocks every future seed decision, and makes §5's unverifiable claim measurable. Everything below is guesswork without it. | **tiny** — one column |
| **2** | **Retire or replace Fresh Finds Country** | 3/8 in-band, 5 of 8 under the floor. It is the worst seed and is costing a seventh of discovery on artists that get dropped free. | **tiny** — one config line |
| **3** | **Add `followers` and the followers/listeners ratio** | Free, already in the payload, and a genuinely better quality signal than reach alone. Would let the funnel rank leads instead of just filtering them. | **small** |
| **4** | **Surface the language flag in the output sheet** | 306 flagged leads are currently indistinguishable from clean ones in the deliverable. The policy is right; the reporting drops it. | **small** |
| **5** | **Investigate the no-contact pool (32% of sampled leads)** | Twitch recovered 16 of 17 from one unread field. Same symptom, unexamined. | **medium** |

Explicitly *not* recommended: reordering the filter stack (already correct), loosening country
or language (they never fired), or promoting the trial playlist to permanent (it earns its
place on the numbers, but the community-id fragility is unchanged).

**Nothing was implemented.**

## Limits

- **n=8 per seed, 56 artists total.** Per-seed rates carry roughly ±17pt at 95%. The direction
  of the two large moves is trustworthy; the exact fractions are not.
- **Country and language never fired**, so §2 cannot clear them of false positives — it can
  only report that they had nothing to reject on this seed set.
- The unknown-bucket classification is **my reading of 25 artist names**, not a lookup. Names
  are weak evidence of nationality.
- The last run's drop counts quoted in the brief (139 too-big, 216 wrong-country, 23
  wrong-language) **could not be reconciled** — dropped artists are not persisted by any run
  artifact, so §2's sample is a fresh free re-measure, not the same population.
- Two of my own measurement bugs were found and fixed mid-audit (playlist `ArtistRef.id` is an
  empty string with the id only in the `uri`; `monthly_listeners` is top-level in `to_dict()`,
  not under `stats`). Both were in the audit script, **not** in the shipped funnel — the live
  run resolves listeners correctly. Noting them because the first is a trap the shipped code
  already had to fix once.
