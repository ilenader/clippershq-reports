## 0. The root cause, in one sentence

**`control.py` quoted precision and recall against the hold-out's *sample size* (71) instead of
each metric's own denominator — 28 delivered accounts for precision, 34 hand-judged real pages
for recall — so both figures read as if they rested on 71 samples and neither carried an
interval, even though both denominators are recorded correctly twelve lines apart in
`repost_finder.py:91-93`.**

That sentence was written before a line of code was changed. The rest of this report is the
evidence for it and the fix that follows from it.

## 1. Where the two numbers came from — they were not invented

The brief was right to say so. Both are real measurements, correctly recorded at their source
and mis-presented downstream.

**Origin: commit `0463213`, 2026-07-28**, *"Repost finder: English gate + duplicate guard take
hold-out precision 73% → 96.4%"*. Its message states the experiment in full:

> Hand-judged on a fresh **71-account hold-out** drawn across all 24 shipped tags (none from
> the 34-account calibration set), the shipped gate delivered 37 accounts at precision 73.0% —
> 13 slips, of which 10 were structurally perfect repost pages in the WRONG LANGUAGE …

and, after the two fixes, the fixture line:

> `off -> 28 delivered, precision 96.4%, recall 79.4%`

**`clippershq/repost_finder.py:91-93` records it correctly, with both denominators:**

```
#     precision 96.4%  (28 delivered, 27 wanted, 1 slip)
#     recall    79.4%  (27 of 34 accounts a human judged to be real English repost pages)
```

So the population is one 71-account hold-out. Within it, 34 accounts were real pages; the
funnel delivered 28; 27 of those were right.

| figure | true fraction | what 71 would have to give |
|---|---|---|
| precision 96.4% | **27 / 28** | 71 × 0.964 = **68.44** — not an integer |
| recall 79.4% | **27 / 34** | 71 × 0.794 = **56.37** — not an integer |

Neither is expressible as k/71, which is the arithmetic tell that 71 is not the denominator.
The smallest n at which 96.4% exists at all is **28**; for 79.4% it is **34** — exactly the two
denominators the source records.

## 2. What he should have been reading

`27/28` and `27/34` are small samples, and their intervals say so:

| | value | Wilson 95% | width |
|---|---|---|---|
| precision | 96.4% (27 of 28 delivered) | **[82.3, 99.4]** | 17.1 pts |
| recall | 79.4% (27 of 34 real pages) | **[63.2, 89.7]** | **26.4 pts** |

**The honest reading, and it is the answer to §4 of the brief:** on this evidence the funnel
**may miss as many as one real page in three**, and **about one in five of what it delivers may
be wrong**. That is materially worse than "96.4% precision" sounds, and it is the number that
belongs on a screen where he is deciding whether to spend.

It is not a reason to stop using the funnel — the same hold-out measured precision **73.0%
(27 of 37 delivered)** before the English gate and duplicate guard, so those two fixes are real
and large. It is a reason to size the run knowing recall may be 63%.

## 3. Every operator-facing figure, audited

`scratch/bl1254_audit.py` → `scratch/bl1254_audit.json`. A percentage quoted to one decimal is
a claim about a fraction k/n; if no integer k in [0, n] produces it, the figure cannot come
from a sample of that size.

**27 performance figures across 14 surfaces; 22 fail the rule.**

| rank | surface | figures failing |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — he reads it before spending** | `clippershq/control.py` :2718, :3861 | **4** (96.4% and 79.4%, twice) |
| 2 — standing operator docs | `docs/FINAL_STATE.md` :113/:120/:135/:137, `docs/HANDOVER.md` :155/:156 | 6 |
| 3 — docstrings and reference docs | `clippershq/clip_pipeline.py` :435 ×3, :3244, :3477, :3478; `repost_finder.py` :126, :127; `docs/GROUND_TRUTH.md` :38 ×2, :79 ×2 | 12 |

Only the rank-1 four are *unreachable at a stated n*; the other eighteen state **no denominator
at all**, which fails the same rule for the same reason.

**Two false findings my own sweep produced, and how they were removed** — recorded because a
tool that manufactures failures is the defect family this round exists to close:

- The first pass read an n from the two lines either side of a figure and reported
  `repost_finder.py:91` — *"precision 96.4% (28 delivered, 27 wanted, 1 slip)"* — as a failure.
  That line is **correct**; the sweep had picked up the 34 and 71 from the neighbouring lines of
  the same comment block. Fixed by judging a figure only against an n on **its own line**.
- The parenthetical pattern then matched `(27 of 34 …)` as denominator **27** — the numerator —
  and called 79.4% unreachable at n=27. Fixed by trying the `k of N` form **before** `N delivered`.

## 4. The guard, and the proof that it can fail

`tests/test_bl1254_stated_n.py` — **7 checks, in the suite, PASS**.

**The rule it enforces:** every performance figure on a surface the operator reads must carry
**both** its denominator and its interval, or must not be shown.

**Scope is deliberately narrow** — `clippershq/control.py` and `dashboard/static/*.html`, the
surfaces whose text is emitted to him. The same defect exists in eighteen places across `docs/`
and two module docstrings; a guard that redded the suite on all twenty-two would be switched off
within a round, which is worse than no guard. Those are named in §3 for a follow-up.

**It is proven to fail, not asserted to.** `TheGuardCanFail` holds the offending line verbatim
as a literal and requires the checker to reject it, so the proof survives the fix:

```
test_the_guard_fails_on_the_prefix_source ............ ok
test_the_guard_rejects_a_figure_with_no_denominator .. ok
test_the_guard_rejects_a_figure_with_a_denominator_but_no_interval ... ok
test_the_guard_accepts_a_properly_stated_figure ...... ok
test_reachability_arithmetic ......................... ok   (96.4%@28 yes, @71 no; 91.3%@22 no, @23 yes)
test_integers_are_deliberately_not_flagged ........... ok
```

And run against the **pre-fix tree** it was red on the real file, captured in
`scratch/bl1254_guard_proof.txt` at HEAD `a9d1b4a`:

```
clippershq/control.py:2718  96.4% is UNREACHABLE at the stated n=71 (no integer k gives k/71 = 96.4%)
clippershq/control.py:2718  79.4% has NO INTERVAL on its line
clippershq/control.py:3861  96.4% has NO DENOMINATOR on its line
```

**Three times the guard flagged correct code and was tightened rather than tolerated** — each
one is commented in the file, because each is the same family it guards:

1. It reported *"no denominator"* on the pre-fix line instead of *"unreachable at n=71"*, because
   `71-account` was not recognised as a stated n. Recognising it makes the diagnosis exact: the
   screen did not omit a sample size, it **named one the figures cannot come from**.
2. It flagged my own explanatory **comment** quoting the old text. A Python comment is not a
   surface. Comment-only lines are now dropped.
3. It split `"…precision 96.4% "` from `"(27 of 28 delivered) [82.3, 99.4]"` — one emitted
   sentence written as two source lines — and then read the 28 as the denominator for the 79.4%
   beside it. Adjacent string-literal continuations are now joined into the sentence he reads.

A fourth: the corrected sentence carries 28, 34 **and** 71, and testing every figure against
every n flagged the correct line three times. The rule is now **reachable at at least one stated
n**, which still catches the pre-fix line, where the only n on offer was 71.
