## ITEM 1 — the view floor N, measured

4 niches × 50 channels = **200 channels**. `podcaster` and `coach` are the shipped presets;
`fitness` and `gaming` were hand-supplied because `NICHE_QUERIES` has no entry for them.

**100 of 200 channels fail the 50,000-subscriber floor.** Their `viewCount` distribution:

| niche | failing | min | p25 | **median** | p75 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| podcaster | 16/50 | 2,527 | 404,117 | **1,736,047** | 4,814,280 | 12,870,131 |
| fitness | 43/50 | 4,014 | 68,336 | **317,727** | 1,783,637 | 12,475,207 |
| gaming | 8/50 | 715,277 | 897,442 | **2,624,169** | 4,418,896 | 9,117,069 |
| coach | 33/50 | 3,666 | 40,692 | **259,361** | 631,158 | 17,431,223 |
| **pooled** | **100** | 2,527 | 99,086 | **484,772** | 1,919,575 | 17,431,223 |

Pooled p90 = **5,391,794**.

### What each candidate N rescues

| N | rescued of 100 failing | share |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000,000 | 34 | 34% |
| 2,000,000 | 23 | 23% |
| **3,000,000** | **20** | **20%** |
| 4,000,000 | 16 | 16% |
| 5,000,000 | 11 | 11% |
| 8,000,000 | 6 | 6% |
| 16,000,000 | 1 | 1% |

### Recommendation: **N = 3,000,000**, config-editable

The reasoning, not just the number:

- The bulk of sub-floor channels are genuinely small — **median 484,772 views, p25 99,086**.
  Any N at or below 1M re-admits a third of them and undoes what the subs floor is for.
- **3,000,000 sits above the pooled p75 (1,919,575)**, so it rescues only the top fifth: the
  channels with real reach that happen to have few subscribers. That is exactly the population
  the rescue is meant for.
- It holds up per-niche rather than only in aggregate — 38% of podcaster failures, 38% of
  gaming, 19% of fitness, 9% of coach. No niche is left with nothing, none is flooded.
- 5,000,000 is defensible but conservative (11%); 2,000,000 dips below p75 and starts pulling
  in the mid-range.

**The brief's prior of "somewhere between 2M and 5M" is confirmed, and 3M is its centre.**

### One finding that weakens the premise, stated plainly

The change was motivated by a podcast at **626 views per subscriber** being dropped while a
218k-sub channel with worse engagement passed. Measured across 200 channels, **views-per-
subscriber does not separate the two groups**:

| group | n | median v/sub | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FAIL the subs floor | 100 | **176** | 671 | 1,848 |
| PASS the subs floor | 99 | **213** | 678 | 1,551 |

Channels below the floor do **not** have systematically better engagement — the passing group's
median is slightly *higher*. The 626 case is real but is an outlier, not a pattern.

**This does not kill the change; it re-frames it.** `subs OR viewCount` is still worth having,
because it rescues genuinely high-reach channels the subs floor misses. But it should be sold
as a **reach** rescue, not an **engagement** rescue, and nobody should expect a systematic
quality lift from it.

## ITEM 2 — the fill rate collapses once the country gate is honoured

Measured on the same 200 channels, `snippet.description` routed through
`bio_parser.valid_email` and `role_policy.quality`:

| population | n | raw regex | after `valid_email` | personal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ALL sampled channels** | 200 | 26 (13.0%) | 25 (12.5%) | 25 (12.5%) |
| **AFTER the country gate** | **99** | 8 (8.1%) | **7 (7.1%)** | **7 (7.1%)** |

**The country gate drops 101 of 200 channels — half the sample.** Of those, **18 carried a
usable description email**: `kuji podcast` (RU), `Flow Podcast` (BR), `Miloš Živković — Profa
Podcast` (RS), `Safwan Nazri Podcast` (MY), `Fitness coach Ali` (IN), `Om Bisht Fitness Coach`
(IN), and 12 more.

Parsed before the gate, those 18 count as wins. **They never convert.** The pre-gate figure
overstates the real yield by roughly **76%** (25 vs 7).

This independently reproduces BL-750's correction on a 4× larger sample and in the same
direction: BL-748's 10.0% was a raw pre-guard, pre-gate rate. **The honest post-gate number is
~7%**, close to the brief's 8%/6% derivation from n=50.

Country distribution of the sample explains it: **US 54, IN 37, none 36, RS 11, IT 7, DE 5,
GB 5, CO 3.** The Balkan and Indian skew the brief describes is visible and large.

## Limits

- **200 channels from 4 single search queries**, one moment in time. YouTube search is
  personalised and ranked; a different query wording moves the population.
- **N is recommended from 100 failing channels.** The per-niche cells are 8–43, so the pooled
  row is the trustworthy one and the per-niche rows are directional.
- **`hiddenSubscriberCount` was true on zero channels**, so item 3 is unmeasurable here — its
  value rests on the API documenting the flag, not on observed frequency.
- The 7.1% post-gate figure treats **country-unknown as surviving**, matching the funnel's
  keep-and-flag policy. 36 of 200 had no country; if those skew foreign, the real rate is lower.
- **No code was changed and no backup was taken**, because nothing was modified. Suites were
  not run for the same reason — there is no diff to test.
