## THE ONE NUMBER

> **The baseline of 97 was never a measurement of the judge. It is 100 minus the three pages
> the operator overturned, and it is forced by arithmetic.**
>
> `agreement = 97 − (disagreements with v4 on a GOOD row) + (disagreements on a BAD row)`
> **v4: 97 − 0 + 0 = 97. v8: 97 − 9 + 1 = 89.** The identity reproduces both exactly.

`ground_truth/BL-1257-FRESH100_marks.jsonl` stores the **baseline judge's own verdict** and the
operator's GOOD/BAD **on that verdict**. `old_verdict` equals the file's `judge_verdict` on
**100 of 100** rows. **Not one of the 100 marks carries a written reason — the record has no
reason field at all.** So truth is v4's answer wherever he pressed GOOD (97 pages) and its
opposite where he pressed BAD (3 pages: `acog.hitbox`, `maestremoros`, `thedunkcentralhq`).

**A judge that reproduced v4 exactly would score 97 without seeing a single grid.** The only
way past 97 is to flip exactly those three. The fall to 89 is therefore not, on its face, a
fall in accuracy: **it is the size of v8's disagreement with v4, priced at one point per page,
on pages where the "right answer" is a silent button press confirming v4.**

This does not make v8 right. It makes the file unable to say. **Which is exactly why §2 puts
the nine pages in front of him** — his eyes are the only instrument that can settle them.

### Why no page was re-judged, and why that is not a shortcut

v8's C24 forces the judge to emit **three counts on every page** — `qualifying_tiles`,
`still_tiles`, `low_band_dialogue_tiles` — plus the chip that fired and the C25 step it fired
at. **The counts are the judge's observations of the grid; the verdict is a deterministic
function of them under C25's order.** So every counterfactual ruleset in §3 is evaluated by
re-running that function over the recorded counts. No grid was re-read and no agent token was
spent on judging.

This rests on the counts being right, and **they are the counts that were already checked**:
BL-1285 had a second agent re-count all ten moved grids from the images, blind, and it matched
the judge on every one — **MISCOUNT: 0**.
