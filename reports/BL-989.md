## 3. What I got wrong — item 5 happened to the tool that was checking item 5

My first premise harness read `MATCHER_FIELDS` with

```python
re.search(r"MATCHER_FIELDS = \((.*?)\)", src, re.S)
```

Non-greedy. On the working-tree copy it stopped at the `)` inside the comment `(MEMEBOT-088)` and reported

```
working tree adds: nothing
```

while `git diff` plainly showed `track_title` being added. **A confident wrong answer from static analysis of a construct containing a parenthesis** — which is precisely what item 5 warns about (*"a resolver that handled `clip.get(f)` and missed `for f in (...): clip.get(f)` already cost a round"*), reproduced by me, inside the check for it. Fixed with `ast.literal_eval` on the tuple node. A greedy regex would have failed differently, on the first `)` after the last field.

**The general form: do not parse Python with a regex, and when the round's own brief tells you a resolver got this wrong before, that includes your resolver.**
