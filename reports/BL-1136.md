# BL-1136 — the audio signal is wired, and the wall is not where we thought

**One sentence:** BPM and danceability now ride on 191 clips at the MEASURED tier behind a
music-only gate; the matcher shows what the waveform says beside what the picture says; and
measuring the funnel per clip found that the phrase lists are worth **7 clips of 2,744**
while the real constraint is a set of gates the operator asked for on purpose.

**Spend: $0.0180 of a $0.50 cap** — 30 billed Instagram pages. Every other leg was free
(local ffmpeg, ONNX OCR, Essentia in WSL2, rules over text).

`tests/test_clip_audio_fields.py` — 15 tests, green — asserts all three points together,
because a field in one and not the others does not raise, it silently drops the value.

**The sweep, read back rather than trusted.** 226 eligible clips → **191 written, 191 read
back**, 0 failed, 0 silent, 3.6 s/clip. Not one non-music clip carries an audio field.

**Danceability works; BPM largely does not, and the record says so.**

| | p10 | p50 | p90 | range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| danceability | 0.145 | 0.452 | 0.875 | 0.039 – 1.000 |
| bpm | 85.1 | 116.6 | 151.9 | — |

**128 of the 191 tempo measurements are SUSPECT** (median confidence 0.99 against a 1.5
floor). `bpm_is_suspect()` names it per clip and the README flags it wherever a number is
shown. Energy bands: still 66, moving 77, driving 48.

**Mood is deliberately absent.** BL-1135 measured Essentia's five mood heads at 6/12 against
the clips' own text, with `sad` firing on half regardless — the heads are trained on music.
Reading the text gives a mood on 20/20. A mood field would make noise readable, and a
readable number gets used.

### 30 clips have audio no ffmpeg on earth can decode

The first sweep silently dropped 35 of 226 and reported no failures. The cause:

```
[aac] AAC USAC Mps212 is not implemented. Update your FFmpeg version…
ffmpeg exits 69 — and still writes a valid 78-byte WAV containing no samples.
```

xHE-AAC with MPEG Surround 2-1-2, on **ffmpeg 8.0**, the newest build. 30 clips; 5 more have
no audio stream at all. The sweep now checks the return code, names the decoder's own words,
and closes its arithmetic: **35 of 35 eligible-and-unmeasured accounted for.**

ground truth yet on whether an energy mismatch costs a post, and a threshold fitted to a
hunch is worse than a flag someone reads.

## 5. One pair shipped, and a defect found by looking at the frame

### `outputs_bl1136_operator/`

**`C:\Users\Game Centar\OneDrive\Desktop\clipper finder\outputs_bl1136_operator\`**

One pair, `01_solidshampooz__ORIGINAL.mp4` and `01_solidshampooz__OURS.mp4`, both re-muxed
with `+faststart` so they double-click. **Verified 1 of 1 from the files**, eleven checks:
0.0% added canvas on magenta with a live control at 67.7%, picture outside the strip at
1.03/255 mean absolute error, full-width strip, no chipped corner, 14.0 s, non-silent, bed
correlation 0.987, ledger row joinable.

His line and ours are the same line: `Me when bro's toxic ex tries to get back with him:`,
redrawn at 96 px on a white strip over his own card. It fired on the phrase
`tries to get back with him` **in the clip's own burned-in line**, with the picture
confirming. Its audio is one of the 30 undecodable ones, so BPM and danceability are absent
rather than zero, and the README says which.

### Two renders were made, and only one survived reading the frames

The second render passed all eleven checks and was **a still photograph of a Chris Isaak
album cover**, with a white strip over it and the fight song under it.

`clip_motion.looks_static` has answered that question correctly since MEMEBOT-098 and **the
render path had never asked it.** Motion 0.08 against a graphic range of 0.20–0.35 and a
real-clip range of 5.0–23.0. Every one of the eleven checks asks whether the picture was
ALTERED; none asks whether it MOVES. The gate is asked now and it refuses **3 of 14**.

It also matched on `really hit that` — a hype phrase meant for an impact, fired by
"he really hit that **note**". **PROPOSED, not applied:** narrow that phrase, or add `note`
to the hype rule's `excludes_any`. It is the operator's file.

### Three defects fixed in this round's own instruments

1. **`pick_song` was called without the source line**, so it fell through to the `none` tier
   and returned an EMPTY `track_id` — which slipped past a guard written as
   `if song.get("track_id") and …`. The renders were right; the new audio column was blank
   on three clips that had been measured. An empty answer is a disagreement, not an
   agreement.
2. **`render_plan` was called without `text_first=True`** while `pick_song` uses it, so the
   two answered from different tiers and disagreed on five clips out of seven.
3. **The ledger was keyed on the PLAYED window, not the marked hook.** `fit_window` stretches
   the window to cover the video (a short hook truncates the clip), so a 15.8 s clip pushed
   h1's 13.769–29.369 out to 31.14 and the row came back
   *"this window is not one of its marked hooks"* — an outcome that would attach to nothing.
   The join key is the hook; the played window rides beside it. Writes are idempotent by
   `(round, clip_id, output)`.

And one in the verifier: it filtered the ledger on `round == "BL-1128"`, so it reported
`joinable: True` about a **different round's row for a different hook window**. A stale True
is worse than a False — it reads as evidence.

### A threshold that was copied as the wrong kind of thing

BL-1130's `CARD_PREFER_MAX_FRAC = 0.32` is a *selection preference among clips that all
pass*. Copied here as a hard drop it became the binding constraint: 4 of 7 candidates refused
at 32%, 35%, 36% and 40%. A large card is the **source's** property — `measure_label_card`
returns the static region its text sits on, and spec 3 says cover it with no gap above. The
fraction is now measured, reported and sorted on; only past **half the frame** does it refuse.
