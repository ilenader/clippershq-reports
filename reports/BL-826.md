### Which drifted, and why

- **Twitch $0.40 → $0.14.** The published figure predates the Helix discovery work and the free-email recovery (panels, X bios, website scrape before paid resolve). 68% email fill at $0.21 per 1,000 deliverable addresses makes it the cheapest paid funnel by a wide margin.
- **Spotify $0.89 → $0.39.** Two causes: the IG-country backfill and negative caching removed repeat paid resolves, and 53% email fill is higher than the published figure assumed. **Note this is per *row*; BL-764's $0.89 was a different quantity** and both can be right.
- **IG/TikTok crawl $0.60 → $0.89 per row — but the row figure is the wrong one to quote.** Only **85 of 1,961** recent crawl rows (4.3%) carry an email, so the cost of a *contactable* crawl lead is **$20.63 per 1,000**. That is 98× the Twitch equivalent. The crawl is a handle machine, not an email machine, and pricing it per row hides that.
- **Google Play $0.00 holds exactly** — 212 rows, 212 emails, zero metered calls. Play mandates a public developer address, so the email is free and the fill is 100%.

## Honest limits

- **`run.log` rotated mid-audit.** Tonight's runs, including any post-stall-fix Spotify timing, are not recoverable from it. BL-791's 49.6-minute figure is therefore **cited, not verified**. This is the largest gap in the report.
- **The ledger-window join is an approximation.** Spend since 2026-07-11 is divided by rows added since 2026-07-11, but a run straddling the boundary, or spend on rows that were deduped away rather than appended, both distort it. Twitch's 877 rows in-window against 877 lifetime suggests that funnel is entirely inside the window; the crawl's 1,961-of-55,349 is mostly outside it.
- **~1% of the ledger ($0.0402) is estimated, not metered** — the `*_killed*_unmetered` back-fills.
- **Research/production is my classification**, by label and campaign heuristics. Reasonable people would bucket `REPOST_VALIDATION` or `CAPTION_FINDER` differently, which would move the 32.5% by several points. The method is in the report so it can be re-run with different rules.
- **Time is valued at a nominal $20/h** purely to make the ratio legible. No real labour cost is known.
- **A paid run was live throughout** (`bl806_grow.py`, 3,272 s CPU at start of audit) and will add to both spend and master after this timeline closes.
