## Method / limits

**"Reconstructed" is detected by campaign and label markers** (`backfill`, `estimate`,
`reconstruct`, `retro`, `recover`). A back-filled row that used none of those words is counted
as metered, which makes the **receipts floor no larger than it truly is** — the conservative
direction, and the one that matters for a floor.

**77.1% of spend can never be receipted.** HikerAPI and TikHub expose no balance, so those legs
are calls × unit price forever. The only leg that could be reconciled is LamaTok's $0.34.

**The per-video figure is an average, not a marginal cost**, and the two differ by more than
two orders of magnitude ($0.0437 vs ~$0.0012). Quoting the average as "what a video costs"
would be the same error as quoting the ledger total as receipts.
