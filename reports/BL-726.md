## Two corrections before the numbers

**1. The client-side English TEXT re-check does not exist.** The brief describes a filter that
"reads bio, title and panel text". There is exactly **one** language check in the Twitch path,
`twitch_finder.py:620-621`, and it calls `market_filter.classify_lang_code(res["stream_language"])`
— the stream's own ISO code from Helix, not text. Nothing reads bio/title/panel for language.
So there is no text filter to loosen, and no 15 false positives to hand-check. What that
check actually drops is measured below (it is zero).

**2. The dropped rows are discarded, so the false-positive hand-audit could not be run.**
`tw.run` returns `results` containing **only channels that passed** (86 results, 86
`passed_filter`). The 12 adult drops and 6 timeouts exist as *counts* in `filter_reasons` and
`adult_by_source`, but the rows themselves — logins, matched signals, titles — are gone by the
time the caller sees anything. Section 3's "hand-check every one, with the exact matched
signal" is **not answerable without a code change that retains dropped rows.** That change was
not made: it is a pipeline edit and the audit brief was a measurement round.

| stage | calls | total s | mean s | share of network time |

| **3a website fetch (FREE scrape)** | **682** | **1409.2** | 2.066 | **81.6%** |

**The slowest stage is the free website scrape, by a factor of nine over the next one.** 682
fetches for 86 channels — roughly **8 fetches per channel**. The paid resolve everyone worries
about is 3.9%.

**Concurrency is real and it is working.** 1,409 s of website fetching completed inside a
320 s wall — an effective **4.4x** parallel speedup. It is not queueing on a shared limiter.

**Helix discovery is nowhere near the rate limit, confirmed.** The whole census below took
**31 pages = ~31 points** against a documented **800 points/min** (the client reserves 700).
That is ~4% of the budget, and the two discovery calls in the audit run took 2.1 s total.
Discovery is free, instant, and not a constraint.

**The 7.5x offline-fixture figure does not describe this funnel.** It measured per-candidate
fan-out with no network. Here the per-channel work is dominated by a scrape that already
parallelises well; the honest headline is 3.65 s/channel wall, not a speedup multiple.

## 3. WHAT IS BEING THROWN AWAY

### Drop reasons, live run

| reason | count |
|---|---|
| passed_filter | **86** |
| adult_content_filtered | 6 |
| adult_content | 6 |
| timeout | 6 |

`adult_by_source`: `{twitch-panels: 6, free-links: 5, instagram: 1}`
`aggregator_status`: `{parsed: 19, unparsable: 8, unreachable: 5}`

**Note what is absent: zero `low_viewers`, zero `too_many_viewers`, zero
`non_target_language`.** Those constraints are applied at the Helix *query* level during
discovery, so channels arrive at `find_contact` already in band and already English. The
per-channel filters never see a violation to reject.

### API-level English filter — **confirmed, still 0 leaks**

The census returned **2,669 streams across both categories, every one `language: "en"`**
(`{en: 2302}`, `{en: 367}`). That extends the prior 0-in-2,654 result to 0 in 2,669.

### Client-side language re-check — **drops 0 beyond the API filter**

`non_target_language` = **0** across 86 channels. Since it re-checks the same ISO code the API
already filtered on, agreement is structural, not luck. **It is not killing English streamers,
because it is not a text filter at all** (see correction 1). No loosening needed; the
keep-and-flag path for a missing code is already the behaviour (`language unknown`).

### Adult/paid-content filter — 12 drops, **hand-check BLOCKED**

12 of 104 channels reaching the filter were dropped (6 pre-panels, 6 post-panels), sourced
`twitch-panels: 6, free-links: 5, instagram: 1`. **The matched signal per channel is not
recoverable** — dropped rows are not returned (correction 2). Given the brief explicitly cites
a past false positive from "must be 18+" in chat rules, and given `ADULT_KEYWORDS_AMBIGUOUS`
still contains `spicy`, `sexy`, `hot tub`, `thicc`, **a 12-drop rate on 104 channels (11.5%)
is high enough to be worth the retention fix before trusting it.** I am not claiming these are
false positives; I am reporting that the run cannot tell you either way.

### Sponsor / platform-junk quarantine — **not observable**

`is_sponsor_url` and `handle_is_owned` reject at field level inside `find_contact`; the
rejected value and its reason are not surfaced in `results`. No quarantine list exists to
audit. Same blocker.

### Viewer ceiling at 1,000 — **29 channels dropped for being too big**

27 in Just Chatting, 2 in IRL, out of 2,669. That is **1.1% of supply**, and they are genuinely
large (Just Chatting tops out at 35,263 concurrent). Against a 260-channel in-band pool the
ceiling is costing you almost nothing, and the rationale — a >1k streamer likely already has
clippers — is intact. **No change recommended.**

## Method

- Census: `scratch/twitch_audit_census.py` — Helix `Get Streams`, `language=en`, paginated to
  cursor exhaustion. FREE (1 point/page).
- Audit run: `scratch/twitch_audit_run.py` — wraps every network boundary with a timer, calls
  `control._find_twitch(..., confirm_fn=lambda: True, categories=["Just Chatting","IRL"])`,
  captures `tw.run`'s stats dict. Dollar-capped at $0.05, actual $0.0180.
- Delivered: 64 new leads (43 with an email, 21 DM-only), 38 emails + 39 handles, 5 m 16 s.
- Artifacts: `scratch/twitch_audit/census.json`, `streams_Just_Chatting.json`,
  `streams_IRL.json`, `auditrun.json`.

## Limits

- **The false-positive audit for adult, sponsor and junk filters was not delivered** — the
  pipeline discards dropped rows. This is the single biggest gap and it needs a retention
  change plus one more live run.
- **One run, one moment.** Twitch supply is time-of-day dependent; this was ~21:45–21:57 local
  on a Wednesday. In-band counts will move.
- The no-contact pool is **10 rows**.
- `3b_link_enrich` shows a 8.9 s mean over only 18 calls — high variance, thin sample.
- Game categories were not measured at all.
- The floor recommendation rests on free liveness proxies (uptime, title, tags), not on whether
  those streamers would actually hire a clipper. Buyer fit at 10–49 viewers is **untested**.
