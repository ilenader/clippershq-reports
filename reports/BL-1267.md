## The answer in one sentence

**Nobody ever decided to pay for judging.** The judge that scores **97 of 100** against his
marks was run by BL-1257 on Claude Code sub-agents for **$0.0000** — `spend.json` contains
**zero** BL-1257 rows — and the paid API harness was built *afterwards*, by BL-1258, to chase a

to measure it precisely was to own the API call, so judging moved onto a meter in order to be
measured, and then stayed there.

### The free path's real constraints, all observed in these 14 runs

1. **It needs a live session.** No unattended, scheduled or headless run.
2. **No receipt** — nothing to reconcile against `spend.json`.
3. **No truncation signal** — a cut reply looks like a normal one.
4. **Output is not strictly JSON.** 1 of 14 wrapped its reply in a ```json fence. A free runner must parse defensively.
5. **A shared scratchpad is a live hazard.** One agent reported reading a tile crop written by a *concurrent* agent, caught it, re-cropped to a unique directory and re-verified. The paid harness has `_assert_independent()` to stop exactly this; **the free path has no equivalent and needs per-agent output directories.**
6. **Wall clock is long-tailed** — mean 42.0 s/page, range 20.9 s to 294.8 s. A wave finishes when its slowest agent does.
7. **Concurrency caps at about 20 agents**, and dispatchers return before their children finish.

## 4. The finding that came free with this, and it is the important one

**Two of BL-1262's three "fixes" were the answers written into the brief, not rules the judge
learned.** v7 names six of the hundred pages by handle. Verbatim, from v7:

> `acog.hitbox` is **7 of 9 textless character renders** with two lined tiles. Its two
> qualifying tiles are real, and under C14 alone it would pass — **C15 is what rejects it**

> `thedunkcentralhq` has **zero** qualifying tiles … so it is a **REJECT**

That is the count, the deciding rule, and the verdict, handed over. **v8 removes every handle**
(verified: 0 of the 100 appear) and keeps the rules. Re-judged blind:

| page | v7 with the answer in the brief | v8, decontaminated |
|---|---|---|
| `maestremoros` | WANT ✓ | **WANT ✓ — survived** |
| `acog.hitbox` | REJECT ✓ | **WANT ✗ — free judge counts 0 of 9 stills, not 7** |
| `thedunkcentralhq` | REJECT ✓ | **WANT ✗ — free judge counts 2 qualifying, not 0** |

`maestremoros` survived because v8 kept its *shape* ("three clean first-person hooks") without
its name — that rule generalises. The other two do not: strip the names and the counts come
back different. **The 12/19 that looked like a regression was measured against a brief holding
its own answer key, so it was never a clean number.** This cost $0.00 to find.

Both surviving misses are **counting** disagreements, not transport ones — whether a Rainbow
Six cinematic frame is a "still", and whether "DAVID BECKHAM 🤝 MICHAEL JORDAN" is a qualifying
descriptive label under C2. Those are brief defects and they are open.

## 7. The three plain lines he asked for

**Can the judging be free?** **Yes — and it already was.** The 97-of-100 judge ran on his
subscription for $0.00, and re-running it today on the same pages with the current brief gives
the same verdicts as the paid API, page for page, 11 of 11 on ordinary pages.

**What changes and what does he lose?** Judging moves back to sub-agents in a live session. He
loses four things, none of them accuracy: **unattended/scheduled runs**, **per-call cost
receipts**, **truncation detection**, and **strict-JSON guarantees** — plus he must give each
agent its own output directory, because one agent in fourteen read another's crop off the
shared scratchpad. Keep the paid harness for exactly one job: **anything that has to run
without him at the keyboard.**

**And the honest floor if he pays anyway:** **$0.0298 per page**, not $0.0435 — that is the
current $0.04134 with BL-1261's free pre-sort removing 28% of the pages. Under a cent per page
is **not** reachable on the paid path without either trimming the brief or cutting the
reasoning stream, and the first is measured to cost accuracy while the second is untested. Under
a cent is reachable **today** on the free path, where the number is zero.
