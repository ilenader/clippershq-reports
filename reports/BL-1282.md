## 1. THE LEAK GUARD READ A SET IT COULD NOT LEAK ON

### 1.1 The hardcode, and why it was fatal rather than merely narrow

`scratch/bl1269_leakguard.py` was built to refuse a score whose brief names the pages being
scored. It ran in the suite. It reported CLEAN. Line 75 read **one** file:

```python
marks = "ground_truth/BL-1257-FRESH100_marks.jsonl"      # his 100
```

and line 78 hardcoded **four** brief paths. `tests/test_bl1269_presort.py:97` loaded the same
single file.

Measured: **|his 100 ∩ `ground_truth/review_marks.jsonl`| = 0.** Not "small" — **zero**. The
BL-1240/1242 scoring queue is built from `review_marks.jsonl` (623 handles, `bl1242_queue.json`
carries all 623 in its `truth` map). So the guard was structurally incapable of seeing a leak
from the only corpus that mattered, and it said so in green, every run.

### 1.2 What the fix is, stated as three properties

`mark_sets()` walks the ground-truth directories with **`os.listdir`, never `glob`** and takes
every file whose records carry a handle *and* a human `mark`. `briefs()` reads `scratch/` the
same way. And **an empty corpus is now `rc=2`, not `rc=0`** — below `MIN_SETS=4` /
`MIN_HANDLES=400` the guard refuses to report rather than printing "0 briefs leak". That last
one is the whole family: if the marks file had been renamed, the old guard would have printed
a clean bill of health.

`os.listdir` is not a style preference here. A `glob` in this repo has already dropped a
`WANT` verdict (`agent01/.boovkanio.json`) out of a merge and counted **34 of 35** paid TikTok
sheets.

Corpus after the fix: **14 marked sets, 723 unique handles** (his 100, `review_marks` 623, the
BL-1210 merge, six backups, the root file). `repost_rejections.jsonl` is deliberately **out** —
it is a machine rejection ledger with no human `mark`, and it contains two real accounts
literally named `a` and `b`, which a naive corpus turns into cry-wolf hits on any quoted
letter. That is the guard's own false-positive trap and it is now closed by definition rather
than by an exemption list.

### 1.6 A discovery rule is also a cost, and mine was 900x too expensive

The first version of the widened corpus took `ground_truth/` plus the project root and
accepted `*.jsonl` **or `*.bak`**. The project root holds ~30 `master_leads.csv.*.bak` files —
backups of a 136,553-line CSV — so every call read tens of megabytes it had no use for. One
call: **40 seconds**. The suite's `setUp` ran it eleven times: `test_bl1269_presort.py` went
from a fast suite to **598 seconds, the slowest in the whole run**.

Fixed by matching `.jsonl` **anywhere in the name** (so `review_marks.jsonl.BL-1210.bak`
counts and `master_leads.csv.…bak` does not) and reading the corpus once in `setUpClass`.
**598s → 0.66s, with the corpus identical: 14 sets, 723 handles, 9 leaking briefs.** Worth
recording because a guard that costs ten minutes is a guard someone runs less often, which is
the same ending as a guard that cannot fail.

**A distinction the carried claim blurred, worth keeping.** `bl1270_brief_tt2.md` was correctly
reported clean against the 159 TikTok handles it was scored on. Both facts are true: it did not
contaminate the TikTok score, **and** it carries all 15 Instagram leaks forward in inherited
text, so the moment that brief is pointed at an Instagram set it is contaminated. The guard
checks the union for exactly this reason, and the reason is now written in the module.

### 2.3 Why "mitigated in practice" was not enough

Green under one runner and vacuous under the standard one is precisely the shape this project

suite of 280 tests.

proves the hook refuses an emptied module, and asserts the sweep saw >250 files — because an
empty sweep is not a clean sweep.

### 4.2 Three fixed, ranked by what they cost him

**1 — `main.RunStats.health_flags` said "the source may be dry" when every well had ERRORED.**
The early `return` on `qualified == 0` skipped every check below it, including the well-error

```

genuinely dry      -> the original message, unchanged
```

**2 — `ig_client._Pacer._reserve_shared` failed open at the paid endpoint.** A torn or
unreadable shared-slot file resolved to `nxt = 0.0`, which is exactly the value meaning
"nobody is ahead of you", so **every process that hit the torn file waited zero and fired at
once**. The one condition under which pacing matters most switched it off. Now `FileNotFoundError`
still means "no slot yet, go" and everything else fails **closed** by exactly one interval —
verified: torn file → 0.500s wait where it was 0.000s; absent file → 0.000s, unchanged.

**3 — `tag_yield.discover_logs` turned a listdir failure into "no logs"** (removed; the
absent-directory case was already answered above it).

### 4.3 The remaining 92 are inside a guard now

`tests/test_silent_zero_shape.py` gains `TheRestOfTheTreeIsRatcheted`: the same `scan_boundary`
over **210 files** (tests and `output/` excluded), with the known **92 violations at 88

**Why a baseline and not 88 fixes:** each remaining site needs its own judgement about what the

## 5. ALL 25 THRESHOLDS, CLASSIFIED — `docs/THRESHOLDS.md`

The full table is the document; it is the deliverable and it is meant to be read, not
summarised. The shape of the answer:

* **Inclusion rule, so the list is not a matter of taste:** a module-level constant in a
  judging/filtering module, read by a comparison **directly or through the parameter it is the
  default of**, whose own note cites labels/marks/examples. That second clause matters — a
  sweep that only looks for direct comparisons misses `MIN_TOTAL_POSTS` and
  `DEFAULT_MAX_DIALOGUE_SHARE`, two of the thresholds this exists to classify.
* The sweep finds **64** compared numeric constants across 12 modules; **25** are label-derived
  (24 rows — the two `NEW_PAGE_*` share one). The other 39 are geometry, retries and buffers.
* **21 FITTED-ONLY. 4 VALIDATED. All four validations were refutations.**

The four validated: `MIN_CAPTION_CHARS`, which **reversed as the corpus grew** and was retired
to 0 — the only threshold in the project retired *because* someone measured it out of sample —
and `presort.py`'s `FOLLOWING_CUT` / `RATIO_CUT` / `BIZ_WORD_CUT`, whose held-out numbers are
`ships: false` on every signal individually and **124 killed of 922, 13 of 148 WANTED (8.8%)**
as a union.

### 5.1 Two thresholds provably cannot fire on their own evidence

**`INFOGRAPHIC_BAND_H_MIN = 0.30`** — its own note names its two labelled positives:
**0.233 and 0.147**. Neither clears 0.30. The "second, independent route to the same verdict"
has never been taken. **Left at 0.30 on purpose**: lowering it to 0.20 so it clears the two
cases it was derived from is re-fitting a gate on n=2, which is the failure this document
exists for. Pinned, so the day a labelled positive clears it the suite goes red and someone
writes a measurement.

**`DEFAULT_MAX_DIALOGUE_SHARE = 0.10`** — BL-1210 measured the share rule firing on 0 of 623
pages; it cannot fire on 1 either, because `judge_page` now does literally
`_ = max_dialogue_share`. It is a parameter, not a threshold. Its docstring described a
shipping rule; corrected, and pinned by AST (`max_dialogue_share` must not appear in any
`ast.Compare`).

### 5.2 A shipped module claimed a validation that was a refutation

`clippershq/presort.py:25` read *"(BL-1261, re-validated out of sample by BL-1269)"*. BL-1269
