## How the figure is defined (so the owner trusts the number)
The earnings page sums a clipper's APPROVED earnings **including** clips whose video is gone (`api/earnings/route.ts:64`, no `videoUnavailable` filter). The withdrawal gate sums the same **excluding** gone clips (`payouts/route.ts:411`, `videoUnavailable:false`). Both then subtract what the clipper was already paid and has locked in flight (`computeBalance`, `balance.ts:156-200`). The **stuck** amount per clipper is the difference:

`stuck = max(displayed_earned - paid - locked, 0) - max(live_earned - paid - locked, 0)`

Summed across every clipper this is **$390.60** (24 clippers), reproduced live and reconciled to BL-657's $390.50 (the 10 cent rise is six more days of the daily cron). This replicates `computeBalance` exactly, so it is the same math the two screens use.

## PART 5 — what paying would look like, mechanically (do NOT build here)

There are three ways to hand these 24 people their money; they differ sharply in blast radius.

1. **Per-person one-off payout, created by the owner (SAFEST).** For each of the 24, create a payout for their `stuck` amount against the relevant campaign, the same object a normal cashout produces. **What it touches:** adds 24 `PayoutRequest` rows; touches no clip, no earning, no flag, no rule. **What must be proven:** that the created amount equals the `stuck` column (not the raw gone-clip sum, or the 6 clamped clippers get double-paid), and that the campaign budget lock (`clip-earnings-writer.ts` L1) is not breached, which it will not be because these campaigns are frozen and the money was already inside the earned pool. This is the mechanism that changes the least and is fully auditable.

2. **Lift `videoUnavailable` on these specific clips.** Do NOT. It would make the clips withdrawable but also re-enter them into tracking, budget spend and every other sum, re-opening the exact asymmetry and inviting the post-farm-delete exploit. Broad blast radius.

3. **Balance adjustment.** There is no first-class "credit a balance" primitive independent of clips and payouts; the honest equivalent is option 1. Avoid ad-hoc SQL on earnings.

**Recommended mechanism: option 1**, per-person payouts for the exact `stuck` amounts. It pays the debt, touches nothing structural, and leaves a normal payout audit trail. Pair it with BL-657's display fix (add `videoUnavailable:false` to `earnings/route.ts:64`) so the earnings page stops showing the growing phantom, otherwise the number reappears on screen even after paying.

**If the owner does nothing:** the money stays visible-but-unwithdrawable. It grows about **$1.15/day** from the daily cron (about $0.10 has accrued since BL-657's $390.50), decelerating because the big backlog was already drained by BL-584, but with step-risk: a second mass Instagram takedown like 2026-07-18 would add hundreds in a day. Each affected clipper keeps seeing a balance they cannot withdraw, and will keep messaging about it.
