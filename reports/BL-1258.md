## The answer in one sentence

**The prefix caches exactly as hoped — 9,863 tokens served from cache on 12 of 12 pages, a
6.4x cut in billed input and 95 → 613 pages per million — but the last stretch to 1,000
pages/M requires trimming the brief to a size that measurably costs accuracy, so the target
and the judgement are in direct conflict and I am not recommending the trim.**

## 2. THE PREFIX CACHES — MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED

`cache_control` is a field on the request. A Claude Code sub-agent does not expose it, so
"cache the prefix" was never a setting anyone could flip on the old procedure — it requires
owning the API call. `scratch/bl1258_judge_api.py` owns it.

**Probe, same three pages, uncached arm then cached arm** (`scratch/bl1258_cache_probe.json`):

| arm | prompt tok | from cache | receipt |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| uncached ×3 | 10,487 | 0 | $0.0699 avg |
| cached, call 1 (the WRITE) | 10,487 | 0 (wrote 9,863) | $0.0852 |
| cached, calls 2–3 (READS) | 10,486 | **9,863** | $0.0234 avg |

The multipliers reproduce the billed cost **to the cent**, which is how they are known rather
than looked up:
- write call prompt cost $0.06476 = 624×$5/M + **9,863×$5/M×1.25**
- read call prompt cost $0.00804 = 624×$5/M + **9,863×$5/M×0.10**

**In the real 12-page run the cache held on 12 of 12 pages across 4 concurrent lanes** —
93.8% of every page's input served from cache. Concurrency does not break it.

**And it is not carried context.** Every request is rebuilt from scratch with exactly one
page's image; `_assert_independent` re-checks on every call that no request mentions another
page's handle. A cached prefix means the same *opening bytes* are already resident on the
vendor's side — page 2 still cannot see page 1.

## 4. THE COST MODEL, AND THE CONFLICT

Per page: new input 647 tokens (page 401 + instruction), output 981 tokens on the pages that
parsed. Opus-5 at $5/$25 per MTok, reproduced by the billed cost.

| config | prefix | billed-eq input/page | pages per 1M | $/page |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v4 uncached (today) | 9,856 | 10,503 | **95** | $0.0770 |
| **v4 cached** | 9,856 | 1,633 | **613** | $0.0327 |
| v4 cached, card dropped | 8,178 | 1,465 | 683 | $0.0319 |
| v5 cached | 5,556 | 1,203 | 832 | $0.0305 |
| v6 cached | 3,474 | 994 | **1,006** | $0.0295 |

**Caching alone: 95 → 613 pages per million, a 6.4x cut.** Reaching 1,000 needs the prefix at
**≤3,530 tokens**. Only v6 fits, and §5 shows what v6 costs.

**Two readings of "1 million tokens", and they give different answers.** A cache read still
*sends* the tokens; it just prices them at a tenth. Every page sends **10,503 input tokens
either way = 95 pages/M**, unchanged by caching. If the budget is billed cost, caching is a
6.4x win. If it is literally context read, caching is 1.0x and only trimming moves it. This
is the same distinction BL-1257 flagged and it is now the deciding one.

**Output is now 75% of the bill** — $0.00816 cached input against $0.02453 output per page.
That output is the written reason. It is the thing that moved the loop 77.6% → 86.7%, and
cutting it to chase an *input* target would be paying for the target with the product.

## 7. THE REAL RUN, INCLUDING WHAT BROKE

12 marks-control pages, shipped brief cached, 560² grid crop, legibility gate, 4 lanes
(`scratch/bl1258_run.json`).

- **cache: 9,863 tokens on 12 of 12 pages**, 93.8% of input
- input 10,510 tok/page · output **1,197** tok/page · wall 66.3s · 10.86 pages/min
- **$0.03268/page on the 7 pages that parsed**; $0.04568 on each of the 5 that truncated
- **legibility gate: 0 escalations** — no page exceeded 2 illegible tiles at 560², which is
  the gate behaving correctly on clean captures rather than the gate being untested. It has
  not yet been exercised on a page that needs it.
- **5 of 12 truncated.** `max_tokens=1500` was too low; parsed pages averaged 981 output
  tokens with a long tail, so ~2,600 would clear it. This is a defect of my harness, not of
  caching, and it is the whole of the overrun.

The gate is the right shape for the reason BL-1257 established: **illegible-tile count rises
before agreement falls** (2 → 7 → 8 → 10 while agreement sat flat, and only then did a verdict
flip). Gating on agreement is impossible in production — there is nothing to agree with — and
gating on legibility is the observable that leads it.

## 8. DO NOT SPLIT THE MODEL — STATED PLAINLY SO IT IS NOT PROPOSED AGAIN

**The model that sees the image is the model that judges it.** There is no arrangement in
which something cheap takes the screenshot and something strong reasons about it, because the
screenshot *is* the input to the reasoning; handing over a description instead means the
strong model is judging the cheap model's summary, not the page.

And the cheap model cannot be gated into the loop either: BL-1244 measured **70.0% against
74.0%, no significant difference (McNemar p=0.754)**, while it returned **"high confidence" on
50 of 50 and was wrong on 15**. A confidence signal that is constant regardless of correctness
carries no information, so there is no threshold on which a router could fire. Nothing in this
round changes that, and this round adds a second reason: the tokenizers differ enough between
tiers (7,590 vs 9,858 for identical content) that a cheap model cannot even be trusted to
*price* the strong model's work.

## 10. What I would do next, in order

1. **Ship the cached prefix with the brief unchanged.** 95 → 613 pages/M and $0.077 → $0.033
   per page, for zero accuracy cost. That is the whole free win and it is available today.
2. **Fix `max_tokens` before anything else runs.** ~2,600, and treat `finish_reason=="length"`
   as a hard failure that retries, because a truncated call is billed in full and returns
   nothing. It cost 43.7% of this round's largest run.
3. **Do not trim the brief to hit 1,000 pages/M on this evidence.** Decide instead which
   budget is real — billed cost (caching gets you there) or context tokens (only trimming
   does) — because they disagree by 6.4x and the answer changes what to build.
4. **Strip the two named control handles out of the brief.** They contaminate every future
   measurement that uses them, and they cost accuracy nothing to remove.
5. **Run capture at 6 lanes.** 1,000 pages goes from 84 minutes to 29 for free.
6. **The remaining error is one category.** 4 of 4 of v4's misses against his marks are "Too
   much dialogue". Any further accuracy work should be there and nowhere else.
