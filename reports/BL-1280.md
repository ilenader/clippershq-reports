## 5. The 40-page best-seed experiment — the seed lever is dead

Three `suggested/profiles` calls on the three best-ranked seeds (**$0.0018**). Fresh pages only:
everything already in `meme_pages_seen.json` (2,688) or already judged in `PAGES_WIDE` (1,119) was
dropped **before** the sample was drawn — otherwise the ranking would be scored against itself.
96 fresh survivors, 40 sampled round-robin (14/13/13). Captured free with Playwright: **40/40 with
a grid, 0 errors, 118.1s at 4 lanes.** Judged free by 5 sub-agents at 8 pages each.

> ### 7 of 40 wanted = **17.5%**, Wilson 95% **[8.7%, 31.9%]**
> ### His baseline is **19.0%**. The best seeds came in **below** it.

| seed (by prior judge-verdict rank) | prior claim | **measured, fresh** | Wilson 95% |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| ranked #1 (a meme-page seed) | 81.0% | **3/14 = 21.4%** | [7.6, 47.6] |
| ranked #2 (a large aggregator seed) | 76.5% | **4/13 = 30.8%** | [12.7, 57.6] |
| ranked #3 (a meme-page seed) | 66.7% | **0/13 = 0.0%** | [0.0, 22.8] |

**What went wrong, and it is worth knowing.** The endpoint returns accounts Instagram *clusters*
with the seed, and the clustering is topical, not formal. The 40 fresh pages came back as **techno
and house record labels, DJ and artist pages, and astrology text-card accounts** — 33 rejects
splitting into creator-page (9), brand-or-label (7+3+1+1+1), still-pictures (7+1+1), and one
ambient-footage page. The third seed's name reads as a meme handle and pulled a zodiac cluster.
**A seed ranked on pages that already survived filtering does not predict what that seed returns
cold.**

**The honest limit.** n=40 and the interval is wide: [8.7%, 31.9%] does not *exclude* the 31.3%
the projection needed. What it does is remove the projection's support — the point estimate is
below baseline, and two of three seeds that were supposed to be the best in the system returned
21.4% and 0.0%. **$2.03 was never measured and is now unsupported.**

### One free finding the probe surfaced

The judges were asked, separately from their verdicts, whether the **handle alone** would have
predicted the page. They said yes on 22 of 40 — **and 0 of those 22 were wanted pages.** That looks
like a perfect free kill. **It is not evidence.** They answered *after* seeing the grid; it is
hindsight, not a blind prediction. The blind version of exactly that test is §2, where it fails.
Recorded because it is the shape of a trap the next round will otherwise fall into.

## 7. The arithmetic

```
$/1,000 wanted = calls_per_page × billing_multiplier × price_per_call × 1000 ÷ want_rate
```

**The billing multiplier is this round's correction.** HikerAPI bills **1.062 requests per call**
(401 calls → 426 billed, BL-1179, n=401). BL-1276's $3.30 omitted it. Both columns are shown
because $3.30 is the number on the record and moving it quietly would be worse than showing the gap.
The multiplier was measured on *profile* calls; whether discovery bills identically is unmeasured.

| step | calls/page | want rate | **$/1,000 (BL-1276 basis)** | **$/1,000 (with multiplier)** | clears $3.00? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|:--:|
| today, pre-BL-1276 | 1.443 | 19.0% | $4.56 | $4.84 | no |
| **`profile_and_contact` wired — BANKED** | 1.043 | 19.0% | **$3.29** | **$3.50** | **no** |
| + best free kill (digits ∪ person-name) | 1.043 | 19.0% | $2.92 | $3.10 | **unshippable** |
| **+ best-seed discovery — MEASURED** | 1.043 | **17.5%** | **$3.58** | **$3.80** | **no** |
| ↳ at the CI's pessimistic end (8.7%) | | | | $7.60 | no |
| ↳ at the CI's optimistic end (31.9%) | | | | $2.08 | — |

**Break-even want rates** at 1.043 calls/page, with the multiplier: **$3.00 needs 22.1%**, $2.50
needs 26.6%, $2.00 needs 33.2%. He is at 19.0%.

### The verdict: DOES NOT CLEAR, and it is not borderline — it is the wrong side

- **Banked and stable: $3.30 / $3.50.** That is real, wired and guarded.
- The free-kill row is the trap. **$2.92 dips under $3.00 on the old basis** — and buys it with
  **26.9% of his wanted pages.** Priced per *retained* wanted page it is not a saving at all, and a
  test already forbids the gentler version of it.
- **The seed row moved the wrong way.** The only unmeasured number in BL-1276's table is now
  measured, and it is worse than baseline.

**Stable or borderline?** Neither. **Nothing measured lands under $3.00 by a route that keeps his
wanted pages.** The $3.30/$3.50 figure is stable — it does not move day to day, because it rests
on a wired call-count change, not on a rate estimate. The routes *below* it are all borderline or
unshippable, and the brief's own standard — "not $2.90 one day and $3.30 the next" — rules them out.
