# BL-752 — The audio benchmark completed: the model is not reproducible at temperature 0, and the requested headline number does not exist

## CLOSE IT — but not for the reason the brief anticipated. **Only 1 of 10 clips returns an identical answer when sent twice at temperature 0** (genre matches 4/10). A field that changes when you re-ask it cannot be a library column regardless of its accuracy, so the verdict does not depend on the accuracy threshold at all. Two supporting failures are as bad: on speech-dominant audio the model invents a music genre **11 of 14 times (78.6%)** — including calling a 100%-speech clip *lo-fi* — and the `vocals` field carries **no signal whatsoever**: it reports "speech" on **28.6%** of genuinely speech-dominant clips and on **28.6%** of near-speechless ones. Identical rates. **The requested headline — "genre agreement on music-under-dialogue" — is not computable**, because that stratum has no declared track, no transcript, and I cannot hear audio; inventing labels for it would have been fabrication. ## PART 2 — CONSISTENCY: the finding that decides it

Ten clips, sent twice, **temperature 0.0**, same model, same tier, same prompt.

| field | identical across the two passes | 95% CI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vocals | 9/10 = 90.0% | [59.6, 98.2] |
| has_build | 7/10 = 70.0% | [39.7, 89.2] |
| energy | 6/10 = 60.0% | [31.3, 83.2] |
| **genre** | **4/10 = 40.0%** | [16.8, 68.7] |
| **all four fields** | **1/10 = 10.0%** | [1.8, 40.4] |

Nine of ten clips changed at least one field. The genre flips are not near-misses:

| clip | pass 1 → pass 2 |
| --- | --- |
| *Cico Buff* | funk → pop |
| *The Fountain* | phonk → orchestral-cinematic |
| *dreams.* | orchestral-cinematic → **none** |
| *Funny Pokie* | funk → pop |
| *hallucinations.* | lo-fi → ambient |

**`temperature: 0.0` is being sent and is not producing determinism.** One clip moved from a named genre to `none` — i.e. "there is music" to "there is no music at all" — on identical bytes.

**This is disqualifying on its own.** A clip library column that returns a different answer on re-ask cannot be cached, cannot be audited, and cannot be compared across runs. No accuracy figure rescues that.

## PART 3 — the Hans Zimmer re-test: instability, not a model limit

*Cornfield Chase* (Hans Zimmer, the Interstellar score — piano and strings), sent 5 times:

| pass | genre | energy | build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **hip-hop-rap** | moderate | no |
| 2 | orchestral-cinematic | intense | yes |
| 3 | orchestral-cinematic | intense | yes |
| 4 | orchestral-cinematic | intense | yes |
| 5 | orchestral-cinematic | intense | yes |

**4 of 5 correct; the original failure did not reproduce.** The model *can* identify this track — `orchestral-cinematic` was in the enum and it picked it four times running. The `hip-hop-rap` answer was a one-off stochastic failure.

**Verdict: instability, not a capability ceiling.** That is the worse of the two outcomes for shipping. A systematic blind spot can be documented and worked around; a 1-in-5 flagrant error on a clip the model usually gets right cannot be predicted or filtered, and it is invisible without repeating every call.

## PART 4 — the null case: it invents music that is not there

`C-dialogue` is speech-dominant by construction (speech_frac 0.40–1.00). The corpus builder's stated expectation is `genre='none'`.

| | result | 95% CI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| said `genre='none'` (correct) | **3/14 = 21.4%** | [7.6, 47.6] |

| said `vocals='speech'` | 4/14 = 28.6% | [11.7, 54.6] |

| 0.99 | pop |
| 0.99 | orchestral-cinematic |

**Two clips that are 100% speech were assigned a music genre.** The prompt explicitly says *"Use 'none' ONLY if there is no music at all (pure speech, silence or ambient room noise)"* — the instruction is present and is being ignored.

## PART 5 — item 4: the speech split, and why it is the most useful negative here

The brief's hypothesis was that `vocals=none` might score well while `vocals=speech` scores badly, giving "a usable tool with a documented boundary". **The data refutes the premise it rests on: `vocals` does not detect speech.**

| actual speech content | model said `vocals='speech'` |
| --- | --- |
| speech_frac **≥ 0.40** (genuinely speech-dominant, n=14) | **4/14 = 28.6%** |
| speech_frac **< 0.20** (essentially no speech, n=14) | **4/14 = 28.6%** |

**The same rate on both.** The field is uncorrelated with whether anyone is talking, so it cannot gate anything — you cannot route on it, filter on it, or use it to mark a clip as "dialogue, treat genre as unreliable".

On the graded stratum the split is not computable at all: of the 16 A-declared clips, only **2** returned `vocals='speech'`, and **both are `unverifiable`** (obscure artists) — so the gradeable subset of `vocals=speech` is **n=0**. That is reported as uncomputable rather than papered over with a blended figure.

## PART 6 — what genre accuracy exists

**A-declared** (16 clips whose own `music_info` names a real track and artist) is the only stratum with objective ground truth. Grades come from knowledge of the named artist/track, **not from listening**; `unverifiable` is used where the artist is genuinely obscure.

| grade | n |
| --- | --- |
| correct | 4 |
| defensible | 4 |
| wrong | 4 |
| unverifiable | 4 |

| | of 12 gradeable | 95% CI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| strict correct | **4/12 = 33.3%** | [13.8, 60.9] |
| correct **or** defensible | **8/12 = 66.7%** | [39.1, 86.2] |

The four `wrong` answers include Cocteau Twins (dream pop) → *funk*, a Batman Beyond orchestral title theme → *rock*, and the same artist (`kowlys`) labelled *lo-fi* on one track and *orchestral-cinematic* on another.

**A standing confound, carried forward from the grading file:** the audio sent is the **clip** — the licensed track with the reel's dialogue and edit mixed over it — not the clean master. A divergence from the declared track is therefore not automatically a model error. This inflates `defensible` and is the honest reason the lenient figure is the fairer one.

**`B-music-no-title` is reported ungraded, deliberately.** No declared track, no transcript, and I cannot hear audio. Its output distribution is in the assets (genre: pop 7, orchestral-cinematic 5, none 4, rock 2, hip-hop-rap 1, electronic-edm 1), and for the brief's literal question the 6 clips with speech_frac ≥ 0.20 returned orchestral-cinematic 2, pop 2, hip-hop-rap 1, rock 1 — with speech heard on only **1 of 6**. **No accuracy number is derivable from that and none is offered.**

**`energy` and `has_build` are reported ungraded everywhere**, exactly as instructed: no ground truth exists for them and I cannot hear the audio, so any figure would be invented.

## VERDICT

The brief set: above ~80% ships, below ~60% closes, in between say so and state what n would settle it.

**The requested headline number does not exist.** "Genre agreement on music-under-dialogue" cannot be computed on `B-music-no-title` — no declared track, no transcript, no audio perception on my side. That is a limit of the corpus, not a result, and it is stated rather than filled with a guess.

**Every number that could be computed says close it:**

| measurement | result | vs threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **reproducibility (all four fields, T=0)** | **1/10 = 10%** | **disqualifying** |
| reproducibility (genre alone) | 4/10 = 40% | below 60 |
| null case — genre on speech-only audio | 3/14 = 21.4% correct | far below 60 |
| speech detection (`vocals`) | 28.6% vs 28.6% — no signal | unusable |
| genre vs declared track, strict | 4/12 = 33.3% | below 60 |
| genre vs declared track, lenient | 8/12 = 66.7% [39.1, 86.2] | inside the 60–80 band |

**Only one measurement lands in the "say so" band — the lenient declared-track figure at 66.7%, CI [39.1, 86.2] — and it is moot.** Reproducibility is upstream of accuracy: if the same clip yields a different genre on re-ask 6 times in 10, an accuracy figure describes one draw from a distribution, not a property of the tool. **Widening n would sharpen a number that reproducibility has already invalidated.**

For completeness, since the brief asked: to separate 66.7% from an 80% ship bar at 95% confidence would need roughly **n ≈ 120–150 gradeable declared-track clips** (the current CI half-width is ±23 points at n=12; it needs to fall under ~7). At $0.55/1,000 that costs cents. **I do not recommend spending it.** The prerequisite is not more samples — it is a model that returns the same answer twice.

### If this is revisited

1. **Fix determinism first, or the rest is unmeasurable.** Pin `seed` if the provider honours it, and check whether the Flex tier is the source — the 24% transient malformed-output rate on first attempt points the same way. Re-run the 10-clip consistency test *before* spending anything on accuracy.
2. **Majority-of-3 is the only shippable shape** on this evidence. The Zimmer clip is exactly the case it fixes: 1 wrong, 4 right, and a 3-vote majority lands on the right answer. It triples cost to ~$1.65/1,000, which is still trivial — but it is a workaround for instability, not a fix.
3. **Do not ship `vocals` in any form.** It is uncorrelated with speech presence.
4. **`genre='none'` cannot be trusted as a music/no-music gate.** It fires on 21.4% of pure-speech audio.
