## Defect 1 — NO SPEECH. Fixed at the cause, proven on the three you rejected.

### Why they got through, and it was three separate faults

| clip | filed as | VAD, whole source | **VAD, the 20 s that ships** | subtitle track |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 @spooderbats | `music-only` | 0.087 | **0.195** | **64% of frames** |
| 02 @clipcapture.tv | `dialogue-over-music` | 0.857 | **0.849** | 45% of frames |
| 03 @lol_buzzmemes | `music-only` | 0.008 | 0.024 | 0% |

1. **The stored class was read, not the audio.** Clip 01 is filed `music-only` and carries a
   subtitle track on 64% of its frames. `clip_speech.SPEECH_PRESENT_MIN = 0.20` is not wrong
   — it was measured for a different question, *which treatment does this clip need*, where
   calling a 0.20 clip music DESTROYS dialogue. For refusal the asymmetry inverts: admitting
   a talking clip costs a rejected batch, refusing a quiet one costs supply.
2. **VAD ran on the whole source, not on what ships.** Sources run 37–59 s; the ceiling trims
   the tail to 20 s. Clip 01 measures **0.087 whole and 0.195 over the head** — 2.2×, and the
   difference is the entire decision.
3. **Nobody looked for the subtitles**, which is your own stated mechanism.
   `clip_text_signal` has separated a hook (high in frame) from a subtitle (low) since
   MEMEBOT-085 and only the hook half was ever read.

### The gate

`clippershq/no_speech.py`, two terms, and it runs **first** in the render path.

* **speech_frac ≥ 0.10 over the shipped window.** Your allowance survives: one word in 20 s
  is ~0.025 and two are ~0.05. It sits below clip 01's 0.195 (a true positive beyond argument
  — 64% of its frames carry captions) and above clip 03's 0.024. The cost is singing:
  BL-852 measured sung clips at median 0.085, so the sung tail is refused. That costs supply
  and cannot cause the defect.
* **a subtitle TRACK on ≥ 25% of sampled frames.** A *track*, not a band — one low band is a
  chyron or a shop sign; a band on frame after frame is somebody talking. Eleven frames, not
  `clip_text_signal`'s three, because a subtitle track is absent between lines.

**UNMEASURED IS REFUSED.** Every other gate here treats absent as unknown and lets it
through. Under a rule that says NONE, a clip nobody could listen to cannot be asserted silent.

### The library cost

**1,356 of the 1,860 clips carrying a measurement — 72.9% of them, 49.7% of the whole 2,731.**
The median stored `speech_frac` is 0.474: half your library is a talking clip. That is a
floor, not a ceiling — the stored figure is measured on the whole source and the gate measures

## What I got wrong along the way

* **The hashtag survey had the flow backwards** — it tried to retrieve hashtag media and got
  `not_found` 51 times before I checked the media types and found zero videos. 96 billed calls.
* **The first walk downloaded video-only DASH renditions**, so all 68 clips came back
  `speech_unmeasured` — which read exactly like "these pages are all dialogue" and actually
  meant "I never downloaded any sound". The gate was right to refuse; the reason is now split
  so `no_audio_stream` points at the caller instead of the clip.
* **I refused 17 clips for having an empty Instagram caption.** Since BL-1130 the label carries
  the *burned-in line*, so an empty caption says nothing about the clip. The caption now only
  refuses page-behaviour signals (promotional, hashtag block, film blurb).
* **Reserved is not billed.** The first survey reserved 112 calls and billed 10, because every
  retrieval failed on a malformed id before reaching the network. Reporting the reservation as
  spend would have overstated it 11×; the client's own `http_requests` is the receipt.
