## 6. THE THING HE SHOULD KNOW BEFORE HE JUDGES THESE PAGES

**Three of the columns he asked for are all but empty, and it is not the export's fault.**

| column | populated |
|---|---|

| **drawn text read from its posts** | **33 of 874 — 3.8%** |

`meme_finder.posts_for()` returns `api_index, code, caption, play_count, taken_at, media_type,
video_url, id, clips_metadata, audio_type` — **and no `top_text` at any point.** The shipped
judging path has no OCR step; BL-1230 said so plainly in its own commit. Neither harness
persisted `full_name` or `taken_at` either. The 33 drawn lines I do have come from earlier
rounds' OCR corpora (BL-1230: 25, BL-1177: 8) and the Aug-7 export.

**So 841 of 874 pages were judged on their CAPTIONS.** That has a consequence the sheet now
states in a column of its own, `Rules that could NOT run`:

`judge_page` decides which surface each rule reads. With `source != "top_text"`, **the
English-only rule votes `unknown` and the promotion rule is skipped entirely** — both on the
operator's explicit instruction that they read the drawn line only. Across all 1,885 pages the
two harvests judged, they fired **zero** times:

```
format_share  622    dialogue  277    creator_page  90    low_views   43
too_few_posts  42    no_text    28    thin_sample   18    news_media   6
foreign (language)  0        promo  0        <- could not fire, not "clean"
```

Eight rules did real work; the language and promotion rules abstained on every page. (`PROMO_DRAWN_ONLY
= True` and the `source == "top_text"` guard on `language_verdict` are both defaults, both
deliberate, both documented at their definitions.) `judge_page`'s own docstring says the drawn
line "is the whole difference between this and its two predecessors, both of which scored at or
below their base rate".

**I am not claiming these 848 pages are bad** — the eight rules that ran rejected 756 of 1,604,
and 90.8% of the file has a clean-audio post. I am saying the filter he is about to trust ran
with its language and promotion rules blind, and he should know that while he reads the sheet
rather than after.
