`format_share` was never the problem in isolation. It was being judged on a population the
other rules had mis-filtered — language passing Hindi pages, promo counting captions, film/TV

its threshold.

### The rule that fired zero times — a missing input, not a bad threshold

`too_few_posts` cannot fire unless `judge_page` is told `media_count`, which lives on the
profile payload alone. `meme_finder.run_funnel` passes it. **`parallel_judge.judge_one` called
`judge_fn(posts)` and passed nothing** — and that is the path BL-1184 shipped as the 2.95×
default. **Which of the seven rules a run enforced depended on which runner it happened to
use.** Fixed: the parallel path forwards `media_count`, `bio` and `category`. The rule now
fires **7 times with 0 false rejects**.

That widened the injectable `judge_fn` contract to `judge_fn(posts, **facts)`, so
`tests/test_parallel_judge.py`'s fixture was updated. Sniffing the signature at the call site
would have hidden a real contract change.

## 4. What his marks changed

| rule | change | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **language** | romanised-Hindi stopword set added; judged on the **drawn line only**; rejects on **any** confidently non-English line | **9 fires, 9 correct, 0 false rejects, 100% [70%, 100%]** |
| **promotion** | read from the **drawn line**, threshold 0.15 → **0.10** | 11 fires, 11 correct, **[74%, 100%]** |

| **views** | 2,000 → **1,000** | his number, row 8 |
| **news** | 0.30 → 0.60 (dominance) | *"a bit of news and but its ok"* |

**The language fix is the round's real find.** He has reported Indian pages after every
previous fix, and both previous diagnoses were wrong. The lines he objected to are:

> *"Hate me but iske jitna overrated hjJda ajtak nhi Dekha"* (#13)
> *"Kabhi business ki chul mach jati he too kabhi job ki kabhi padhai ki"* (#67)

That is Hindi, and it is **romanised** — correctly Latin script. `language_verdict` is a
script test followed by stopword sets for six European languages plus Indonesian, and **there
was no Hindi set in it at all.** Romanised Hindi scored `en` or `unknown` on the caption *and*
on the drawn text, so choosing between those surfaces could never have caught it. The gate was
not weak; it was blind. A previous round moved language to the caption on the reasoning that
"OCR garbles stylised type into Latin" — a true observation, aimed at the wrong defect.

**Two of my own guards caught me, and both were right.** Adding the `dialogue` rule to the
judge without adding it to the sheet's plain-language map turned `test_review_sheet.py` red —
that test exists so a new rule cannot silently vanish from the operator's view, which is the
whole premise of the sheet. And stamping `RULES_VERSION` onto the seen-set batch used `mf` in
`run()`, where it is only imported inside `judge_one`: a `NameError` on every batch write.
Both passed standalone before those edits and failed in the full suite afterwards, which is
the ordinary reason to run the whole thing rather than the files you touched.

**One correction to myself mid-round.** My first pass read *"its ok if there is a couple of
meme clips"* as "make promo a dominance test" and set it to 0.60. His labels said 0.10 — at
0.60 the rule fires on nobody and two pages he rejects walk through. Both readings are true
because they are about **different surfaces**: what he was defending was *caption* promo,
which this rule no longer reads at all. **His words set the surface; his labels set the
number.** Getting that backwards is the round's main mistake.

**The one rule below the bar, stated plainly.** `dialogue` at 0.10 fires on 2 pages and he
rejected both — but n=2 gives [34%, 100%], which does not clear 40.6%. It ships anyway, and
that is a judgement call, not a measurement: he named it explicitly and repeatedly, it has
zero observed false rejects, and its blast radius is 2% of pages. It is the first thing to
re-measure on the next batch of marks. **`film_tv` now fires zero times** on these 100 —
`narrates_action` never triggers — so it is a second dead rule, reported rather than papered
over.

## 7. The server that was dead twice

The cause was on this machine while the round ran: **a server from an earlier launch still
listening on 8777.** A plain bind raises `WinError 10048`, the `.bat` prints a traceback
nobody reads, and the page never appears. Three fixes, one per failure mode:

1. **Already up is a success.** If the thing on the port answers our own `/ping`, it opens the
   browser at it and returns. Re-running the `.bat` is now idempotent — verified against his
   live window: `already_serving(8777) → True`.
2. **Someone else on the port is not fatal** — it walks to the next free port and prints the
   URL it actually bound.
3. `allow_reuse_address` so a restart is not blocked by TIME_WAIT, and `daemon_threads` so a
   closed window does not leave a half-dead process holding the port for the next attempt.

## 8. What is still wrong, and whose it is

**26 rows still disagree, and 15 of them are one missing rule.** His single largest complaint —
C1, *"this is a person like a content creator this isnt meme page"* — has **no rule at all**.
The filter has no concept of "personal account vs repost page".

I measured the one free signal available: Instagram's own `category`. Treating creator-ish
categories as a rejection gives 67% precision [44%, 84%] — it clears the bar — **but it
rejects rows 21 and 65, the two pages he called "one of the best ones" and "automatic
approved".** Buying precision by cutting his favourite pages is the wrong trade, and *"Reel
creator"* and *"Digital creator"* have 43% and 60% want-rates in his own labels, so they are
not creator signals in his taste at all. **Not shipped.** This needs either a vision read (is
the same face in every clip?) or more labels; it is the highest-value thing left.
