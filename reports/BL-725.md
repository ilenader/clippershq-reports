## Two corrections to BL-717 before anything else

**1. `gemini-2.5-flash` is retired.** Every call returns:

> `HTTP 404 NOT_FOUND — "This model models/gemini-2.5-flash is no longer available to new users. Please update your code to use a newer model."`

Working audio-capable models on this key: **`gemini-3-flash-preview`** (used here, ~2 s/call) and `gemini-flash-latest`. `gemini-2.0-flash` returns 429 with `free_tier_requests, limit: 0` — no free tier at all.

**2. The "free ground truth does not exist" finding is wrong.** BL-717 concluded that declared-track clips carry no downloadable audio (0 of 80) because it checked `original_sound_info.progressive_download_url`. But **all 311 declared-title clips carry `video_versions[0].url`** in payloads already on disk, and ffmpeg demuxes the audio out of it at **$0**. All 16 fetched successfully.

That correction is what made this round's only usable stratum possible — a genre check against a *named, real song* rather than against a guess.

**On the 12 verifiable clips: 8 correct-or-defensible, 4 wrong = 66.7%.**

That sits **below the ~80% ship bar** and above the ~60% close bar — in the ambiguous band, on n=12, which is exactly the sample size BL-718 refused to draw conclusions from.

### The two failures worth naming

**Hans Zimmer's *Cornfield Chase* → `hip-hop-rap`.** This is the Interstellar score: piano and strings. Both `orchestral-cinematic` and `sad-piano-emotional` were in the enum and available. This is not a near-miss between adjacent genres.

**The same artist got two different answers.** Kowlys' *hallucinations.* → `lo-fi`; kowlys' *dreams.* → `orchestral-cinematic`. Not a consistency test as designed — different tracks — but from one producer working in one idiom, it is a poor sign for the stability the consistency test was meant to measure.

### The confound, stated plainly

The audio sent is **the clip, not the isolated track** — licensed music with the reel's dialogue and edit mixed over it. Two stratum-A clips came back `vocals=speech`, confirming dialogue dominates them. So some divergence is legitimately the clip differing from the track, not the model misreading. That confound **inflates** the error count and cannot be separated without the isolated track, which is not obtainable.

It does not rescue the Hans Zimmer case: no amount of voiceover turns an orchestral score into hip-hop.
