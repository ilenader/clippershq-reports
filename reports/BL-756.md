## Three corrections the implementation needs

**1. "All six present on 12/12" conflates *present* with *populated*.** On the gql endpoint all
six are present on 36/36 — but `save_count` is **non-null on only 66.7%** and `reshare_count` on
**88.9%**. A field that is present-and-null is exactly the trap `view_count` sets. The capture
must record absent-vs-zero honestly, or `save_count` will read as 0 on a third of clips and the
saves-per-view ratio will be silently wrong on those.

**2. The brief's `reshare_count (8/12)` = 66.7% is pessimistic.** Real rate is **88.9% on gql
and 98.6% on REST**. The caveat in item 5 was right to flag the small sample — but the error
runs the other way. Reshare is the *more* reliable of the two valuable fields, not the less.

**3. `user.follower_count` is NOT on "every clip" — it is gql-only.** 100% on `/gql/user/clips`,
**0% on `/v2/user/clips`**. Item 2's claim holds only while the walk stays on gql. If it ever
falls back to the REST pages — which BL-682 flagged as the deprecation-safe path — account size
disappears and any engagement-per-follower ratio silently loses its denominator. **That
dependency should be written into the schema comment, not just assumed.**

`view_count` as a decoy is confirmed at 3× the original sample: **present on 36/36 gql clips,
non-null on none of them**, while `play_count` carries the real number on all 36. It does not
appear in REST payloads at all — so the trap is specific to gql.
