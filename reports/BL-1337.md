## §1 — THE BUG, AND IT IS WORSE THAN "THE FETCH WASN'T RUNNING"

### What the code does

```python
clippershq/tiktok_finder.py:501    thin = n_seen < min_videos
clippershq/tiktok_finder.py:519    ok = not (thin or dead or foreign)
```

`dead` (below the view floor) and `foreign` (not English) are claims about **the page**. `thin`
is a claim about **our crawl** — it counts how many videos discovery happened to return. They
are OR-ed into one boolean, and `is_target: False` is read downstream as a reject.

### But the fetch IS already running — and that makes it worse, not better

`videos_from_handle` is called at `tiktok_finder.py:1344`, **before** the floor at `:1390`. So
the account's own videos *are* requested. The failure is the fall-through, and the comment
sitting directly above it says the opposite of what the code does:

> `:1353` *"A page a search cannot reach keeps what discovery gave it and is judged as THIN
> rather than rejected — unreachable is unknown, never a verdict."*

The code keeps the 1–2 discovery videos and sends them into `judge_author`, which sets
`thin=True` → `ok=False` → **reject**. **A search that comes back nearly empty is being treated
as evidence that the page is thin.**

### The fix, shipped and tested

`clippershq/tiktok_evidence.py` — three states, `TARGET / NOT_TARGET / UNJUDGED`, and the third
one cannot be collapsed by accident:

- `Verdict.__bool__` **raises**. A two-valued caller cannot silently turn UNJUDGED into false.
- `is_reject(verdict)` **raises** on UNJUDGED rather than guessing.
- A transport failure returns `looked=False` — a 502 is not evidence about a page, which is the
  distinction BL-1323 had to build after 58 × HTTP 502 were reported as "deleted or private".
- It has **no follower parameter**, because there is no safe value (below).

`tests/test_tiktok_evidence.py` — **13 tests, all green**, including the one that matters:
a careless caller trying to read UNJUDGED as a reject gets an exception.

**It is not yet wired into `tiktok_finder.py`.** Its only importers are its test and this
round's runner. I am saying that plainly rather than letting it join `triage.py` and
`presort.py` on the list of correct modules with no caller.
