## 5. THE STRUCTURAL FINDING — attack this next

> **Fixes whose output improved read fields filled 94–99.9%. Fixes whose output did NOT read
> fields filled 3.4–70.6%.** The loop is broken **upstream in the reading**, not in the fixing.
> Two correct, tested, landed watermark fixes moved the output by **exactly zero**, because the
> field they depend on is not populated often enough to fire. Every future round that lands a
> correct rule against a sparse field will get the same result, and will spend its time
> proving the rule rather than checking the fill. **Check the fill rate of every field a rule
> reads before writing the rule.** Where the fill is low the work is in the READER — the OCR,
> the vision call, the scrape — not in another rule.
