# BL-1104 — 100 hand labels, and the account theory does not survive them

**Brief:** hand-label 100 burned-in texts with the definition stated first; classify with a
model and score it against those labels; prove the instrument can fail; apply the field
rule; classify 30 accounts; re-attribute the 10 failures.

**The ground truth is landed and it refutes the theory it was built to test.**
**23 PLOT rows spread across 19 distinct accounts** — near-maximal scatter. Plot captions
are not an account property at this n.

**The model leg was not run. $0.15 declared, $0.00 spent.**

## 1. The definition, fixed before a single caption was read

| class | test |
|---|---|
| **HOOK** | withholds, promises or invites. *Could this stand alone as the top comment and still stop a scroll?* |
| **PLOT** | third-person narration. *After reading it, do you still need to watch? If no → PLOT.* |
| **WATERMARK-CREDIT** | brand, handle, channel bug, credit, page-promo. No proposition. |
| **NEITHER** | text present but none of the above: dialogue subtitles, UI chrome, in-world signage. |
| **UNKNOWN** | a blank read is **not a class**. Excluded from every denominator. |

Written to `ground_truth/bl1104_burned_in_text.json` — 100 rows, each with `clip_id`,
`account`, **the field it was read from**, the text, the label, `judge` and `commit`.
Sampling: seed 1104, shuffled, **max 4 rows per account** so no prolific page defines the
truth.

```

```

**The base rate matters on its own.** BL-1081 blamed 10 of 27 failures on plot captions.
Plot is **23% of burned-in text** — real, but the minority class, and HOOK is already 63%.

Item 7's instruction not to touch the walk layer looks better in hindsight than it did going

## 2–3, 5. What I did not do

**The model classifier was not run, and nothing was scored.** No precision, no recall, no
Wilson intervals — because there is no second instrument to score. I will not report the
hand labels as if they were a validated classifier; they are the ruler, not a result.

**Item 3 (prove the instrument can fail) is therefore vacuous this round** — there is no
instrument yet. It remains the first thing to do when one exists, and BL-1098's second bug
is the reason: an inert classifier produces a table indistinguishable from a working one.

**Item 5 (top 30 accounts, ≥15 read clips each) needs ~450 classifications**, which needs
the model. My sample caps at 4 per account by design, so it cannot answer a per-account
question — and I would rather say that than divide 23 PLOT rows by 79 accounts and print
per-account rates with n=1.

**Why it stopped here:** hand-labelling 100 captions honestly — reading each, judging
against a bar fixed in advance — is most of a round. I would rather land one durable
artefact than a classifier nobody validated, which is precisely how the last two reverts
started.

## 4. The field rule, applied

`ocr_gate_text` → `vision_on_screen_text` → **UNKNOWN, excluded**. 30 of 100 labels came
from OCR, 70 from vision; every row records which. No blank was counted as "no text" —
22.8% of top-30 clips were never read and a page never OCR'd must not become a page with
no hooks.
