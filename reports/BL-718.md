# BL-718: the fingerprinting pilot is underpowered before it starts — the target slice is 8.7%, not 24%

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Status:** **Step 1 complete. Steps 2–7 NOT RUN.** Two independent blockers, one procedural and one statistical.
**Spend:** **$0.** No AudD request was made. No account was created.

---

## Headline: the slice this pilot exists to measure is a third of its assumed size

The pilot was scoped on a ~23 / 24 / 53 split. Measured on 138 clips with faster-whisper VAD:

| group | n | measured | assumed |
|---|---|---|---|
| has-declared-title | 70 | **50.7%** | ~23% |
| **music-no-title (the target)** | **12** | **8.7%** | **~24%** |
| dialogue-only | 56 | 40.6% | ~53% |
| silent / unusable | 0 | 0.0% | — |

The sample was stratified 75 declared / 75 undeclared, so the *declared* share is an artefact of sampling and should be read against the corpus rate (311/605 = 51.4%, which it matches). The number that is **not** an artefact, and that decides everything:

**Among clips with no declared title, only 17.6% carry exposed music. 82.4% are dialogue.**
The pilot assumed 31.2% (24 of 24+53). The real figure is roughly half that.

---

## Blocker 1 (statistical): n=12 cannot return the verdict that was asked for

The brief sets a ship bar at ~60% and a close bar at ~40%. On 12 clips, Wilson 95% intervals:

| outcome | hit rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| 5/12 | 41.7% | **19% – 68%** |
| 7/12 | 58.3% | **32% – 81%** |
| 9/12 | 75.0% | **47% – 91%** |
| 12/12 | 100% | 76% – 100% |

**Both decision bars sit inside the interval for every plausible outcome short of a clean sweep.** A pilot run at this size returns a number that cannot distinguish "ship" from "close" — which is precisely the failure mode BL-711 objected to, arriving from the opposite direction: a verdict rendered on something other than a measurement.

Spending the 300-request free tier on 12 targets would consume the one free shot at this question and still not answer it.

**The fix is free.** 294 undeclared clips exist in the saved corpus. At the measured 17.6% that is **~52 target clips**, found for **20 minutes of CPU and $0**. At n=52 a 60% result carries a 95% CI of 46–72% — still wide, but it clears the 40% bar decisively, which is all the verdict requires.

## Blocker 2 (procedural): there is no API token, and getting one is not mine to do

Checked and confirmed absent: no `audd` key anywhere in `config.json`, no `AUDD_API_TOKEN` in the environment. The configured API blocks are `api`, `ig_api`, `tikhub_api`, `youtube_api`, `twitch_api` — none is AudD.

**What signup requires** (researched read-only, no account created):

- **300 free requests on signup, no credit card** — confirmed across the vendor site and independent 2026 free-tier surveys
- an account created through the Dashboard, which means **accepting the vendor's terms**
- the token is then passed as an `api_token` parameter on every request

The brief said explicitly: *do NOT create an account or accept terms in my name.* Creating an account **is** accepting terms, so steps 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 stop here. This is a decision for the account holder, not something to work around.

Two things worth knowing before that decision:

- **The URL path works as hoped.** AudD accepts a remote `url` parameter and fetches the file itself, so `progressive_download_url` can be sent directly with no upload and no local file handling.
- **The docs do not document a time-offset parameter.** Step 3's plan — aim a window at exposed music — may therefore need the audio trimmed locally and re-hosted, or may not be expressible at all through the `url` path. That should be confirmed before assuming one call per clip.

## What step 1 did establish, and it is worth keeping

**The aiming premise is sound.** On the 12 targets, the longest speech-free window is:

- median **21.7 s**, min 6.8 s, max 48.3 s
- **12/12 have a ≥5 s clean window**; 8/12 have ≥12 s

So there is genuinely exposed music to point a fingerprinter at, and step 3's "aim, don't guess" approach is supported by the data rather than hoped for. Per-clip window start/length is persisted in `scratch/bl715_split.json`.

**VAD costs 2.6× more than projected.** Measured **4.15 s/clip** (138 clips, 573 s, faster-whisper `tiny`, CPU int8) against BL-700's ~1.6 s. At 1,000 clips/day that is **69 minutes**, not 27. The feature still works; the budget line was optimistic.

**Audio fetch is cheap and reliable.** 150 requested, 138 downloaded in 63 s, **zero expired URLs** — the ~33 h window held comfortably.

---

## Recommendation

1. **Expand the target group to ~52 before spending anything.** 20 minutes, $0, and it converts an unanswerable pilot into an answerable one.
2. **Then decide on the AudD account.** If you create it and give me the token, the pilot runs as specified — positive control first, then targets, then hand-checks.
3. **Confirm the time-window question early**, because "one call per clip" versus "three" changes whether 300 free requests covers a 52-clip pilot plus a 15-clip control. At one call each that is 67 requests; at three it is 201.

Nothing about the fingerprinting question has been answered here, and this report does not claim otherwise. What has changed is that the pilot is now correctly sized — and it was about to be run at a size that could not have produced a verdict.
