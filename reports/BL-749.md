## Method, and the two ways it can be wrong

Every saved payload was flattened to leaf paths, each field's fill rate computed across the
records in that file, and "is it read" decided by grepping the leaf name across the owning
module **and its callers**.

**Weakness 1 — the grep is generous.** Any appearance of the name anywhere in those modules
counts as read. So the UNREAD list is a **lower bound**: everything on it is confidently
unread, but some genuinely-unread fields will have been absorbed by a coincidental name match.
Generic names (`id`, `name`, `url`, `count`, `type`…) are excluded from findings entirely
because they match everything.

**Weakness 2 — `False` is indistinguishable from missing.** My emptiness test treats `0`,
`""`, `[]` and `False` alike. That inflates the raw decoy counts: `is_verified: False` on a
small account is *real data*, not a decoy. Only fields verified by hand below are called
decoys in this report; the machine counts are reported as raw and should not be quoted.

## The finding: the gql clip walk receives a complete engagement panel and reads none of it

Verified by hand on the 12 deduped clips of `ig_gql_user_clips_clipcapture_tv_sorted.json`:

| field | present | non-zero | sample values | read by code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `play_count` | 12/12 | **12/12** | 21,579,646 · 3,975,983 | **yes** |
| **`like_count`** | 12/12 | **12/12** | 182,077 · 92,623 · 3 | **NO** |
| **`comment_count`** | 12/12 | **12/12** | 1,319 · 110 · 130 | **NO** |
| **`save_count`** | 12/12 | **12/12** | **34,291** · 7,824 · 2,901 | **NO** |
| **`reshare_count`** | 12/12 | 8/12 | **508,480** · 1,749 | **NO** |
| **`user.follower_count`** | 12/12 | **12/12** | 52,414 | **NO** |
| `view_count` | 12/12 | **0/12** | `None` throughout | **NO** — decoy |

This is the same shape as Spotify's unread `followers` (BL-744) and Twitch's unread
`socialMedias`, and it is the largest instance found: **six free fields on every clip, in a
request already being made.**

`save_count` and `reshare_count` are the interesting ones. A save is a stronger intent signal
than a like — 34,291 saves on a 21.6M-view clip is a real ratio — and `reshare_count` reaching
508,480 says the clip travelled off-platform. Neither is available anywhere else for free.
`user.follower_count` gives account size on every clip without a profile call.

**Also unread:** `media.save_count` (100% fill) in the REST clips path, and
`aweme_list[].author.follower_count` and `.author.following_count` (both 100% fill) in the
TikTok editors payload.

## Decoys — confirmed by hand

| field | funnel | behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **`view_count`** | IG gql clips | **present on 12/12, value `None` on 12/12**, while `play_count` carries 21.6M. Exactly the trap named in the brief, now confirmed on a real saved payload. |
| `aweme_list[].cha_list[].view_count` | TikTok editors | present, 0% non-empty |
| `paid_content_info.category` | TikTok editors | present, 0% non-empty |

**Not decoys, despite what the raw counts say:** `is_verified`, `is_private`,
`friendship_status.is_private` all read as `False` on these samples. That is a real value for a
small unverified account, and my test cannot tell `False` from absent. **Do not treat the 132
and 198 raw decoy counts as findings** — they are almost entirely legitimate booleans.

## Limits

- **Four of eight funnels were not swept**, because no payload is saved for them. That is the
  single biggest gap and is #6 in the ranking rather than a footnote.
- **Fill rates on n=1 files are meaningless.** `IG search`, `TikTok editors`, `IG profile`,
  `IG suggested` and `IG hashtag info` each have one record; "100% fill" there means "present
  once". Only the IG clip payloads (n=12–15) support real rates.
- **All IG clip samples come from the same three accounts** used throughout this project. A
  field that is `None` for `clipcapture.tv` may be populated elsewhere — `reshare_count` at
  8/12 is the visible example.
- **The read/unread test is a grep**, not call-graph analysis. It over-counts reads, so the
  unread list is a floor.
- **`False` vs missing is unresolved** by the method; only hand-checked decoys are reported.
- Nothing here was verified against a live payload — this is a sweep of what was already on
  disk, some of it days old.

**Nothing was implemented.**
