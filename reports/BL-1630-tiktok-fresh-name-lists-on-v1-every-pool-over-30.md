## 6. What I got wrong

- **I merged two of your lists in one pool.** Caption-mined names went inside their kind's pool and were walked first, so the pools were killed mostly on caption-mined tags. Run v1b tested the free-store names on their own for $0.389; they were worse.
- **`gen30 --more` rewrote the tag-origin map without the tags already walked**, so 420 early tags lost their origin. Rebuilt with the same logic and the ledger filter off (`tags_origin_all.json`); the origin table above uses the rebuilt map.
- **Calibration over-promised live precision:** 58.5% on old keeps, 47.6% on fresh names.
- **My expected range was wrong.** BL-1628 said "$17–25 while fresh names last"; the measured price was $34–200. Those tags had been pre-selected by v2 history, and that does not carry to fresh names.
