# BL-792 — Read out loud: `niche` is blank on 30 of 30, and where it IS populated it says "0% OFF". The template needs a different field, and it already exists at 100% fill

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Type:** Read-out-loud check, READ-ONLY · **Spend:** **$0.0000** — no paid call, no network, no write. Email local-parts are redacted; no address appears in this report at all.

## The headline

**The field the brief asked me to read out loud is empty.** `niche` is blank on all 30 sampled rows, on 98.4% of the top block, and on 97.4% of the export.

**And where it is populated, it is not a niche.** All 66 non-empty values in the export are scraped bio fragments:

```
unknown 12 · 100% 9 · 0% OFF 7 · 25% 5 · 50% 4 · 12% OFF 3 · 45% 2 · 55% 2 · 10% OFF 2
```

Master is the same: 1,614 of 58,038 filled (2.8%), top values `unknown` 553, `100%` 180, `0% OFF` 117. These are discount codes and sale text lifted out of bios. Merging one produces:

> Hi Areq — saw your **0% OFF** edits, the one at 8.1M views was great

**So the answer to "does niche read like `scenepack` or like `anime`" is neither — it reads like a coupon.** Drop it from the template.

**The replacement already exists and is at 100% fill.** `source_hashtag` in master carries the tag the editor was found under — `tt:hashtag:bridgerton`, `tt:hashtag:arcaneedit` — populated on **1,349 of 1,352** rows in the top block. It is not in the export.

## What reads badly

### 1. `niche` — unusable, 100% of the time

Blank on 30/30 sampled, 98.4% of the block. The 1.6% that is populated is coupon text. **Remove from the template.**

### 2. `display_name` — 51% needs work

| problem | rows | % of top block | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 660 | **49%** | `Areq`, `Jennifer`, `Shurk` |
| decorative symbols | 281 | 21% | `geo★`, `soulen®`, `Arabella⸆⸉`, `leah ೃ࿔*𖦹` |
| same as the handle | 250 | 18% | `hauntdfx`, `meowwyx`, `mel1nda` |
| no Latin letters | 139 | 10% | `絶望` |
| page name, not a person | 15 | 1% | `Melhores filmes e séries 🎬🍿` |
| stylised unicode | 7 | 1% | `𝕰𝖙𝖍𝖆𝖓`, `𝕸𝖆𝖙𝖙𝖎` |

"Hi 絶望" and "Hi 𝕸𝖆𝖙𝖙𝖎" are the visible failures. `𝕰𝖙𝖍𝖆𝖓` is just "Ethan" in mathematical-bold; a unicode NFKD fold recovers it. "Hi Melhores filmes e séries 🎬🍿" is greeting a page as though it were a person.

**Same-as-handle at 18% is harmless** — "Hi hauntdfx" is exactly what "Hi @handle" would have said, so it is no worse than today.

### 3. Fandom — 77% clean, 23% runs together

| shape | rows | % | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| single readable token | 1,037 | **77%** | `titanic`, `moana`, `frieren`, `outlander` |
| runs together (≥14 chars) | 312 | 23% | `AcrossTheSpiderVerse`, `500DaysOfSummer`, `TheTimeTravelersWife` |
| blank | 3 | 0% | — |

**A separate problem the sample exposes: 11 of the 30 fandoms are CHARACTER names, not titles** — `bella`, `daphne`, `holly`, `aria`, `sylvie`, `tara`, `lucy`, `mirabel`, `frances`, `jules`, `spartan`. "saw your holly edits" reads like a typo for a person's name. Editors do tag by character, so the data is not wrong; it is just the wrong noun for this sentence.

### 4. Numbers — both need formatting

`followers` and `median_views` are **bare integers** in the CSV: `2500000`, `8095740`. Every sentence above shows them rounded (`8.1M`, `570k`, `821`) because I formatted them in the render. **The CSV does not do this** — merged raw, the line reads "the one at 8095740 views was great". Rounding must happen in the merge tool or in a new column.

## Item 4 — the 23 low-token rows

**None of them are in the top 1,352 block.** All 23 sit further down the clipper list, and all 23 are missing the same three fields:

```
missing: niche, median_views, days_since_post     23 rows
```

So they carry `display_name` + `followers`, and **all 23 have a handle**. They have no reach figure and no recency, which is exactly what the personalised sentence is built on.

**Yes, give them a generic template** — but note the group is about to change size. Once `niche` is dropped from the token count (it is unusable for everyone), the token maths shifts for the whole file; these 23 are distinguished by lacking *reach*, not by lacking niche. The honest rule is **"no `median_views` → generic template"**, which is a property worth branching on rather than a token count.

## Recommended template

Using field names exactly as they appear in the CSV, plus one that needs adding:

```
Hi {display_name|handle} — saw your {fandom} edits,
the one at {median_views:rounded} views was great
```

| slot | source | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `{display_name\|handle}` | `display_name`, falling back to `handle` | NFKD-fold stylised unicode; strip trailing decorative symbols; if the result has no Latin letters, fall back to `handle` |
| `{fandom}` | **NEW COLUMN** — derive from `source_hashtag` | strip `tt:hashtag:` / `ig:hashtag:`, strip trailing `edit`/`edits` |
| `{median_views:rounded}` | `median_views` | ≥1M → `8.1M`; ≥1k → `570k`; else the integer |
| ~~`{niche}`~~ | — | **do not use** |

**`fandom` is not in the export.** It is the one field this recommendation needs that does not exist yet — `source_hashtag` is in master at 100% fill on the block and was never carried across. That is a one-line addition of the same kind as BL-790's merge columns.

**Fallback for the 23 (and anything without `median_views`):**

```
Hi {display_name|handle} — saw your {fandom} edits, really strong work
```

## Limits

- **30 rows sampled evenly across the 1,352-row block**, not randomly and not the whole block. The percentage tables are measured on all 1,352; the sentences are the sample.
- **"Reads badly" is my judgement.** The counts are mechanical (regex for decorative symbols, Latin letters, stylised-unicode ranges) but the thresholds are mine — `≥14 chars = runs together` is a rule of thumb, not a measurement of readability.
- **The character-vs-title split (11 of 30) was hand-classified** on the sample only. I did not classify all 1,349 fandoms.
- **Nothing was changed.** No code, no config, no export. The `fandom` column does not exist; every B sentence above was rendered by deriving it on the fly for this report.
- **No reply data exists**, so which template performs better is unmeasured and unmeasurable today — this is a readability check, not an effectiveness one.
