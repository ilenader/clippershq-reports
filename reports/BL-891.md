## 4. A puzzle from BL-884, resolved

BL-884 reported the suite count moving **87 → 88 → 89 → 91** and could not say why. `tests/` holds only **76** suites, and it held 76 throughout my sampling window.

The answer is in `run_all.py`'s own docstring: it used to call `os.listdir(HERE)`, flat and `tests/`-only, and now uses **`os.walk(ROOT)`** — so *any* `tests/` directory anywhere in the tree is enrolled, including `memebot/scraper/tests/`.

**76 in `tests/` + ~15 elsewhere = the 91.** So the growth BL-884 saw was other rounds adding suites under `memebot/`, not instability in `tests/` itself. That is a better-behaved explanation than it looked, and it also means **the suite count is not a reliable stillness signal on its own** — it was stable at 76 during four minutes in which the tree changed three times.
