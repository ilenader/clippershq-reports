## Item 5 — answered in full, no run needed

**Library size at ~5 kept clips per account:**

| pool | pages | clips |
|---|---|---|
| new pages from a 140 run | 140 | **700** |
| whole repost pool afterwards | 182 | **910** |

**Catalogue-walk cost.** Using the measured figures from BL-682/BL-690 — `/v2/user/clips`
returns **12 items per page** (the `count` parameter is ignored) at **$0.0006 per call**:

| catalogue size | calls/account | $/account | 140 accounts | 182 accounts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 posts | 9 | $0.0054 | **$0.76** | $0.98 |
| 200 posts | 17 | $0.0102 | **$1.43** | $1.86 |
| 300 posts | 25 | $0.0150 | **$2.10** | $2.73 |
| 500 posts | 42 | $0.0252 | **$3.53** | $4.59 |

So a full walk over the post-run pool is **roughly $1–$4.60** — an order of magnitude more than
the $0.13 discovery run that produces the pool, but still small in absolute terms.

Two caveats worth carrying:

- **A 6-month cutoff may not reduce this much.** BL-690 measured these pages at a ~58s median
  clip and high cadence; a page posting 3×/day reaches ~540 posts in six months, so for the
  busiest accounts six months *is* the full catalogue.
- **`/v1/user/clips/chunk` may serve ~50 per page**, which would divide every figure above by
  roughly four. BL-682 flagged that as **unverified** — one paid call settles it, and it is
  the single cheapest way to cut this cost.
