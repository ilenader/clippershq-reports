## 2. THE WATERMARK TERM — precise, and it misses two thirds

Scored against my 60 labels:

| | |
|---|---:|
| tp / fp / fn / tn | 8 / 1 / 14 / 37 |
| **Selected** | **9 of 60 — 15%** |
| **Precision** | **0.889** (95% Wilson **0.565–0.980**) |
| **Recall** | **0.364** (95% Wilson **0.197–0.570**) |
| Base rate (my labels say watermark) | **22 of 60 = 36.7%** |

**The rejection count is reported because precision without it is the base rate wearing a
threshold's clothes.** At 15% selected against a 36.7% base rate, this term is genuinely
selective — it is not flagging everything. But **it misses 14 of 22 watermarked clips.** The
field being fuller did not make the term complete; it made it *more precise on the fraction
it catches*. The 96.9% precision figure quoted for a bare brand word is not what this term
achieves on frames drawn from the newly-filled set.
