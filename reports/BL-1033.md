# BL-1033 — Eight rounds fixed the matcher. The field it reads was blank.

**READ-ONLY on product code. No paid calls.** A join across six audits, not a new measurement.

---

## The answer

The postable rate stopped moving because the fixes since MEMEBOT-098 all act on **selection**,
and **every selection fix that changed the output decides on a field that is populated. Every
one that did not decides on a field that is not.** There is no overlap:

| | deciding field filled on |
|---|---|
| selection fixes whose output **MOVED** | **94.1 % – 99.9 %** |
| selection fixes whose output **did not** | **3.4 % – 70.6 %** |

```
duration / too long        MOVED   media_duration_s        98.8%   SELECTION
song / audio attribution   MOVED   audio_class             94.1%   SELECTION
non-English caption        MOVED   caption                 99.9%   SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
third-party watermark      no      vision_on_screen_text   70.6%   SELECTION
content safety             no      vision_on_screen_text   70.6%   SELECTION
static / not-a-clip        no      motion                   3.4%   SELECTION
```

And 70.6% overstates it: BL-1027 measured `vision_on_screen_text` **wrong on 11 of the 11
clips it called empty**.

**The loop is not broken in the fixing. It is broken one step upstream, in the reading.** No
amount of matcher correctness could have moved the output, because the matcher was reading a
blank.

## 3. The comparison trap, obeyed

Rates are reported per audit with the judge named and **never averaged**:

| audit | n | postable | rate | why it is not comparable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| BL-950 | 25 | 0 | 0% | one-sided; no positives at all |
| MEMEBOT-074 | 30 | 3 | 10% | all 3 postable are **static graphics** — this judge accepted static as "the format" |
| MEMEBOT-086 | 30 | 5 | — | only **7 of 30 tabulated**; 5/7 is not a rate over 30 |
| MEMEBOT-089 | 21 | 0 | 0% | caption named the blocker |
| MEMEBOT-098 | 30 | 13 | 43% | |
| MEMEBOT-108 | 30 | 10 | 33% | |

**13/30 → 10/30 is one draw against another, not a decline** — MEMEBOT-108 said so itself.
And on the 61 tracked rows an **"always say no" rule scores 82.0%**, which is why no
cross-audit agreement number appears anywhere in this report.

**The sampling trap.** MEMEBOT-108's 30 came **18 from 4 accounts**. Across the 61 tracked
rows the top 4 accounts hold 19 (31%) of 30 distinct accounts. A defect rate measured on a
concentrated sample is a property of those accounts.

## 5. Three actions, and one stop

**ACTION 1 — finish `ocr_gate_text`. It is the input to three stuck fixes.**
Coverage is 64 of 2,728 (2.3%). The watermark term, the content-safety read and the
fake-tweet-card detector all depend on reading text off the frame, and all three are stuck at
exactly the point where that read fails. **Evidence:** two matcher fixes moved the output by
zero; BL-1027 measured the field wrong on 11 of 11. **Test the frame-scaling lead first** —
BL-1027 measured 120–136 s/frame at full resolution against BL-823's 1.4 s at 360×640; at the
measured rate a full pass is ~60 CPU-hours and at the scaled rate it is closer to one.

**ACTION 2 — take the render half seriously as its own problem.**
MEMEBOT-108 split its failures **exactly 10 selection / 10 render**, and this round shows the
two halves fail for different reasons: fill rate explains the selection half and is
**irrelevant** to the render half (caption is 99.9% filled and still ships sliced; renditions
99.4% and still pillarboxed). Every round since MEMEBOT-098 has worked the selection half.

**ACTION 3 — tabulate every defect class in every audit, even the ones at zero.**
Dead canvas went 10 → *(untabulated)* → 4 and got called "NEW". A class that stops being
counted cannot be seen to recur. This is free: it is a column in the next audit's table.

**STOP — stop fixing the watermark matcher.**
Two rounds, both correct, both landed, both verified with tests, and the output moved by
**zero** — *4 before, 4 after, twice.* A third pass cannot pay while 2 of the 4 marks in the
output are not in the field being matched. It becomes worth doing again the moment Action 1
lands, and not before.

**Also stop re-deriving the postable rate as a progress metric on n=30.** 13→10 consumed a
round's attention and was noise. With 8 positives in 61 rows, always-say-no scores 82%; the
defect *classes* move long before the rate does, and they are what this ledger tracks.

## What I got wrong

**My first hypothesis test reported "DOES NOT HOLD" and I nearly published that.** I pooled
selection and render defects, so `caption cut / sliced` — a render defect reading a 99.9%-
filled field — sat in the same table as the selection gates and destroyed the separation. The
fix was not a better statistic; it was noticing that MEMEBOT-108's own 10/10 split is a split
between **two different failure mechanisms**, and that pooling them was the error. Re-run on
selection fixes alone, the separation is clean and has no overlap.
