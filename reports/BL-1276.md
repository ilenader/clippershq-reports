### `taken_at` exists and is worthless

`response.sections[].layout_content.medias[].media.taken_at` is present on **27 of 27**. It is

dates the **post**, not the account's latest activity. TikTok's `create_time` works because its
discovery search returns an account's mixed history; Instagram's does not. **The field is
present, and it carries zero information.**

## 5. The arithmetic

| step | calls/page | want rate | **$ / 1,000 wanted** | clears $2.00? | basis |
|---|---:|---:|---:|:--:|---|
| today (double-buy live) | 1.443 | 19.0% | **$4.56** | no | measured |
| **+ double-buy WIRED (this round)** | 1.043 | 19.0% | **$3.30** | no | measured |
| + a free kill signal | 1.043 | 19.0% | $3.30 | no | **none exists** |
| + best-seed discovery | 1.043 | 30.8% | **$2.03** | **no** | **PROJECTED** |

**Two rates that are not the same number**, and conflating them is how this arithmetic goes
wrong: **40%** of walked pages pass the meme verdict — that is what makes the second call fire
— and **19%** are pages *he* wants, which is the denominator of "per 1,000 wanted".

**The wired fix is a measured 27.6% cut. Everything after it is projection.** The seeding
figure applies the advisory label's 1.62× best-seed lift to his true 19%, and lands at $2.03 —
**within 2% of the ceiling and not under it.** I will not claim it clears.

**The cheap experiment that would settle it:** discover ~40 pages from the three best seeds,
capture with Playwright (free), judge with sub-agents (free), and measure the true want rate.
Cost is about **$0.05**. If it comes back at 31.3% or better, Instagram clears; below that, it
does not. That is one round's work and it replaces the only projected number in the table.

**The subscription stays visible and separate:** judging is $0.00 in vendor dollars and
~66,439 tokens per page on his $200 plan. It is not free, and BL-1270 measured it as the
binding constraint on volume.

**Empty results are billed** — verified in BL-1274, offset=40 and offset=100 each returned 0
items and each moved the receipt counter by 1. Charging attempts is correct and this round
changes nothing about it.
