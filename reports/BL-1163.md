## 1. THE TRIM ARITHMETIC — three clocks, named, because they were confused

### The clocks

| clock | symbol | what lives on it |
|---|---|---|
| **SONG** | `t_song` | seconds into the mp3. `start_s`, `end_s`, `drop_at`, and ffmpeg's `atrim` |
| **WINDOW** | `u = t_song − start_s`, in `[0, hook_len]` | **`drop_offset_s`, and nothing else.** He marked it by ear against the window he was listening to |
| **CLIP** | `t_clip` | seconds into the finished video. `action_s`, ffmpeg's `adelay`. The clock he judges by eye |

### The rule, which is his instruction made arithmetic

```
lead  = min(drop_offset_s, action_s)     how much of the window's run-up the clip can carry
trim  = drop_offset_s − lead             the lead-in CUT off the front. >= 0, never negative
bed   = start_s + trim                   SONG second the bed opens. Always in [start_s, drop]
delay = action_s − lead                  CLIP second the bed's first sample plays

              the drop sounds at   delay + lead = (action − lead) + lead = action
```

which becomes `atrim=start=bed:end=end_s`, `aloop=loop=-1:size=(end_s−bed)*44100`,
`adelay=delay*1000`. Both his directions fall out of the same two lines:

- **`offset > action_s`** — the window's run-up is longer than the clip has room for, so
  `trim` seconds of it are **cut** and the bed opens mid-build. *His example inverted: drop
  7.16s in, action at 6.0s → 1.16s cut, delay 0.*
- **`offset ≤ action_s`** — the whole run-up fits, nothing is cut, and the bed is **delayed**
  into position. *His example: drop 3s in, action at 6s → nothing cut, delay 3.0s. The bed
  does NOT open at the drop, which is the "don't start the song at second 3" he warned about.*
- **`offset == 0`** — `trim == 0`. **Cut nothing**, exactly as he said.

### What was wrong, measured

The lead was capped with `min(drop_at, action_s)` where `drop_at = start_s + offset` — a
**SONG-clock** quantity capped against a **CLIP-clock** one. The thing it was trying to
bound is how much run-up the *marked window* can supply, which is `offset`, on the **WINDOW**
clock. The two agree only when `start_s == 0`, which is true of none of his 21 windows
(the closest, sng_0002 h2, starts at 0.36s).

`scratch/bl1163_arith.py`, over all **21 windows × the 19 action seconds** a 20.0s clip can
produce (399 cases):

| | old rule | BL-1163 |
|---|---|---|
| bed opens **before** the marked window | **337 of 399** | **0 of 399** |
| windows affected | **21 of 21** | 0 |
| worst intrusion | **19.00s of unmarked song** (sng_0002 h3, h6; sng_0003 h4; sng_0004 h4, h5) | 0.00s |
| longest `aloop` period | **42.82s** | 26.93s (= the longest marked window) |
| invariant violations (`delay + lead ≠ action`, or drop outside period 1) | — | **0** |

The intrusion is not cosmetic. `aloop`'s period is `end_s − ss`, so a bed that opened 19s
early **looped 19 seconds of music he never marked**, forever, under the clip. And on every

### The traps, and what was done about each

1. **`fit_window` WIDENS the marked hook** — it once read 11 of 20 renders as "missing their
   song" when the truth was 20 of 20. *Not applicable here by construction:* the reference is
   cut from the SONG FILE at `drop_at`. No window the pipeline computed is involved, so the
   widening cannot reach the ruler.
2. **A plan of 0.00 trivially matches a lag of 0.00.** *Every row records `trivial` (adelay
   == 0) and the headline is reported over the non-trivial subset separately.* The five rows
   where a wrong answer was possible are all exact; the other five are reported as what they
   are.
3. **40–250 Hz correlation returns ~0 on dialogue clips, the bed sitting 7–14 dB under
   speech.** *Sidestepped, and not by luck:* these render with `--treatment mute`, the
   shipping default for a music-only clip, so the source audio is dropped and the file's
   audio **is** the bed. The residual correlation of each source's own audio inside its
   render is recorded per row and runs **0.07–0.13** — i.e. the source is not in there.
4. **A refusal that returns 0.0.** `measured_drop_clip_s` is **None** below r = 0.30, never
   0.0, and `error_s` is None with it.

### A fifth trap this round found the hard way, twice

**(a) A fourth clock — the render's own timeline.** `white_frame` ships two random
anti-fingerprint knobs: `speed` 0.93–1.08 and `trim_start_sec` 0.1–1.5s. The first smoke test
produced a **9.567s render from a 12.072s source**, so the action second — measured on the
*source* — pointed at a different moment in the file, and the drop read 0.793s early.
`paste_batch` already neutralises both (`_neutralise_transform`); the harness now calls that
same function. **This is a live hazard for any future caller that does not:** a placement is
only valid on a timeline that has not been rescaled under it. Today `hook_chain` has exactly
one shipping caller (`paste_batch.py:1133`), and it does neutralise.

**(b) The ruler was wrong before the render was.** `adelay` writes the bed's leading silence
into the mp4 as a **timeline gap, not silent samples**. On render 05 the audio packets ran
`pts −0.023, 0.023,` then jumped straight to **2.4200** — the adelay, exactly. A plain
`ffmpeg -i file -f f32le -` decode emits only coded samples and *closes* that gap, so the bed
appeared to start at t=0 and the drop measured **2.373s early** on a file that was correct.
Decoding with `aresample=async=1:first_pts=0` — what a player does — put it at 0.000s. The
`head gap` column above is that gap read straight from packet timestamps, and it matches
every `adelay` to within 23 ms of container quantisation: **an independent confirmation of
all five delayed placements that uses no correlation at all.**

## 3. WHAT CANNOT BE PLACED — and it is not 0.0

`place_at_s` is **None** on every refusal and `chain_args()` returns **None**, so no chain is
written and no `adelay` is emitted. New in this round: `placement_trim_s`,
`placement_bed_start_s` and `placement_drop_offset_s` are **also None on a refusal**, because
`trim_s: 0.0` means the real thing "cut nothing" on a *successful* placement and a refusal
must not be able to borrow that reading.

| state | `place_at_s` | `trim_s` | what the record says |
|---|---|---|---|
| placed, nothing cut | `8.0` | `0.0` | a real zero — the drop was already at the window start |
| placed, lead-in cut | `6.0` | `1.16` | the note says *"the clip could not carry the full 7.16s run-up … 1.16s was CUT and the bed opens mid-build"* |
| **no drop in the window** | **`None`** | **`None`** | *"window h3 is flagged no_drop — it has no drop to put on the action. It can still play as a bed; it cannot be ALIGNED."* |
| **drop on the loop seam** | **`None`** | **`None`** | `offset == hook_len`: `aloop` wraps at that instant so the drop never sounds inside a period |
| action unmeasurable / out of range / no usable window | `None` | `None` | unchanged from BL-1146 |

**On "the clip is too short to carry the lead-in":** under the trim rule this is **not** a
refusal, and saying it were would be wrong. When the action arrives before the window's
run-up would have finished, the run-up is cut and the drop still lands exactly on the action
— that is what he asked for. What is lost is the approach, not the placement, so it is
recorded as a **truncation** (`trim_s > 0`, and the sentence in `placement_why` says how much
was cut and that the bed opens mid-build) rather than dressed up as a failure. Five of the
ten renders shipped in that state and all five are exact.

The genuinely unplaceable case that *this* round added is the **loop seam**: a drop marked at
the window's very end coincides with `aloop`'s wrap, so it never sounds inside period 1.
Previously an offset of 999 was clamped to the window end and then confidently placed —
`tests/test_hook_select.py` pinned that behaviour, and the guard now pins the refusal.

## 4. THE TWO WINDOWS WITH NO DROP — recommendation

His own notes contradict their own numbers:

```
sng_0002 h1   9.857–19.466s    drop_offset_s: 0.0    " just singing no beat drop "
sng_0002 h3  19.404–28.641s    drop_offset_s: 0.0    "no beat drop but this is the main
                                                      part of the song refrain "
```

`0.0` currently means **"the drop is at the window's start"**. For these two it means
**"there is no drop"**. Those are different facts and the placer cannot tell them apart, so
today it will confidently align a drop that does not exist onto the action — the same shape
as a refusal returning a valid-looking number.

**Yes, "no drop" deserves to be its own state.** Not a sentinel offset — `null` already means
"unmarked", and overloading it a third way is how this class of bug recurs. A separate
boolean:

```json
{ "hook_id": "h3", "start_s": 19.404, "end_s": 28.641,
  "drop_offset_s": null, "no_drop": true }
```

**Shipped in this round, ready for the flag:** `drop_placer.no_drop_window()` reads it, and

- a `no_drop` window is **set aside, not discarded** — `choose_hook` prefers any window that
  *has* a drop, so the song keeps working;
- if it is the only window, `plan()` refuses with its own sentence, no chain is written, and
  **edit.py falls back to its own input-side windowing — so the window still plays as a bed**.
  This matters: h3 is the song's refrain and is probably the best bed of the six. Losing it
  would be the wrong fix. Only the *alignment* is refused;
- it is read from the **field only, never from the note text**. A phrase matcher would have
  to separate *"no beat drop but this is the main part"* from *"a bit of a bit drop"*, and a
  wrong answer silently places a drop that is not there. `tests/test_drop_trim.py` pins that
  the note is never consulted.

**What is needed from him: one word on two windows.** I did not edit `scratch/songs.json` —
it is his live file, mtime 16:02 today, and he was in it while this round ran. The marking
page (`dashboard/dropmark_routes.py`) is where the checkbox belongs; that file is unclaimed
but is dashboard surface, and BL-1159 holds `dashboard/server.py` next to it, so it is left
for a round that can take the page as a whole.

Until the flag is set, **sng_0002 h1 and h3 will be aligned as though their drop were at the
window start.** That is stated here rather than silently mitigated, because guessing which of
his eight zeros are real is exactly the judgement no measurement in this repo can make.
