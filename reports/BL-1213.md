## THE FOUR THINGS THAT MATTER

1. **The Instagram cross-link — the number the brief called the most valuable — is small.**
   Not "most TikTok bios". **4.6% floor across 49,810 TikTok bios; 10.3% [4.8, 20.8] measured live
   on 58 discovered pages; ~19.9% absolute ceiling** if every unattributed bare `@handle` turns out
   to be Instagram. The route is real but it is a minor supplement, not the spine of the funnel.
2. **The two routes that produce most of Instagram's email yield do not exist on TikTok.**
   Instagram's business/contact field gave 40 hits and the contact button alone gave 31 addresses
   (51% of yield). TikTok's profile payload has **no email field of any kind**. The entire TikTok
   email surface is one string.

4. **`/v1/user/playlists/by/{username,secUid}` exist, work, and appear in zero code files and zero
   reports.** They were the last plausible route around "no per-account video listing". I probed
   them: they return real playlists on 25% of pages — but only `mixId` and `videoCount`, never the
   videos, and no endpoint expands a `mixId`. **The constraint stands, one hop later than believed.**

## SECTION 1 — What already exists for TikTok

Two rounds worked TikTok: **BL-1189** (study, built nothing) and **BL-1191** (built and ran once,

### `clippershq/tiktok_finder.py` — 460 lines

**It is a library with no entry point.** No `main()`, no `argparse`, no `__main__` block, no CLI.
Its only non-test caller in the tree is `scratch/bl1191_run.py`, which is not in the package, not

  **`frame_text` is never imported.** The string `/v1/media/video/download/by/id` appears **exactly

### frame_text and clip_speech — never run on live TikTok video

### The Instagram cross-link, measured four independent ways — it is not "most"

The brief called this possibly the most valuable figure in the audit. It is valuable, and it is
**much smaller than hoped**:

| cohort | n | names an Instagram | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| master TikTok bios, strict (URL or `ig:`/`insta` cue) | 49,810 | **4.57%** | — |
| stored discovered bios, strict | 125 | 4.8% | [2.2, 10.1] |
| **live read today, bio text strict** | 58 | **8.6%** | [3.7, 18.6] |

| upper band: any unattributed bare `@handle` | 49,810 | 19.86% | — |

The intervals all overlap. **The defensible reading is 5–10%, with a hard ceiling near 20%** if
every bare `@handle` in a TikTok bio turns out to be an Instagram (it will not — many are collab
credits and TikTok handles). Nothing in the repo labels which platform a bare `@handle` names, and
that 15.3% ambiguous band cannot be resolved from local data.

**I over-counted this myself first.** My initial live pass reported 30.0% by treating any `@handle`
as Instagram. Tightening the detector moved it to 8.6%. The loose number is the one worth
distrusting.

**Pricing it honestly:** 2,277 master TikTok rows name an IG handle; 1,912 of those carry no email.
Attempting all of them costs **$1.15** and, at Instagram's 16.9%, yields **≈323 addresses** —
about $0.0036 each. Cheap and worth doing. But it moves the funnel's overall email rate by roughly
**+0.8 to +1.7 points**, not by a multiple.

### `bioLink` — a live field the pipeline ignores, and a stale artifact corrected

`clippershq/tiktok_finder.profile_of()` reads **only** `users.<handle>.signature`. It never looks at
`bioLink`.

The audit initially concluded from `_probe_samples/user_by_username.json` that TikTok returns no
link field at all (0 occurrences of `bioLink`/`link`/`website`), corroborated by `link_in_bio` being
populated on only 0.24% of master TikTok rows.

**That sample is dated 2026-06-30 and has 39 fields without `bioLink`.** My live reads today find
`bioLink` present in the payload and **populated on 19 of 58 discovered pages (32.8%)**. The vendor
payload gained the field in the last six weeks; the stored evidence and the 0.24% master figure are
both stale, and the 0.24% is an artifact of code that never captured it.

This is the **largest single actionable gap** in the TikTok email surface: a third of accounts
expose a link that is already paid for and never read.

### NFKD folding is on the wrong path

`_plain()` (`tiktok_finder.py:94-110`) NFKD-folds styled unicode and is called by
**`advertises_promo()` only**. `bio_parser`, `enrich_links`, `ig_discovery`, `email_finder` and
`meme_finder` contain **zero** `unicodedata` references. `profile_of` passes the **raw** signature to
`extract_emails`.

So the famous 35.5% → 38.7% fix improved **promo detection**, not email extraction, and no email
path on either platform folds. Measured cost over stored data: **9 emails and 31 Instagram handles
currently missed**, plus 41 promo hits — 8.59% of TikTok bios contain styled-unicode blocks. Small
in absolute terms, and free to fix.

## SECTION 5 — The plan, the cost, and the honest ceiling

### The binding constraint is not money and not OCR — it is evidence per account

On Instagram the constraint was OCR at 10.9 s/page, with the vendor idle 98.8% of a 562-second run.
On TikTok the constraint is upstream of that: **you cannot list an account's videos, so you cannot
accumulate evidence about an account except by discovering more of its videos.** At 1.15
videos/author, the operator's three core rules — ~3,000 average views across recent videos,
text-on-screen, not-mainly-talking — are all being asked of a single video, or of none.

Every downstream number follows from that. It is why the filter passes 98.3%, why `min_videos` was
quietly dropped from 3 to 1, and why the send file's evidence columns are blank.

### Build order — each step gated on the one before

| # | build | cost | expected yield | why this order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Read `bioLink`** in `profile_of` and NFKD-fold before `extract_emails` | ~0 — a field on a call already paid for | **+32.8% of accounts gain a link route**; +9 emails / +31 IG handles on stored rows | free, and the largest single gap found |
| **1** | **Deepen discovery instead of widening it** — repeat queries and paginate the same hashtags/keywords so authors accumulate videos, and measure videos/author as the target metric | discovery is ~free: 6,681 handles for $0.15 on the Instagram side; TikTok search returns 20/call | raise 1.15 → 3+ videos/author, which is what makes every other rule computable | nothing else works until this does |
| **2** | **Re-enable `min_videos = 3`** and re-measure the funnel honestly | none | fewer, better authors | today's 10.9% is measured at min_videos=1 |
| **3** | **Wire the video-download → `frame_text` leg** (`/v1/media/video/download/by/id` is in the spec but in no code) and run OCR on the discovered videos | ~15 s/video, money-free | the first real text-on-screen measurement on TikTok | this is where OCR becomes the binding constraint, as on Instagram |
| **4** | **Mark 300–500 TikTok pages** with the existing windowed sheet, with TikTok-specific `RULE_TEXT` | operator hours | the only thing that has ever moved the Instagram filter | do it *after* step 3 so there is evidence to mark against |
| **5** | **Instagram cross-link hop** for the 1,912 IG-naming TikTok rows with no email | **$1.15** | **≈323 addresses** | cheap, additive, and independent of everything above |
| **6** | Derive the TikTok filter from the marks | — | — | not before step 4 |

**Do not build:** a sound-cluster path (absent), an account-first video listing (absent, verified
twice, playlists stop one hop short), a followers-based discovery path (0 emails from 48 profiles,
confirmed twice), or an email-finder subscription (refused three times, ~$700/mo, cannot address
free-provider individuals).

### The honest ceiling

Money is not the constraint. At $0.000600 per profile call, discovery effectively free, and an
email rate of **11–16%** from bio + `bioLink` + the IG hop:

| target | authors to profile | vendor cost | OCR hours at 15 s/video |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 emails | ~700–900 | ~$0.50 | ~3–4 h |
| 500 emails | ~3,500–4,500 | ~$2.50 | ~15–19 h |
| 1,000 emails | ~7,000–9,000 | ~$5.00 | ~29–38 h |

**So ~1,000 TikTok addresses costs about $5 of vendor spend and roughly 30–38 hours of OCR wall
clock** — and OCR barely parallelises (thread pools plateau at 1.77×; a process pool goes *backwards*
because ONNX already uses every core).

**The real ceiling is not the email rate — it is that none of those addresses are yet filtered to
pages that would actually post our sound.** Today the filter passes 98.3% of what it sees. Until
steps 1–4 are done, scaling this funnel scales an unfiltered list, and the 12 addresses already
delivered were selected by one rule: average views over a single video.

### What I would tell him in one line

The TikTok funnel is cheap to run and currently cannot judge a page. Fix evidence-per-account first
(steps 0–2, nearly free), then OCR, then marks. The Instagram cross-link is worth doing at $1.15 but
it is a supplement, not the answer he hoped it was.
