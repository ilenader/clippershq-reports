## 1. THE SUPPLY BUG — the cause is not what three rounds assumed

**`/v2/search` DOES NOT PAGINATE.** Measured live:

```
/v2/search          offset=0 -> 20 videos    offset=20 -> 0    offset=40 -> 0
/v1/hashtag/medias  cursor=0 -> 20 videos    cursor=20 -> 19   cursor=40 -> 20
```

The empty pages come back with **no error** — an empty list and an empty error string.
Nothing distinguishes "no more" from "there was never a second page".

Every plan to "run more queries so authors recur" has been aimed at an endpoint structurally
incapable of returning a second page. That is why videos-per-author has sat at 1.09–1.37
through three rounds of trying, and it is why this was deferred twice without being solved:
the lever being pulled was not connected to anything.

**Search gives exactly 20 videos per keyword, ever. Hashtags are the only surface with depth.**

### The supply curve, swept to exhaustion

12 hashtags, paginated until every one returned empty (depth 10):

| depth | videos | authors | videos/author | authors ≥2 | **authors ≥3** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 238 | 191 | 1.25 | 28 | 7 |
| 3 | 717 | 567 | 1.26 | 90 | 25 |
| 5 | 1,148 | 873 | 1.31 | 162 | 45 |
| 7 | 1,489 | 1,108 | 1.34 | 218 | 63 |
| **9 (exhausted)** | **1,684** | **1,228** | **1.37** | **266** | **71** |
| 10 | 1,684 | 1,228 | 1.37 | 266 | 71 |

**Before: 1.09 videos/author (one page per keyword, the old shape, re-measured on the same
queries rather than quoted). After: 1.37 at exhaustion.**

**The pool does not saturate.** Each page brings mostly new authors, so the ≥3 cohort grows
roughly linearly with the author count and stays at **5.8% (71 of 1,228)**. Depth buys breadth,
not recurrence.

**So supply is NOT fixed, and per your own instruction I did not implement his account rules.**
A view average, an English-only test and a dialogue judgement asked of 1.37 videos are the same
category error as asking them of 1.15. The honest position is that they are applicable to the
71-author ≥3 cohort and to nothing else, and a 71-author cohort is not a funnel.

**A billing fact nobody had recorded:** a hashtag call costs **two** requests (`hashtag_info`
then `hashtag_medias`). Measured: 104 calls, quota fell 208. Budgeting one per call understates
hashtag spend by exactly 2×.

### The OCR is still not wired, and here is the specific blocker

`frame_text.read_video(mp4, workdir, n=6)` needs a **local mp4**. The discovery payload carries
only `aweme_id` — no URL of any kind (`video keys: aweme_id, author_bio, created, desc,
duration, found_via, handle, sec_uid, views`). And `/v1/media/video/download/by/id` does not
return video bytes: it returns **992 bytes of `application/json`** containing request headers
and a URL to follow.

So wiring OCR needs a two-hop fetch (ask for the URL, then fetch it) plus a download method the
client does not have. I did not build it. **`frame_text` has still never run on a TikTok frame**,
and I am not going to claim otherwise.

**I did not run the cross-link leg this round.** The address extraction and the send file were
the deliverable, and I chose to bank 104 new addresses rather than spend the remaining round on

**Two shared-tree hazards worth naming:**

1. **`crossdedup.append_leads` failed five times with `PermissionError` on `os.replace`** before

## WHAT IS STILL BROKEN, AND WHOSE

1. **OCR has never run on a TikTok frame.** Needs a two-hop download (the endpoint returns a
   JSON URL-provider, not bytes) and a client method that does not exist. **Mine, not done.**
2. **Supply does not support his account rules** — 5.8% of authors reach 3 videos even at tag
   exhaustion. Not a bug to fix; a constraint to design around. The only levers left are more
   tags, or accepting that per-account judgement applies to a small qualifying cohort.
3. **The Instagram cross-link leg is costed and unrun** — 55 authors, ≈9 addresses, $0.033.
4. **`tiktok_send_list` has two dead checks** (inherited): `activity` is always `"UNKNOWN"`
   because `send_suppress.classify` never returns that key, and `assert_clean`'s `dead_mx`
   branch compares table-reader outputs (`dead/no_route/ok/unknown`) against `mx_probe` verdict
   names (`NXDOMAIN/NULL_MX/NO_RECORDS`), so it **can never fire**. Not mine, not fixed.
5. **The hashtag id-walk is a single ordered pass** over `("challengeInfo","challenge","data",
   "response")` and returns `"no hashtag id"` if the payload nests differently. It works today;
   it is one vendor change from silently zeroing discovery.

No lead data appears in this report: no address, and no handle paired with contact data.
