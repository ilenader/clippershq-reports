# BL-674 — what the 403 is, and whether a clipper is locked out of a campaign they earned on

## NO CLIPPER IS LOCKED OUT OF THEIR OWN EARNED HISTORY. Every one of somesome's 82 clippers can still see all 944 of their clips, all $5,401.55 of their earnings and their full payout history on that campaign, because none of those routes reads campaign status. What they lose is the campaign BRIEF page only, and the way they lose it is bad: the page renders a completely BLANK screen with no explanation. The 403 is deliberate and three months old; it was not introduced by BL-641, BL-651 or BL-671.

**2026-07-29 · AUDIT ONLY. READ ONLY on code, config, data and money. Nothing changed, nothing fixed, nothing built. No state-changing request was made; the 403 was traced through the auth check, not reproduced by calling it.**
**Base** origin/main `c8321777` (`post-merge-BL-668`) · **Branch** `checkpoint/BL-674` · **Worktree** `C:/b674` (short path, node_modules never junctioned)

## PART 1 — the 403, exactly

**`src/app/api/campaigns/[id]/route.ts:67-69`**, on the **GET** handler only:

```ts
// PAST campaigns are display-only. CLIPPER and ADMIN see them in the
// horizontal strip on /campaigns but can't open the detail page. Only
// OWNER can view/edit (to flip back to ACTIVE).
if (campaign.status === "PAST" && role !== "OWNER") {
  return NextResponse.json({ error: "This campaign has ended" }, { status: 403 });
}
```

**Who it refuses:** an **authenticated, non-banned, non-CLIENT** user whose role is **anything other than OWNER**, requesting a campaign whose `status` is `PAST`. That is every **CLIPPER** and every **ADMIN**, including an admin assigned to that very campaign.

**Who it does NOT refuse.** An unauthenticated request never reaches line 67; `requireNotBanned()` at `:27` returns **401** first. A banned user is stopped by the same guard. A **CLIENT** is refused earlier and for a different reason at `:45-47` (CLIENTs must use `/api/client/campaigns/[id]`). A DRAFT campaign returns **404** at `:60-62`, and an invisible test campaign returns **404** at `:55-57` so existence is not leaked. So the 403 is **narrow and specific: non-OWNER opening a PAST campaign's detail.**

**Why it exists.** The comment states the intent plainly: PAST is display-only, and only the OWNER may open it in order to flip it back to ACTIVE. This is a product decision, not an accident or a leak guard, and it is not protecting a sensitive field (the CLIPPER branch at `:72-87` would have stripped `ownerCpm`, `agencyFee`, `clientName`, `aiKnowledge` and, since BL-531/BL-535, `lockedOwnerShareDecimal` through `shapeCampaignForClipper` anyway).

**Traced, not called.** No request was issued. The refusal is read directly from the guard chain: `requireNotBanned` (`:27`) → CLIENT gate (`:45`) → `findUnique` (`:49`) → 404 gates (`:55`, `:60`) → **the PAST gate (`:67`)** → CLIPPER field-strip (`:72`) → full row (`:90`).

## PART 2 — a clipper CAN still reach their own history

**FIRST LINE, plainly: NO, a clipper is NOT locked out of a campaign they earned on.** Their clips, their earnings and their payouts on a PAST campaign are all still reachable through the normal clipper routes. The lockout is confined to the campaign brief page. Nobody's money history is hidden from them.

Verified live against somesome (`cmoaa70j2000z0ppfyaco13mj`, `status=PAST`, `isArchived=false`, `isTestCampaign=false`, budget $9,750):

| Surface | Route and predicate | PAST campaign | Live proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Their own clips | `/clips` → `/api/clips/mine` (`mine/route.ts:47-54`): `isDeleted:false` + `campaign:{ isArchived:false }`, **no status filter** | **VISIBLE** | 82 clippers, **944 clips** satisfy the exact predicate |
| Their own earnings | `/earnings` → `/api/earnings` (`earnings/route.ts:64`): `campaign:{ isTestCampaign:false }`, **no status filter** | **VISIBLE** | 82 clippers, **$5,401.55** satisfy the exact predicate |
| Campaign filter on the earnings page | `earnings/page.tsx:66-72` builds the dropdown from the clipper's OWN clips, then re-queries `?campaignIds=` | **somesome APPEARS in the dropdown** | follows from the 944 clips above |
| Their payout history | `/payouts` → `/api/payouts` (`payouts/route.ts:56`) selects `campaign.name`, **no status filter** | **VISIBLE** | **25 PAID totalling $5,139.36**, 16 REJECTED totalling $614.05 |
| Requesting a withdrawal | balance gate is status-blind (BL-641 measured zero campaign-status references in `api/earnings`, `api/payouts`, `balance.ts`) | **UNCHANGED** | consistent with BL-641 |
| The campaign card | `/campaigns` Completed strip, `/api/campaigns/past` | **VISIBLE**, $9,750 of $9,750 at 100% | BL-641 |

### The blank page is the real defect

`src/app/(app)/campaigns/[id]/page.tsx:132` fetches the detail with an unconditional `.then((r) => r.json())`, discarding `r.status`. The 403 body `{ error: "This campaign has ended" }` is stored as the campaign object. Then **`page.tsx:264`**:

```ts
if (!campaign || campaign.error || !campaign.id) return null;
```

**`return null` renders nothing at all.** A clipper who types the URL, uses browser history, or clicks an old campaign email link (`src/lib/email.ts:761` embeds `https://clipershq.com/campaigns/<id>` in every campaign alert) lands on an empty screen with no heading, no message and no route out. The guard was written for the **404** test-campaign case (its own comment says so, F-TESTUSER-FIX1) and swallows the 403 as a side effect. **The clipper is never told the campaign has ended, even though the API said exactly that in words.**

## PART 3 — the card was never clickable in this design, and none of the three rounds caused it

**The line:** `src/app/(app)/campaigns/CampaignsRedesign.tsx:198`

| Change | Commit | Date | Effect on the completed card |

| The non-clickable div | `5311c857` **BL-412** (clipper campaigns redesign) | **2026-07-12** | introduced `if (isPast) return <div>` |
| BL-641 | `d6a5ca46` | 2026-07-23 | moved somesome to PAST. **Did not touch this line** |

| BL-671 | `b281627f` (branch, not on main) | 2026-07-29 | ported the drag engine. **`MiniCard` byte-identical**, and its own report says so |

**So the loss predates all three by months.** BL-641 did not remove clickability; it moved a campaign **into** a state where the existing rule applied, and its own PART 3 disclosed this in advance: *"the card is no longer clickable (`api/campaigns/[id]/route.ts:67` returns 403 for PAST to non-OWNER). Both are existing, documented PAST behaviour."* That disclosure was accurate.

**Was it ever meant to open? Once, yes.** The **legacy** clipper layout still passes a real href to past cards at `src/app/(app)/campaigns/page.tsx:208` (`href={`/campaigns/${c.id}`}`), under a section comment that already reads *"Past — horizontal scroll, non-clickable"*. So the original design linked past cards into a route that 403s, and BL-412's redesign **deliberately stopped linking them** rather than send clippers into a dead end. That legacy branch is unreachable today: `page.tsx:22` hardcodes `const isTestUser = true` (BL-477 rolled the redesign out to all clippers), so `page.tsx:94` always returns `CampaignsRedesign`.

**Verdict for PART 3: the plain div is a correct accommodation of the 403, not a regression.** Given an API that refuses, a card that does not link is the right call. The open question is whether the API should refuse, not whether the card lost something.

## PART 4 — blast radius

**The GET 403 has exactly ONE clipper-facing caller.** A full sweep of `/api/campaigns/${...}` across `src` finds the GET consumed only at `src/app/(app)/campaigns/[id]/page.tsx:132`. Every other hit is a different verb or a different route (`PATCH` and `DELETE` from `/admin/campaigns`, plus `/restore`, `/destroy`, `/notify`, `/events`).

| Surface | Affected by the 403? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clipper earnings page `/earnings` | **NO** | never calls the detail route; `/api/earnings` has no campaign-status filter |
| Clipper payout history `/payouts` | **NO** | `/api/payouts` has no campaign-status filter |
| Clipper clips page `/clips` | **NO** | `/api/clips/mine` filters on `isArchived` only |
| Owner admin view `/admin/campaigns` | **NO** | it fetches `/api/campaigns?scope=manage&includePast=true` (`admin/campaigns/page.tsx:125`) and edits via **PATCH**, never via the GET. The OWNER is also exempt at `route.ts:67` |
| **ADMIN opening `/campaigns/[id]` for a PAST campaign** | **YES** | `role !== "OWNER"` catches ADMIN too, so an assigned admin gets the same blank page. Low impact (their real tools are on `/admin/campaigns`), but it is a refusal nobody asked for |
| **Old campaign emails** | **YES** | `src/lib/email.ts:761` puts `https://clipershq.com/campaigns/<id>` in every campaign alert. Once a campaign turns PAST, that link becomes the blank page for every clipper who kept the email |
| Favorites page `/favorites` | **NO 403, but a quiet loss** | `favorites/page.tsx:87` links every favorite to `/campaigns/[id]` unconditionally, however its source `/api/campaigns` is fetched **without** `includePast`, so `route.ts:114` drops PAST from the list and a favourited campaign silently **vanishes** from Favorites instead of dead-linking |
| Legacy campaigns layout | **NO** | `page.tsx:208` would dead-link, but the branch is unreachable (`isTestUser = true`) |
| Money, balances, withdrawals | **NO** | no money path reads campaign status for these; consistent with BL-641's measurements |

**Nothing in the blast radius touches a stored figure.** This is entirely a navigation and disclosure problem.

## PART 5 — verdict and fix spec

### ONE LINE

**No clipper is locked out of money they earned: their clips, earnings and payouts on a PAST campaign are fully visible on `/clips`, `/earnings` and `/payouts`; what is broken is that opening a completed campaign shows a silent BLANK PAGE instead of saying the campaign has ended.**

### Fix 1 (REQUIRED, independent of any policy debate) — stop rendering a blank page

The 403 is legitimate behaviour and can stay. Rendering **nothing** in response to it is not.

* **`src/app/(app)/campaigns/[id]/page.tsx:132`** — capture the status alongside the body instead of discarding it (`r.json().then(b => ({ status: r.status, body: b }))` or equivalent), so a 403 is distinguishable from the 404 the guard was written for.
* **`src/app/(app)/campaigns/[id]/page.tsx:264`** — on a 403, render an explicit finished-campaign panel: the campaign name if known, the words this campaign has finished, and links to `/clips` and `/earnings` where the clipper's own figures live. Keep `return null` for the genuine 404 case only.
* **Must be proven:** a PAST campaign renders the panel and never a blank screen; an unknown or test campaign still behaves exactly as today; an ACTIVE campaign's render is unchanged; no new call is added to any money route.
* **Rollback:** one file, `git revert <commit>`.

### Fix 2 (OWNER'S CALL) — should a completed card open at all?

Two coherent answers. Both are small.

* **Answer A, keep the 403 and give the card a destination that works.** Change **`CampaignsRedesign.tsx:198`** from a plain div to a link pointing at the clipper's own money for that campaign rather than at the brief. Zero authorisation change, zero API change. Honest caveat: **`earnings/page.tsx:36` does not read the URL** (`selectedCampaigns` starts empty and is set only by the in-page filter), so a deep link would need that state seeded from a query param, or the card links to plain `/earnings` and the clipper picks the campaign from the dropdown that already lists it.
* **Answer B, let a completed campaign open read-only.** Relax **`route.ts:67`** so a CLIPPER receives the same `shapeCampaignForClipper` response an ACTIVE campaign gives, and drop the early return at **`CampaignsRedesign.tsx:198`**. **Must be proven before this ships:** `shapeCampaignForClipper` still strips `ownerCpm`, `agencyFee`, `clientName`, `aiKnowledge` and `lockedOwnerShareDecimal`; join and leave controls are hidden for PAST so nobody can join a frozen campaign; no earnings figure on any surface changes; ADMIN is decided explicitly rather than by accident.
* **Rollback for either:** revert the one or two files. Neither touches a money file.

**Recommendation: ship Fix 1 now and Answer A with it.** Together they cost two small files, need no authorisation change, and turn a blank screen into a card that takes the clipper straight to the money they earned. Answer B is defensible but is a policy change the owner should make deliberately, and it is not needed to protect anyone's history, which is already intact.

**Also worth the owner's attention, flagged not fixed:** `src/lib/email.ts:761` will keep sending clippers to a URL that becomes a blank page the moment a campaign turns PAST, and Fix 1 alone repairs that too.
