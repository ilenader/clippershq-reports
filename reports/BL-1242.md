## 5. WHY IT STILL PLATEAUS BELOW 97%, WITH NUMBERS

**Presence of a caption band is not the discriminator. Density is, and density is not enough.**

| population | n | carry ANY low band | Wilson |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| pages he chipped "Too much dialogue" | 31 | 54.8% | [37.8, 70.8] |
| **pages he WANTS despite dialogue** | 7 | **71.4%** | [35.9, 91.8] |
| fresh controls he wants | 9 | 33.3% | [12.1, 64.6] |

His "90% of people who have dialogue will have text on the screen" measures **54.8%** on his own
chipped pages — and the pages he *wants* carry it more often. The two legs of this round agree on
why: the automated detector's *continuous* density (`two_band_frac`) does separate — CHIP 0.592 vs
WANT-DESPITE 0.307 vs CONTROL 0.242, AUC 0.819 — while a binary "any tile" flag does not.

**Where the method is structurally blind.** Of the three paired pages fixed, only `scenetoprint`
(9/15 tiles) was fixed by the two-band test; `roastoday` (**0 low bands in 25 tiles**) and
`meme_.graveyard` (2/25, under threshold) were caught by the *commentary* tell instead — a
third-person top line rather than a first-person setup. `crustytrap` (0/20) and `memezar` (0/30)
remain wrong and **carry no low band at all**. On a page whose dialogue is simply not captioned,
no frame-based test can see it.

**The ceiling his own marks allow.** Two careful raters agreed on only 55.6% of one category; his
own repeat-marks were inconsistent on 1 of 34 handles. A judge cannot exceed the consistency of
the labels it is scored against, and 58.1% [40.8, 73.6] on the dialogue rows already overlaps
that band.

**What would actually be needed to clear 97%** — none of it cheap, all of it stated rather than
implied:
1. **Operator re-marks the 34 chip pages saying which tile made him say it.** 13 of 31 currently
   have no visible dialogue evidence at all; without knowing what he saw, the remaining error is
   unaddressable.
2. **A density threshold fitted on more than 38 pages**, since the only result that cleared
   (`two_band_frac < 0.25`) was fitted on the sample it was tested on.
3. **Reconcile C3 and C10** — they already disagree. `hereticccc` has 3 of 12 tiles of podcast
   footage (C3 fires) and zero low bands (C10 does not). That exact shape needs his ruling.
4. Do **not** buy audio. Closed with numbers: the speech-vs-music energy read scored 29.2%
   [14.9, 49.2] against a 30.9% base; asking a model about a clip's audio measured 40%
   reproducibility and noticed a missing audio track 5% of the time. Movement now joins that list.

## 9. WHAT I GOT WRONG

**I under-powered iteration 4 for the thing the round was about.** A random 50-page draw contains
2–3 dialogue-chip rows. I ran it that way first and got a category number of n=2 — uninformative —
before switching iteration 5 to a targeted design. The targeted run should have been first; one
50-page iteration was spent learning that.

**The 3-of-12 threshold in the brief is looser than the evidence supports at the margin.** Two
judges independently flagged it: `ivorclips` (5/35) and `meme_.graveyard` (2/25) are both operator
chips that fall *under* the threshold and were only rejected via the commentary tell. The number
was written into the brief before it was measured.
