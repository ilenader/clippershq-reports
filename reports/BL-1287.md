## 4. The three-way split

Tuned on B, scored **once** on A. Shipped as `clippershq/triage.py`.

| bucket | B (tuned) | A (held out) |
|---|---|---|
| **DROP** — decided in code, never judged | 25 of 474 = **5.3%** | 1 of 100 = **1.0%** |
| ↳ **WANTED PAGES LOST** | **0** | **0** |
| **KEEP** — decided in code, never judged | **0** | **0** |
| **JUDGE** | 449 = 94.7% | 99 = 99.0% |
| want rate in the middle | 52.1% | 19.2% |

**The rules, and only two survived.**

- **`dead_page`** — the profile did not render, or the grid is empty. Sending it to the judge spends
  8,548 tokens to be told there is no picture. This is the only rule testable on A.
- **`tiny_supply` (`cap_n <= 5`)** — too few items in the discovery payload to *be* a meme page.
  25 fires, 25 rejects, **zero wanted killed**. Set at 5 although the zero-loss frontier on B is 6,
  because tuning to the edge of a zero-loss band on 474 rows is how "loses nothing" starts losing
  things. **⚠️ Unvalidated out of sample** — A carries no post-count field, and `tiles` is a
  viewport artefact (12 on 98 of 100 pages), not a proxy for supply. Said plainly.

**No follower floor, at any value.** I tried one and measured it out: on B a floor kills a wanted
page at **every** threshold that fires on more than one account — `<=50` kills 1, `<=100` kills 1,
`<=128` kills 1, `<=200` kills 4, `<=373` kills 5. That reproduces BL-1276's refusal from a second,
independent dataset. **The rule is deleted rather than re-tuned, because re-tuning is how it keeps
coming back.**

### Why CLEARLY GOOD is empty — a measurement, not an omission

| band | fires | precision on B | base 49.4% |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `cap_mean_len >= 1200` | 22 | **77.3%** | lift 1.56× |
| `cap_mean_len >= 500 AND cap_n == 12` | 166 | 72.9% | lift 1.48× |
| `followers >= 500000` | 59 | 69.5% | lift 1.41× |

**77.3% looks shippable and is not.** Dataset B's base want rate is **49.4%**; production is
**19.0%**. Carrying the lift across gives **~30% precision in production** — so roughly **seven in
ten pages in the bucket would be pages he does not want**, arriving on his send list having never
been looked at. *A precision measured on an enriched review sheet does not transfer.* The model's
AUC collapse (0.727 → 0.597) says the same thing from a second direction.

> ### The honest answer to his instruction
> He asked for the judge to see only the uncertain 5–20%. **On today's free data the confident
> buckets that lose nothing hold 5.3%, so the judge still sees ~95%.** The bands are set where the
> measured error is zero, exactly as instructed, and that is what zero costs. A 10% saving with no
> losses beats a 60% saving that costs him pages — but it does mean the middle is nearly everything.

## 5. Does the judge need the paid data? No.

An A/B on the **same 24 pages**, both arms on the free path, batched 8 per agent, **$0.00**.

| measure | k/n | rate | Wilson 95% |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **agreement between arms** | 22/24 | **91.7%** | [74.2, 97.7] |
| Arm 1 (grid only) accuracy | 20/24 | 83.3% | [64.1, 93.3] |
| Arm 2 (grid + paid fields) accuracy | 20/24 | 83.3% | [64.1, 93.3] |

**The arms do not differ by more than noise — they do not differ at all.** Confusion matrices are
*identical* (WANT 7/2, REJECT 2/13); McNemar discordant pairs **1 and 1**, perfectly balanced.

**`paid_field_used` on 3 of 24 pages (12.5% [4.3, 31.0])**: `bio` ×3 (one verdict change),
`category` ×1 (confidence only). **`followers`, `media_count`, `full_name`, `verified`: named ZERO
times**, despite `followers` being supplied on all 24.

The one field to name is **`bio`** — on `acog.hitbox`, Arm 1 wrote its own blind spot down before
failing (*"I could not settle from the grid alone whether the gameplay is self-recorded"*) and Arm 2,
holding the bio *"i just make siege animations"*, fired the creator rule and was right.

**Two findings that matter more than the A/B:**

1. **The shipped free judge never receives these fields at all.** No code passes bio/category/
   media_count/full_name/verified to it; the batch builders carry handle, image, `followers_text`,
   tiles. **So Arm 1 *is* the production input set and the 1-in-24 gap is an upper bound, not a live
   dependency.**
2. **The real dependency is in the other component.** The shipped *rule filter* `judge_page`
   (`meme_finder.py:1294`) uses all six for real. **Do not move the rule filter ahead of the profile
   buy on this evidence** — only the sub-agent judge.

> ⚠️ **This reorder saves MONEY, not PLAN.** The same number of pages still get judged, so
> subscription-token consumption is **unchanged**. Stated here because it has been mis-sold once.

**Free mitigation:** Playwright already loads the profile page. Extracting `bio` and `full_name` into
`META_JS` closes the one exposure at zero vendor cost — and would make every bio signal testable on
held-out data, which today it is not.

## 7. What I built

any held file. It reproduces the measured split exactly on both datasets (verified: B 25 DROP / 0
wanted lost; A 1 DROP / 0 wanted lost) and returns `JUDGE` for unknown or missing input, so a store
that stops populating a field degrades to judging rather than to silently dropping.
