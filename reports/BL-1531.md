## PART 0 — the real outage window, from the data

Hourly clip submissions, 6 to 9 September, UTC:

| day | hours with submissions | total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 Sept | 01:00 through 21:00, continuous | 191 |
| 7 Sept | 01:00 through 19:00, continuous | 165 |
| 8 Sept | **none until 20:21** | **0 for the first 20 hours** |

**The clip series alone cannot separate an outage from a quiet night**, because 6 September also has no
submissions between 22:00 and 00:00. So the window is derived from the **hourly tracking cron**, which runs
whether or not anybody is awake and is therefore the honest heartbeat:

| event | timestamp, cast `::text` |
| --- | --- |
| last cron write before the gap | **2026-09-07 19:40:30** |
| last clip submitted | 2026-09-07 19:37:38 |
| last signup | 2026-09-07 21:21:56 |
| last audit row | 2026-09-07 21:46:36 |
| **first cron write after the gap** | **2026-09-08 19:51:01** |
| db `now()` at measurement | 2026-09-08 20:12:15 |

**THE OUTAGE RAN FROM ABOUT 19:40 ON 7 SEPTEMBER TO 19:51 ON 8 SEPTEMBER: twenty four hours and eleven
minutes.** The staggered endings are the failure mode itself: heavy paths died first, light paths limped on.
Submitting a clip is a heavy path, so **posting died at 19:37, roughly two hours before the owner believes
the outage began**, while signups and audit rows continued until about 21:46.

### Where this differs from what the owner believes, and who it moves

- **"Roughly 21:30 on 7 September" is right for when the platform stopped serving anything, and wrong for
  when clippers stopped being able to post.** That was 19:37. **This ADDS anyone who normally posts in the
  20:00 to 21:30 UTC window**; on 6 September that window carried 7 clips.
- **"Until the compute upgrade on 8 September" understates it.** Recovery was at 19:51 UTC, so **the whole
  of 8 September was lost in every timezone from UTC westward to about UTC+5**, not merely the morning.
- **Zero clips were submitted on 8 September for the first twenty hours.** Against a normal day of roughly
  150 to 190, that is the outage stated in one number. It is no longer zero: **8 clips arrived from 3 real
  clippers between 20:21 and 20:37 while this round was being written.** The platform is back.

## PART 1 — what a streak actually does

### It drives money, so this was treated as a money change throughout

Tier table, `src/lib/earnings-calc.ts:35-42`: 3 days +1%, 7 days +2%, 14 days +3%, 30 days +5%, 60 days
+7%, 90 days +10%. The percent is summed with the level and PWA bonuses, capped at 25
(`earnings-calc.ts:164`), and applied to the capped base at `:184`.

### THE DECISIVE FACT: the streak bonus on a clip is FROZEN AT APPROVAL

`Clip.streakBonusPercentAtApproval` (`prisma/schema.prisma:993`), declared at `:989-992` as "NEVER
recomputed". The recalc path passes it as an override at `gamification.ts:967`
(`streakBonusPercentOverride: lockedStreakPct ?? 0`), and `earnings-calc.ts:148-149` takes the override
whenever it is non-null. **Because `?? 0` makes the override always non-null, the live-streak branch at
`earnings-calc.ts:151-153` is unreachable from a recalculation.**

**So extending a streak cannot raise a bonus already recorded.** That is the mirror of the defect BL-716 and
BL-827 each paid for, and it is closed by construction rather than by care.

Two further guards, either one sufficient on its own: `recalculateUnpaidEarnings` **skips every clip whose
campaign has any PAID payout** (`gamification.ts:911-914`), and it writes only through `writeClipEarnings`
(`:1084-1095`). **Measured: all 116 PAID payouts carry a `campaignId`, so the `.filter(Boolean)` at `:798`
has no live occupants.**

### What else a streak touches

Displays on six clipper and admin surfaces; gates the `STREAK_WARNING` bell and the growth engine's
`STREAK_SAVER_TONIGHT` nudge; caps the owner's manual restore via `longestStreak`
(`restore-streak/route.ts:40`). `lastActiveDate` additionally **gates bulk user deletion** at
`admin/reset-data/route.ts:108-109`, which is worth knowing and is untouched here.

### THE OWNER ASKED WHETHER THE STREAK IS THE ONLY THING THE OUTAGE BROKE. IT IS NOT

Checked rather than agreed. Seven things broke; these are the ones with live exposure:

1. **THE PWA BONUS, AND IT IS REAL MONEY.** `gamification.ts:621-645` strips `isPWAUser` after two days
   without opening the app, and that is a **+2% loss on future clips**. **MEASURED: 7 users were inside the
   two day window when the platform died and are outside it now purely because they could not open the app.**
   Their last opens run 2026-09-05 19:49 to 2026-09-06 18:00, all cast `::text`. **All 7 hold
   `currentStreak = 0`, so they are a completely different group from the 10 the streak remedy helps and
   this round does nothing for them.** Reported, not fixed: restoring a bonus is a second money decision and
   it is the owner's.
2. **Daily posting allowances expired unused**: the per-campaign daily cap
   (`clipper-submit-core.ts:382-389`) and the marketplace `dailySlotCount`
   (`marketplace/submissions/route.ts:445-477`). No carry-over exists. Everyone lost one day of allowance.
3. **Express payout SLAs burned**: `deadlineAt` is stamped once and never recomputed by design
   (`payouts/route.ts:688`, `payout-sla.ts:44-51`). Any express payout in flight lost its whole 24 hours.
4. **The streak warning never fired**: `growth/send-timing.ts:230-239` sends `STREAK_SAVER_TONIGHT` in the
   clipper's local evening only. Every clipper whose 7 September evening fell inside the outage lost their
   streak **with no warning at all**.
5. **False inactivity labels**: `growth/lifecycle-state.ts:82-97` reads activity from event rows that could
   not be written, so 8 September reads as inactive for everybody and pushes daily posters one day closer to
   STALLED.
6. **One marketplace strike-decay day cannot be made up** (`cron/marketplace/decay-strikes`), costing one
   strike-day. **0 active bans, so nobody is stuck.**
7. **The tracking cadence ladder slipped** for clips posted on 7 September, which spent their peak window
   untracked.

**Zero live exposure, measured rather than assumed:** the marketplace 24 hour submission TTL and
post-deadline strike sweeps could not have struck anybody, because `marketplace_submissions` holds **0
rows**.

### The exclusion rule, and what it excluded

**A clipper is helped only if the day before the outage day was itself a passing day.** The outage day
inherits the verdict of the day before it, so somebody who had already stopped posting inherits a failure
and gains nothing.

- **54 live clippers would see their stored streak fall, 119 streak days in total. The remedy returns 63 of

## PART 3 — the change, and why it could not be data only

### A data-only remedy is impossible here, and this is the proof

`updateStreak` **recomputes `currentStreak` from clip history on every call and overwrites whatever is
stored** (`gamification.ts:447`). Writing a number into that column is undone by the next dashboard load.
The one existing lever, `streakRestoredAt`, only makes the walk **skip for 36 hours**
(`gamification.ts:339-344`); after that the walk runs, finds 8 September empty, and breaks the streak
anyway. **A 36 hour hold would tell 10 clippers their streak was safe and then take it away**, which is
worse than doing nothing.

A day passes only if it holds an APPROVED clip or one with `streakDayLocked` (`gamification.ts:307`). The
affected clippers have **no clip at all** on 8 September, and fabricating one is not on the table.

**So the remedy is a code change. One file, `src/lib/gamification.ts`, nine lines of actual code.**

### What was written

```ts
const OUTAGE_FORGIVEN_LOCAL_DAYS: readonly string[] = ["2026-09-08"];
```

`evaluateDayByStr` makes that local day **inherit the verdict of the day before it**. Nothing else.

**Why inheritance and not a flat free day.** A flat pass would hand a one day streak to every dormant
account on the platform, because the walk starts at yesterday and would find it passed and stop there. That
is a fabricated streak for somebody who posted nothing, and it would flip `streakChanged` for roughly 1,500
clippers, **each one firing `recalculateUnpaidEarnings`**. Inheritance cannot do that: a dormant account's
7 September is failed, so its 8 September is failed too. **No allowlist and no hardcoded user ids.**

Two further edits, both consequences of the first:

- `getStreakDayStatuses` applies the same inheritance, **because it does its own bucketing and would
  otherwise paint 8 September as an empty square beside a streak number saying the run survived.** That is
  the two-derivations-of-one-rule trap, and the file's own comment says the clip filter is shared precisely
  to avoid it.
- **The grid now fetches one day wider than it paints.** Found by review, not by testing: when the outage
  day is the OLDEST square, the inherited day fell outside the query range and painted "empty" while the
  walk said "passed". It bites a real caller, `computeGamificationState` asking for a 3 day window, from 10
  September onward.

### What was deliberately NOT written

**No row was written to any real user, clip, campaign or payout.** No `currentStreak` was set by hand, no
`streakRestoredAt` stamped, no schema change, no migration, no data script. **There is no rollback SQL
because there is nothing to roll back**; the rollback is `git revert` of one commit, and the code path
reverts to the untouched original for every date.

### The rule itself is unmodified

The remedy is gated on **one literal date string**. Every other day, before and after, takes the original
path with the original bounds. **From 9 September the normal rule resumes untouched: a clipper who does not
post that day loses their streak as usual**, and the sandbox rehearsal proves exactly that by showing a
dormant control evaluate to 0.

### Idempotence

**Proven twice.** The walk is a pure function of clip history and the local date: run three times over 50
real clippers, identical every time. And in the sandbox, `updateStreak` was called three times in a row and
the stored streak read **8, 8, 8** with the money fingerprint identical after each.

createNotification(userId, "STREAK_MILESTONE", "Your streak is safe", "<the message below>", { link: "/progress" });

## PART 6 — evidence, and what changed

**Code changed: YES, and exactly one file.** `src/lib/gamification.ts`, nine lines of code inside seventy
six lines of explanation. **This needs a Railway REDEPLOY to take effect**, and until it does, every one of
the 10 clippers loses their streak the moment they open the app.

**A sandbox rehearsal was done**, using BL-842's tooling under `SANDBOX_ROUND=bl857`, and it is the
strongest evidence in this report because it proves the money question by writing rather than by reading.

**The 6 money files plus `tracking.ts` and `campaign-era.ts` are BYTE-IDENTICAL by blob OID.** No schema
change, no `prisma migrate`, no Apify actor run, the 11 BL-678 guards untouched, no payout created or
modified by this round, no broad UPDATE anywhere because there was no UPDATE at all.

### Three defects in this round's own instruments, found and corrected rather than published

1. **The affected list was wrong on the first attempt.** `AT TIME ZONE` was applied once instead of twice to
   a `timestamp WITHOUT time zone`, which shifted posts across day boundaries. Corrected before any
   conclusion rested on it.
2. **The proof script omitted the freeze rule** and reported 106 clippers losing 186 streak days. Most were
   frozen and could never lose anything. The real figures are 54 and 119.
3. **A check passed on an empty set.** The faithfulness control excluded every valid control, so it proved
   nothing while showing green. Replaced with a comparison against the product's own
   `getStreakDayStatuses`: **280 of 280 day verdicts agree**, and the control set is now asserted non-empty
   so the check can actually fail.

### Build honesty

`eslint v9.39.4` confirmed present, so the hooks gate is a real check. Clean tsc baseline on the untouched
worktree before any edit: exit 0, `grep -c "error TS"` = 0. Build written to a log with the exit code echoed
by hand and **never piped through `tail`**: **BUILD_EXIT=0**, 0 TS errors, hooks gate **0 errors and 10
warnings** against a ceiling of 11 with zero added.

### What remains unproven

1. **The fan-out on 9 September is unmeasured.** Every clipper whose streak changes triggers
   `recalculateUnpaidEarnings`, a pass over up to 5,000 clips. That happens with or without this remedy, and
   the load was not simulated.
2. **A clipper in a UTC-negative timezone has not been observed.** For them 8 September is still today, so
   the remedy has not yet entered their walk. The rehearsal ran in `Asia/Calcutta` where it is already the
   9th.
3. **`evaluateDay`, the `Date`-based twin at `gamification.ts:209`, does not carry the forgiveness.** It has
   **zero callers**, so nothing is wrong today, but a future caller would silently get the unforgiven answer.
