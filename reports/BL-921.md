## 2. The depth measurement, and it inverts the premise

Every depth figure in circulation was measured under a cap of 2, so a clip needing three pages was recorded as a **failure**, never as *depth 3*. I sampled 24 clips at `max_pages=12`, deliberately including the population that `select()` currently discards.

**VERIFIED:**

| group | n | found | actual pages (min/p50/max) | beyond the cap of 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| A — `estimated_pages ≤ 2` | 12 | **12 (100%)** | 1 / 1 / **1** | **0** |
| **B — `estimated_pages ≥ 3` (the censored population)** | 12 | **12 (100%)** | 1 / 1 / **2** | **0** |

Group B carried estimates of **3, 5, 5, 5, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9, 9, 10**. Every one was found in **one or two pages**.

> **`estimated_pages` was a true lower bound on 0 of 12** — and its own docstring claims *"true depth is worse, never better."* It is exactly backwards.

### Root cause, and it is one line of mismatch

```python
# clip_vision.estimated_pages -- counts clips with a NEWER posted_at
newer = sum(1 for p in newer_by_account.get(acct, ()) if p > posted)
return 1 + newer // page_size

# ig_client.user_clips_by_views -- what retrieve() actually walks
params = {"user_id": uid, "sort_by_views": "true"}
```

**The estimator models a CHRONOLOGICAL feed. `retrieve()` walks a VIEW-SORTED one.** Position by recency has no relationship to position by views, so the estimate is not a weak signal — it is a signal about a different ordering. And because `select()` ranks by `log10(play_count)`, the clips it picks sit near the *front* of a view-sorted feed by construction, which is also why BL-864 measured "1.00 pages per clip".

**162 clips (8.1% of the library) currently carry `estimated_pages > 2`** and are excluded as `unreachable_within_2_pages`. On this sample they are not unreachable at all.

### CORRECTION to my own framing in BL-910

The finishing run gave the sharper answer, and it contradicts the simple reading:

```
=== ESTIMATE vs OUTCOME ===
  est_pages 14  vs  actual billed 42   (1.56 vs 10.50 per clip)
FAILURES BY REASON (5):
   2  clip not found in 12 page(s) - it may have been deleted
   2  openrouter HTTP 502
   1  clip not found in 1 page(s)
```

**10.50 pages per clip — but not because clips are deep.** Because the residual pool is full of clips that are **gone from the feed entirely**, and a deleted clip burns the *whole* cap and yields nothing. Two of them cost 12 pages each.

So "depth" was conflating two populations that behave oppositely:

- **clips that exist** — found on page 1–2, essentially always;
- **clips that are deleted** — cost exactly `max_pages` and return nothing.

> **`DEFAULT_MAX_PAGES` is a WASTE CONTROL, not a reachability limit.** Raising it does not find more clips; it raises the bill on dead ones from $0.0012 to $0.0072 each. Lowering it does not lose reachable clips. Every cost model that treated the cap as a coverage/price trade-off — including MEMEBOT-004's 55/40/5 planning split — had the variable pointing the wrong way.

## 5. `lossy` has a reader

`scratch/memebot024_clusters.py` — the clustering consumer that actually reads `vision_beats` — now calls `is_lossy()` and reports the count before every sub-cluster table.

It matters most there, and the reason is specific: `vision_text()` concatenates scene + title + on-screen text + **every beat's `what`**. A truncated row contributes **less text for a reason that has nothing to do with its content**, so it is systematically less likely to match any phrase family and systematically more likely to land in `unsorted_comedy` — the bucket whose size MEMEBOT-024 read as evidence about the library.

Rows are **counted and reported, never dropped**: 0 carry the marker today, and a silent filter would be indistinguishable from one that worked. The output says so in as many words:

```
of which TRUNCATED responses (vision_parse_lossy): 0  [0 across all parked]
-> 0 rows carry the marker yet. It is written by three of four runners and
   cannot be backfilled, so this reads 0 because the FIELD is young, not
   because no row was truncated. Every count below is unadjusted.
```

**16 rows now carry the marker, 0 lossy.**
