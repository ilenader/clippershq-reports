## 2. The recency gate — the biggest number in the round

No video timestamp existed anywhere on disk: `probe34_raw.json`, `profiles.json` and
`rates.json` contain **zero** `create_time` fields between them, and a cover cannot be dated by
looking at it. So this was the round's only unavoidable paid leg — 159 calls, **$0.0954**.

| | pages | |
|---|---:|---|
| **FRESH** (within 62 days) | **83** | kept |
| **STALE** | **68** | **removed — 42.8%** |
| **UNREACHABLE** | 8 | **kept as UNKNOWN, never cut** |

The stale tail is worse than "a 2024 page": **median 295 days, 30 of the 68 older than a year,
the oldest 2,147 days — 5.9 years.**

**Unreachable is not stale.** The username variant of `/v2/search` reaches most pages, not all
(105 of 115 [84.7, 95.2]), so 8 pages have no date. They are kept. A gate that reads *"I could
not look"* as *"it is old"* would delete live pages for a vendor's indexing gap.

**What the gate costs: 31 of the 68 stale pages would have been judged WANT.** That is the
price of his rule, and it is his rule — a page that stopped posting ten months ago is not a
partner, however good the grid looks.

## 6. The honest ceiling — and it is a refusal

The funnel is four fractions, each measured at its own stage:

| stage | measured | rate |
|---|---|---:|
| survives the two-month gate | 91 of 159 | **57.2%** |
| judged WANT | 45 of 91 | **49.5%** |

| **end to end** | | **0.030 emails per discovered page** |

**To reach 1,000 emails a day** you need **9,434 wanted pages**, **19,078 judged**, and
**33,333 discovered** — every day.

| judging at | judged/day (24h) | wanted | **emails/day** | of target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10.18 pages/min (his figure) | 14,659 | 7,249 | **768** | 76.8% |
| 6.46 pages/min (free path, measured) | 9,302 | 4,600 | **488** | 48.8% |

**Not reachable. The honest number is 768 emails/day**, judging 24 hours a day at the faster
rate — and that assumes discovery can feed 33,333 pages a day, which has not been demonstrated.

**The binding constraint is JUDGING.** Capture runs at 123.22 pages/min against judging's
10.18 — **12× faster**, so capture is never the limit. The recency and email rates are
properties of the population, not of throughput: no filter raises them.

**What would have to change, in order of honesty:**

1. **Judge 13.3 pages/min sustained, 24h** — 1.3× the fastest measured rate. The free
   sub-agent path caps near 20 concurrent agents, so this means the paid API path with lanes,
   priced at $0.041/page — **about $780/day** to judge 19,078 pages.
2. **Or raise the email rate above 10.6%** — blocked by the platform, not by us.
3. **Or accept 768/day** and stop paying for the difference.
