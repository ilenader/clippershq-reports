## 3. Per wanted page — and the premise was wrong, in his favour

### The correction

BL-1276 published **1.043 calls per walked page**, BL-1280 (mine) restored the 1.062 billing
multiplier and published **$3.50**. Both omit a second billed call the shipped code makes.

**`run_funnel`'s actual order for one walked page** (HEAD line numbers — the working tree is mid-edit):

> discovery `:2851` **[BILLED, amortised]** → freshness gate `:2864` (free) → **`profile_and_contact` `:2890`
> [BILLED, unconditional]** → `should_buy_posts` `:2920` → **`posts_for` `:2929` [BILLED]** → `attach_speech`
> `:2941` (free) → `attach_top_text` `:2951` (free) → `judge_page` `:2955` (free rules) → branch `:2996`
> → contact written **only if it passed** `:3005-3010`

I verified the second call myself at HEAD, not in the mid-edit tree: `posts_for` does `on_call(1)` then
`client.user_medias(...)`, and `ig_client.py:587-593` documents `user_medias` as a *"**SEPARATE paid
call** from `user_by_username`"*. There is no response cache. `should_buy_posts` skips it on 6 of 50
pages (12%), so it fires on **~0.88 per page**.

**Why 1.043 was not wrong when it was written, and is wrong now.** It is a **two-term model of the
double-buy *delta***, not a per-page cost — 1.443 − 1.043 = 0.400 is exactly one contact call on the
40% that pass. **The posts call cancels in a delta**, so omitting it did not damage what BL-1276
measured. It has been read as a *level* ever since, by BL-1280 included.

**`docs/FACTS.md:145-151` independently composes 2.12 billed requests/page and flags itself
"this round's arithmetic, not a measurement any round published". Nobody reconciled the two. The
difference is exactly the posts call.**

**Why #1 is not the same kind of claim as the two projections this project has already buried.**
BL-1276's $2.03 and BL-1280's seed lever both rested on a **want-rate estimate**, and both died when
the rate was measured. **#1 rests on call arithmetic — which calls are made, not how often they
succeed.** At 0.233 calls/page, **$3.00 clears at any want rate above 4.9%**. It stops depending on the
lever that killed the last two rounds.

**What blocks it is plumbing, not economics.** `run_funnel` has **no capture stage and no sub-agent
judge stage** — neither exists in product code. Capture is `scratch/bl1257_capture.py`; the free judge
is a brief in a scratch harness; `parallel_judge.py` has zero production callers. **It is a build, not
a reorder.** The real blockers are non-monetary: the free judge needs a live session (no headless
runs), and judging runs 6.46 pages/min against capture's 123.22.

**And nothing is lost that is currently kept.** The funnel buys contact on 100% of walked pages and
**writes it only on the ~40% that pass** (`:2996` vs `:3005-3010`) — the reject-side email is already
fetched and dropped on the floor.
