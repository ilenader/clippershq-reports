## WHAT I GOT WRONG

Three, all caught before publication.

1. **I nearly published the raw ledger row count.** 700 rows for what is 355 records and 277
   finished videos. I had *written the rows-versus-records warning at the top of the page*
   and then walked into it one layer down, because `runs.jsonl` looks like one-row-one-render
   until you know the ledger keeps revisions. The renderer's own resolver was the fix.
2. **I reimplemented the suite discovery rule instead of importing it** and reported **331
   suites across 19 directories**. The real rule has a `SKIP_DIRS` containing `scratch`; the
   answer is **193 across 3**. Describing a rule by re-implementing it is how a document ends
   up describing something the code does not do — the exact failure this round exists to fix.
3. **My timings harness wrote a real lead's email address into a tracked file.** The send
   gate names the address in its refusal, and quoting that refusal is the whole point of the
   step. Redaction now happens *at the source* in the harness rather than being left to the
   secret scanner downstream — verified 0 addresses in the artefact.

My own citation guard also caught three unanchored `file:line` references in these very
documents, where line-wrapping had separated an anchor from its citation.
