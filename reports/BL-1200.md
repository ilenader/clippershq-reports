### The vocabulary could not be derived, and that is the finding

| | |

## 3. Creator: his own labels invert the hypothesis

**His hypothesis:** *"is every single video them describing? That could be a strong indication
it's a creator."* **His counter-example:** *"sometimes it's the same guy from the same movie
five times."*

Measured over the 161 marks:

| | pages he WANTS (50) | pages he REJECTS (111) |
|---|---:|---:|
| **a subject recurs** | **34%** | **5%** |
| film context present | **76%** | 41% |

**A recurring subject is a meme-page marker, seven times more common among the pages he wants.**
His counter-example is not the exception — at 76% film context it is the majority case.

Scored as reject rules, with the bar being the base rate's upper bound:

| candidate | TRAIN precision (bar 40.6) | HELD-OUT precision (bar 43.6) |
|---|---|---|
| creator OLD, bio+handle only | 33.8% [24.2, 44.9] | 38.0% [25.9, 51.8] |
| creator self-narration only | 30.5% [22.2, 40.4] | 31.1% [20.9, 43.6] |
| creator recurring-subject only | **24.4%** [16.6, 34.5] | **23.1%** [13.7, 36.1] |
| creator SIGNAL, no film exemption | 23.5% [15.6, 33.8] | 23.1% [13.7, 36.1] |
| creator PAIR **with** film exemption | 29.0% [20.8, 38.9] | 27.6% [17.8, 40.2] |

**Nothing clears. Every creator variant scores at or below the base rate it must beat**, and
the recurring-subject arm is the worst of them — it rejects 23 pages and loses 17 he wants.

**The film exemption works and is not enough.** It rescues **18 of the 28** pages the signal
fires on, **12 of which are pages he wants** (`scenevault01`, `supermovie.clips1928`,
`ceove_rse1`, `clips.culture_` …). It is doing real work; it is rescuing a signal that should
not have fired. A version of this exemption **already ships** — BL-1193 added `is_film_page`
from captions naming a title, with row 41 as its documented case.

**So `clippershq/creator_signals.py` ships as a measured NO.** The functions exist so the
finding is reproducible and so a later round does not re-derive the idea;
`tests/test_bl1200_creator.py` **fails by AST if anything imports it into `judge_page`**.

## 4. The face model: not worth buying, and here is the number

Face detection is currently impossible here — `cv2` 5.0.0 has no `CascadeClassifier` and
`FaceDetectorYN` needs a 340 KB YuNet ONNX that is not bundled.

**Adding it would make this rule worse, not better.** A face model measures "the same person
recurs" more reliably than proper nouns do — and **that quantity predicts the pages he WANTS**
(34% vs 5%). Detecting it better sharpens a signal that points the wrong way.

It would be worth revisiting only if he supplies labels for a different question — "is this a
*real* person rather than a character" — because the film exemption already separates those two
from captions at no cost. **That is his call; this is the number to make it on.**
