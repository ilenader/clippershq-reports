# BL-711 — song identification: line CLOSED by owner decision, with the decisive question still unmeasured

## The pilot did not run, and it is important to be exact about why. It was not blocked by the corpus, which is ready: **227 of 237 clips have a live audio URL right now**, `faster_whisper` is installed for the dialogue split, and **47 clips carry a declared title+artist** — real ground truth for catching a confident wrong answer without anyone listening. It was blocked at AudD signup, which is an Auth0 flow requiring an account under the owner's email, acceptance of AudD's terms in the owner's name, and email (possibly phone) verification I cannot complete. Presented with that, **the owner chose to close the line** rather than authorise the account. So: **the hit rate of audio fingerprinting under dialogue remains unmeasured — by this round, and by anyone.** This line is closed on cost/benefit, not on a failed measurement, and that distinction is the whole point of this document.

## PART 1 — the verdict

**CLOSE THE LINE.** The owner's decision, and the evidence supports it as a cost/benefit call:

* **BL-691 measured 0 of 108 clips carrying any track metadata at all.** On the repost/meme population this funnel targets, there is essentially no declared music to enrich.
* **Templated accounts already yield declared genre from captions at zero marginal cost.** Fingerprinting would only fill the untemplated remainder.
* **BL-699 priced the production path at roughly 7× the existing pipeline** (~$9-10 per 1,000 accounts versus $1.08-1.45).

Spending 7× to fill a remainder of a population where the base rate of usable music metadata is already near zero is a poor trade **even if fingerprinting works perfectly** — and nobody has established that it does under dialogue.

**What this round does NOT establish, and must not be cited as establishing:** that fingerprinting fails under dialogue. It was never tested. If this line is ever reopened, it reopens as an open question, not a settled negative. That is the opposite of how mood classification and drop detection were closed — both of those were measured first and closed on their numbers. This one is closed on economics with the technical question still open.

## PART 2 — what signing up actually requires (the checkpoint)

`https://dashboard.audd.io/` issues a 302 to `https://oauth.audd.io/3rd/auth0?...`, which 307s to `https://auth.audd.io/authorize`. It is an **Auth0-hosted OAuth flow**, and the authorize URL requests these scopes verbatim:

```
scope = openid email email_verified profile phone_number phone_number_verified
```

So a signup requires:

| requirement | detail |
|---|---|
| an account under the owner's email | created on a third-party service, in the owner's name |
| acceptance of AudD's Terms of Service | a binding agreement, accepted *as* the owner |
| email verification | `email_verified` is in the requested scope; needs inbox access |
| possibly phone verification | `phone_number` and `phone_number_verified` are requested scopes |
| **credit card** | **NOT requested at signup — this part of BL-699 holds** |

No payment details were entered anywhere, and no account was created. Creating a third-party account and accepting terms on someone's behalf is not a decision an agent should take unilaterally, and the brief itself asked for these requirements to be reported *before* proceeding. They were, and the answer was to close.

## PART 3 — two corrections to BL-699

**(a) MP4 on the standard endpoint is not documented.** BL-699 states AudD "accepts HTTP URLs and video files (MP4, MOV, etc.) directly — no demuxing needed", and the brief for this round repeated it. The documentation supports the URL half but **not** the video half: video is described only on the **enterprise** endpoint ("large audio and video files"). The standard `/recognize` endpoint documents a `url` parameter with "our server will download the file and identify the music", and does not mention MP4.

Not fatal — `original_sound_info.progressive_download_url` is an **audio-only** asset (`mimeType="audio/mp4"`, HE-AAC per BL-682) and is present on 153 clips — so the pilot would have sent that rather than the video URL. But anyone planning this on the assumption that "we can just send the reel" should check the endpoint tier first.

**(b) The "300 free requests" figure is marketing-site, not documentation.** The docs say only *"Sign up on the Dashboard to get one"* for a token and *"some number of requests is included with your monthly bill"* — no free-tier count, no card statement. The 300 figure appears on audd.io and in third-party summaries. It is probably right; it is not documented where an integration would look.

## PART 4 — the corpus was ready, and this is worth recording

If this reopens, none of the setup work needs redoing. Measured this round:

**URL liveness — stratified across every capture, 2 probes each, free HTTP range requests:**

| payload age | probes | result |
|---|---|---|
| 4.6 - 6.5 h (17 payloads) | 34 | **206 Partial Content — all live** |
| 316.6 h (1 payload) | 2 | **403 — expired** |

**34 of 36 probes live.** The only failures are the 13-day-old capture, which independently confirms BL-690's ~33-hour expiry window: everything captured today is fetchable, everything from two weeks ago is not. **227 of 237 clips currently have a live audio URL.**

**Audio sources available:** `original_sound_info.progressive_download_url` on 153 clips, `music_asset_info.progressive_download_url` on 47, `video_versions[].url` on all 237.

**The dialogue split is automatable.** `faster_whisper 1.2.0` and `whisper 20250625` are installed. BL-682 benchmarked ~1.6 s per 30 s clip on CPU with `no_speech_prob` available, which gives the dialogue / no-dialogue axis without anyone listening — the axis the whole question turns on.

**Ground truth for the correctness check exists.** 47 clips carry a declared `music_asset_info.title` + `display_artist` (audio_type mix across 237: `original_sounds` 154, `licensed_music` 47, no `clips_metadata` 36). A returned title can be checked against the platform's own declared title — so "a confident wrong answer is worse than a miss" is testable **by string comparison, not by ear.** That is the piece that made this pilot genuinely runnable where the mood benchmark (BL-689) was not.

## PART 5 — the exact recipe, if this is ever reopened

Everything below is prepared; only the token is missing.

1. Obtain an `api_token` from `dashboard.audd.io` — **the owner signs up, or supplies the token.** No agent should create the account.
2. Refresh the corpus if the captures are older than ~24 h (BL-690 expiry; re-probe first — it is free).
3. Split the clip set with `faster_whisper` on `no_speech_prob` into **dialogue present** / **dialogue absent**.
4. Send `progressive_download_url` (audio-only, not the MP4) to `/recognize` with `return=apple_music,spotify,musicbrainz`, ~100 clips of the 300 free.
5. Report hit rate in three buckets: **music under dialogue** / **music only** / **pure dialogue, no music**. Misses in the third bucket are correct behaviour and must not count as failures.
6. On hits, measure **ISRC** and **genre** fill rate — both are documented-optional with unknown coverage (`isrc` appears in the apple_music and spotify blocks; genre only via apple_music `genreNames`).
7. Verify 15 hits against the 47 declared titles. Any confident mismatch is disqualifying on its own.
8. Bar: **~50% under dialogue.** Below it, the line stays closed permanently.

**The unknown is still the unknown.** Speech puts transient harmonic peaks at 300 Hz-4 kHz, competing for exactly the spectral maxima a peak-based fingerprint is built from, and the documented white-noise robustness (-4 to -7 dB SNR) does not transfer to a structured interferer. No peer-reviewed measurement of fingerprinting under dialogue exists. This round did not produce one either.
