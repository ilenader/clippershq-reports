## 2. The editing-page question, ready to ask

**No new labels were collected, deliberately.** Two careful raters agree on only 55.6% of
this class; below ~90% more labels buy precision on a moving target.

The rule fires on **28** of his marked pages with a stored record. **He wants 11 of them
(39.3%) — and the base rate among those 28 is 39.3%.** A coin.

**What separates the two piles is the tell he gave us himself**, written into the
pre-registered definition before any page was opened: *"there isn't text above the video in
the middle."*

| | pages he WANTS | pages he REJECTS |
|---|---|---|
| **median `drawn_share`** | **0.83** | **0.17** |
| median followers | 5,131 | 19,410 |

Followers point the **wrong** way — the pages he rejects are bigger. What a page calls itself
does not separate them either: `bio_edit` fires on 7 wanted and 7 rejected.

Cutting at `drawn_share < 0.30` among those 28 rejects 11 pages and costs **zero** he wants —
**100.0% [74.1, 100.0]**.

**It does not ship and I did not ship it.** The bar is the lower bound clearing the base
rate's upper: **74.1 against 76.4 — it misses by 2.3 points**, and it misses on both halves
of a held-out split. At n=28 this is exactly the shape that has collapsed here before (one
signal 6-for-6 at 31 labels, then 53%).

**So it becomes the question instead.** `scratch/bl1223/EDITING_PAGE_QUESTION.md` is written
for him to read: the 11 he wants beside the 17 he does not, with his own words against each,
and one question —

> **When you reject an "editing page", is the thing you are actually rejecting that the video
> has no text written across it? And if a page DOES put text on its videos, do you stop
> caring whether it calls itself an editor?**

`drawn_share` is already computed on every page, so a "yes" costs nothing to ship and can be
verified on held-out data immediately.

Two measurements of one function disagree by 22 points, so they are answering different
questions on different populations — I am reporting mine with its population stated (610

**Recommendation: keep it, and do not spend another round here.** It measures better than the
brief claims, and its blast radius is seven pages either way.

### The caption pollution is one advertiser's paragraph, copied

Not truncation, not entities, not encoding. Scanning all 7,605 posts found **zero** HTML
entities, **zero** mojibake, **zero** escaped sequences, **zero** JSON fragments, **zero**
literal `null` strings and **zero** whitespace-only captions.

What it actually is: **one advertiser's caption paragraph written onto many different media
items across unrelated accounts.** The largest single smear is a *Vogue World: Hollywood*
branded-content paragraph found on **122 distinct media codes across 26 handles** — gym-meme,
football and anime pages whose burned-in lines have nothing to do with it.

| | |
|---|---|
| distinct smear paragraphs | 101 |
| **pages polluted** | **121 of 681 — 17.77% [15.08, 20.82]** |
| captions polluted | 672 of 7,344 — **9.15% [8.51, 9.83]** |
| pages **half or more** polluted | 55 |

The caption-level rate reproduces the brief's 10.0% [6.6, 14.9]; the **page**-level rate is
nearly double it.

**The cleaner is in `scratch/bl1223/defects.py` and is NOT wired into shipped code.** It
