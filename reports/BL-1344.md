## 1. PHASE 1 — where the money actually goes

**The first lead was wrong, and it was the cheap one to check.** TikTok pays the vision API
**nothing**. `tiktok_finder.py` and `tiktok_triage.py` make zero paid vision calls; judging on
this platform has been free the whole time. 100% of the $7.82 is LamaTok discovery. There was
no vision bill to remove.

The money goes to three things, in order of size.

### Root cause 1 — the funnel pays before it asks

`videos_from_handle` is one billed call per author, and it ran **above** both free gates.
On BL-1341's 359-row run, **200 of 359 paid calls (55.7%) were bought and then discarded** —
22 for staleness, 178 for thinness — by gates that need no network at all. The comment sitting
on those free cuts claimed they ran "before anything is bought for this page." That was true
of the profile call and false of the evidence call.

### Root cause 2 — a rate limit was being recorded as an empty account

`api_client.py:522` funnelled every unexpected status into `return None`.
`videos_from_handle` turns `None` into `(out, "")` — an empty list **with an empty error
string**. So a throttled request read as *"this account has 0 videos and nothing went wrong."*
The page was then cut as thin and cached `passed: false` in the seen-set **permanently**.

- 39 of these in BL-1341's log, **all 427**
- 15 traced to specific pages cut for "only N video(s) observed"
- the run reported **zero errors** while losing them

This is the same defect family as the blind cut: a failure to see is being written down as a
verdict. It is worse here because the seen-set makes it durable — those pages will never be
reconsidered.

### Root cause 3 — discovery runs on four hashtags

261 of 359 rows came from `memes` / `funnymemes` / `relatablememes` / `memepage`, and
**71.8% of authors came back already-seen**. `editing_hashtags` and `editing_searches` are
both **empty**, so the "edit + meme + quotes" diversity the goal assumes is not reachable by
the shipped config — the funnel is a meme-page finder today.

Want rate by source is **not** uniform, and this is the important part:

| seed | want rate | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| relatablememes | **76.5%** | [60.0, 87.6] |
| memes | **36.0%** | [24.1, 49.9] |

Non-overlapping. Which seed you page is worth more than any efficiency fix in this report.

## 2. PHASE 2 — the fixes

Both are in, both are pinned by `tests/test_bl1344_pay_last.py` (8 checks).

1. **The free recency gate now runs before the billed evidence call.** A stale page returns
   without buying anything and the row records `evidence_call_skipped`, so the saving is
   visible in the artefact rather than inferred.
2. **427 / 425 / 408 raise retryable** instead of returning `None`, and **every** unexpected
   status is now counted on the client, so a run can never again report zero errors while
   silently losing pages.

**What I deliberately did not do.** The evidence floor did **not** move up with the recency
gate. Pre-empting the purchase with the same broken video count is exactly the blind cut that
BL-1331/BL-1338 undid — it threw away 69 pages, 7 of them wanted. There is a regression test
whose only job is to fail if a future round moves `judge_author` or `min_videos` above the
paid call.

The fixes bought a 38% price cut on an unchanged population without moving the want rate.

**My seed-widening hypothesis did not pay off, and I am not going to dress that up.** I

*causes* a worse rate, only that it did not deliver the improvement I expected and cost more
in this instance. The combined $5.92 is the honest headline because it is what actually ran.

## 6. What would actually reach $2

Neither is a filter change. Both raise the number of wanted pages per fetched author, which
is the only term with enough room in it.

1. **Seed targeting.** `relatablememes` 76.5% [60.0, 87.6] vs `memes` 36.0% [24.1, 49.9],
   non-overlapping. Paging the good seed preferentially is a direct multiplier on the
   denominator and costs nothing to try. Note this cuts *against* naive widening — run 2 is
   the evidence that adding seeds indiscriminately is not the same move.
2. **Un-poison the seen-set.** `tiktok_pages_seen.json` holds **1,735 pages, of which 697
   (40.2%) are cached `passed: false`** — and an unknown share of those were written by the
   427 bug, i.e. pages that were never actually looked at. Every one is excluded from every
   future run, permanently. The bug is fixed going forward, but the existing cache still
   carries its damage and nothing re-examines it. **I have not measured what fraction of the
   697 is bad**, and that measurement is the obvious next round: it is free, it needs no
   vendor call, and it sizes a supply pool we already paid for.

Honest caveat on both: they raise the want rate, they do not touch the **$3.81/1,000**
discovery-plus-evidence floor. Reaching $2.00 needs the want rate roughly to double *and* that
floor to come down — and nothing I found this round brings the floor down.
