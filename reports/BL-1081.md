### The ceiling spike did NOT occur here — and the ceiling is binding hard

Checked at **strictly 20.0 s, with no tolerance**, because a limit that permits its own
violation is a suggestion. Nothing overran. But **11 of 40 (27.5%) land within 0.5 ms of
20.000 s** and the maximum is exactly 20.000 — the ceiling is not a safety margin, it is the
binding constraint on more than a quarter of the batch. BL-1080's in-flight
`CEILING_MARGIN_S` aims one frame under for exactly this reason; at HEAD the aim is
*exactly* 20.0 and this batch happened to round the safe way.

### Dialogue clips are reported separately, never pooled

The 40–250 Hz signed correlation runs against the **applied** window with a bounded lag and
every other store track as the null.

| population | n | carries the configured track |
|---|---|---|
| **music-only** | 28 | **26** |
| **dialogue-over-music** | 12 | not applicable — bed sits 7–14 dB under speech |

Pooling them would let a real music-path regression hide inside the dialogue clips' expected
zeros. The 2 music clips that do not carry their track are the same 2 described next.

### 4 of 40 are permanently unattributable — and all 4 share one cause

Every unjoinable record used a bed from **`scratch/bl691_audio/` — the LRU corpus, not the
store**. That is 10% here against the 18-of-100 previously measured. Two of the four are
`music-only`, so the attribution check has nothing to correlate against and they are the only
two structural FAILs: **the videos are fine, the provenance is gone.** One of them

## 3. All 40 watched — and the captions are the story

Judged by eye off contact sheets (three frames each for composition) and **full-width caption
strips**, because a 42 pt caption downscaled into a contact sheet is how a truncation
survives an audit. The bar: *would a 100k repost page post this unedited?*

**13 of 40 postable.** The 27 failures fall into four groups and one dominates:

| why it failed | n | example |
|---|---|---|
| **machine-output caption** | **10** | "Joey attempts to record a birthday message for Emma's future self" |
| **competitor watermark** | **7** | a full `CINEMAVAULT ✓` channel card over the top third |
| **no hook / dead canvas** | 9 | no caption at all; or a 16:9 strip floating in white |
| **foreign-language text** | 1 | Hindi caption over a mobile-game screen recording |

### The single biggest lever is the caption, and it is ours

Ten failures are our own template writing a **plot description** where a hook belongs —
"Achilles insults the King and refuses to acknowledge his authority", "The house defends its
owner against the intruders attacking her". Every one of the 13 that passed carries the
**source author's own** caption: "THE COLDEST SCENE IN BREAKING BAD", "I do NOT remember this
from Jackie Chan", "people who have grandparents are lucky because some are not".

That is a selection-and-generation finding, not a render one: the renderer is producing clean
files (40/40 structural) and then labelling a quarter of them with text no repost page would
write.

### The instruments the brief warned about, and what I did instead

- I read the sheets **by eye**; no detector scored anything. Four detectors in this project
  have been wrong in the same direction.
- `cinebyverse`'s caption **opens with a quote mark** — the source author's own, and *not*
  a defect. It failed on its watermark, which is a different thing.
- Burned-in subtitles ("ENDURE 10,000", "Help me line up the rooster") are the **source
  authors' own text** and were not counted as truncation. The one caption I did call
  truncated — "The defeated Gallic leader face…" — is in **our** band, in our serif face.
- My own caption strips were **wrong on the first pass**: I cropped y=0–430 assuming the
  caption sat at the top of the frame; content actually starts at y=408, so five strips came
  out unreadable. Measured, re-cropped, re-read.

## 6. What I got wrong

- **I wrote production state from a dry-run.** Proving that a filled row records something, I
  ran `outcome_loop import` against the **live** `memebot/runs.jsonl` and appended a
  synthetic outcome (`views=12345`, `post_url=…/PLANTED/`). That is the trap
  `decision_log.py` documents as having happened three times already in this repo. I removed
  the line byte-exactly (3,102,241 → 3,102,092 bytes, 899 lines all parsing, `outcome_loop
  status` back to 0 of 437) and re-ran the proof against a **copy**, with the live file's
  hash asserted unchanged either side. The evidence in §5 is from the copy.
- **My first render crashed the whole batch, on a bug I shipped last round.**
  `decision_log._record_threshold` writes `threshold=None` when one reason carries two
  different thresholds — which the duration gate does *by construction*, `MIN_DURATION_S`
  and `MAX_DURATION_S` under one normalised reason — and the next drop on that reason
  evaluated `abs(None - 7.626)`. BL-1056's own test stopped one clip short of it: two clips
  set the sentinel, none read it back. Fixed, with a third clip in the test.
- **I rendered into a temp directory, and it broke a green suite.** All 40 ledger records
  carried an `output` path outside the repo, so the dashboard player refused every one —
  `403, that record's output is outside the repo` — and `tests/test_dashboard_video.py`
  went red. Worse than the test: **the scratchpad was already swept once during this
  session**, so the files would have vanished and left 40 records pointing at nothing.
  Fixed by copying all 40 under `output/bl1081/` and **appending** `rev+1` records with the
  corrected path — an append, never a rewrite, because the ledger is resolved last-wins and
  rewriting 150 lines in place would destroy another round's concurrent appends. Verified
  by reading the last-wins view back: **40/40 inside the repo, 40/40 files present**,
  `test_dashboard_video` green (43 tests), and all 40 re-verified from the new paths.
- **My caption crop was wrong** and I only noticed because I looked at the output (§3).
- **I replaced BL-1023's batch in `outputs_for_operator/`** — its 8 `.mp4`s are gitignored
  and regenerable, and nothing tracked was lost, but its `README.md` now describes files that
  are not there. `START_HERE.md` says so at the top rather than leaving two documents
  disagreeing.
