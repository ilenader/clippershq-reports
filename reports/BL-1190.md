# BL-1190 — film_tv was not broken, the runner was; 50 fresh pages judged and ready to review

**Three rules were switched off in production and nothing said so.** `creator_page` — his
biggest complaint, shipped last round — **could not fire on the parallel runner at all**, and
neither could `too_few_posts`. The first 50-page run proved it: both fired **zero times across
50 pages**. That is the same failure that made `too_few_posts` fire zero times in 100 pages,
found twice more in one round, one level further up the chain each time.

Meanwhile `film_tv` firing zero times turned out **not to be a defect at all**.

**Interpreter: every number below was produced by `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, Python 3.11.9.**
Bare `python` here is 3.13.14 and lacks the packages; each script prints its interpreter at run
time. **Spend from the vendor receipt: 554 billed calls, $0.3324** against a $2.00 cap.

## 1. film_tv: the zero is correct, and the obvious repair is a regression

`_SPEAKER_RE` is `MULTILINE`-anchored, so a one-line transcript is invisible to it. The brief
asked whether `film_tv` has the same shape. **It does not.** Three causes were separated rather
than guessed between (`scratch/bl1190_filmtv.py`):

| | |
|---|---|
| **A. detector never matches** | `narrates_action` fires on **2 of 685** drawn lines |
| **B. share never reaches the bar** | no page reaches 0.60; max narration share is **0.11** |
| **C. wrong surface** | the catalogue vocabulary is in the **captions**, not the drawn lines |

But the decisive number is who his film/TV rows actually are. **Of the 7 rows whose prose
mentions film/TV, 5 are pages he WANTS:**

> *"we can do film tv shows and all of that from now on"* · *"it can be film and tv shows just
> cant have a big dialogi in them"* · *"this one was good we can do tv shows or cartons"*

**The mentions are approvals, not complaints.** BL-1188 grouped them into a "complaint" bucket
of 7 and called film/TV his third-largest complaint. **That grouping was wrong and this report
corrects it.** The 2 he does reject are rejected in his own words for other reasons —
`cinemaelevate` *"a bit to promotional"*, `moviezar` *"its alot of dialogs"* — and both are
**already rejected today** by `format_share`/`news_media`.

**So `film_tv` at zero is a fact about the corpus, not a dead rule.** Narration is rare;
the rule is correctly narrow.

### The repair that would have broken his favourite pages

The obvious fix is to switch `_FILM_TV_RE` (Genre:/Starring/IMDb/S1E2/trailer) back on. It has
had no call site since BL-1187. Measured before touching it:

| catalogue vocabulary in the captions | pages |
|---|---:|
| of the **31 he WANTS** | **13 (42%)** |
| of the 69 he rejects | 11 (16%) |

**It points the wrong way**, and `_sylora_0` — one of the two pages he called *"one of the
best"* — hits it on **10 of 10** captions. It stays retired, with the number written beside it
in the source, and `tests/test_bl1190_filmtv.py` fails if anything calls it again.

*One limitation named rather than hidden:* `moviezar`'s *"alot of dialogs"* refers to **spoken
audio**, which nothing in this filter reads. Its drawn lines are commentary, not dialogue.

## 4. The runner was the real dead rule — twice

`tests/test_rule_reachability.py` (BL-1188) proves each rule *can* fire and that every
`judge_page` call site passes every rule-bearing keyword. **It missed both of these:**

1. **`parallel_judge` forwarded 3 of 7 keywords.** The guard looks for calls named
   `judge_page`; this one goes through an injected `judge_fn` alias. Fixed, and the guard now
   follows the alias.
2. **The fetcher never wrote the profile facts onto the page dict.** `parallel_judge` reads
   `page["full_name"|"verified"|"followers"|"media_count"]` and nothing put them there.

Firing counts on the fresh 50, **before and after** the second fix:

```
before:  format_share 12  no_text 13  low_views 6  dialogue 2  promotional 1  not_english 1
         creator_page 0   too_few_posts 0        <-- the operator's biggest complaint, OFF
after:   format_share 12  no_text 13  low_views 7  dialogue 2  promotional 2  not_english 1
         creator_page 8   too_few_posts 10
```

The pass rate moved **42.0% → 34.0%** purely from switching two rules back on.
`tests/test_bl1190_filmtv.py` now asserts RULE 9 can fire *end-to-end through
`parallel_judge`*, which is the level the previous guard did not reach.

## 7. The sheet

`output/REVIEW_50.html` — 50 numbered rows, one plain sentence naming the deciding rule, every
rule's own result with the number it tested, average views with each post's count, the OCR'd
drawn line beside the caption **labelled NOT judged**, thumbnails (374 frames in
`review_50_thumbs`), clickable profile links, append-only marks.

```
.venv\Scripts\python.exe clippershq\review_sheet.py --serve ^
   --pages scratch\bl1190_pages.jsonl --summary scratch\bl1190_judge.json ^
   --thumbs review_50_thumbs --out output\REVIEW_50.html
```

**The mark box now names the axis on every row.** A round nearly built an entire analysis on
the wrong one: `mark` is a verdict on **the filter**, not the page. Under the wrong reading the
marks say "64 good pages", which reconciles with nothing; under the right one they produce both
published figures. Each row now reads *"#12 — the filter **REJECTED it**. Was that the right
call?"* with *"(you are grading the FILTER, not the page)"* beneath, and the options are
"GOOD — right call" / "BAD — wrong call".

**Server reliability** (BL-1187's hardening, verified live this round): `/ping` reuse — a second
`serve()` on a busy port **opens the existing instance instead of dying**; port-walk on a
foreign occupant; `allow_reuse_address` so a restart is not blocked by TIME_WAIT;
`daemon_threads` so a closed window leaves nothing holding the port. A live POST appended a
mark and the file grew by exactly one line.

> **One thing I broke and repaired.** That live test appended a synthetic row
> (`__bl1190_selftest`) to `ground_truth/review_marks.jsonl` — his real labels. I removed it and
> re-verified the file at **103 lines / 100 distinct rows / GOOD 65, BAD 38**, unchanged from
> the start of the round. Append-only is the file's rule; a fake mark in the ground truth is
> worse than breaking it once, deliberately, and saying so.
