**The bio signal does not survive.** Six for six became ten of nineteen, and its lower bound

**Drawn text on every post does survive**: 1.86× lift, lower bound 44% clear of 38.7%.

given a veto over a rule whose rejections are more accurate than 57%. *"Clears the bar as a

first is true. It ships as evidence on the verdict — for ranking, for sorting his review
sheet, and for gating once there are enough labels to place it *beside* `format_share`

## 5. The ceiling — 97% is not reachable, and here is what is

**At n=149, agreement is 71.1% and the 95% interval is ±7.2 points.** That interval is the
whole point: quoting 71% as if it were exact is how three earlier filters were oversold.

**Two separate limits, and the sample size is only the second one.**

**First — 97% cannot be *demonstrated* at this sample size even if it were true.** At n=149,
a 97% result reads [93.3%, 99.0%] and a 90% result reads [84.1%, 93.8%]; **the intervals
overlap**, so 149 labels cannot tell 97% from 90%. Separating them needs **n ≈ 300**. Halving
today's ±7.2 points needs n ≈ 600; getting to ±2 points needs n ≈ 2,000.

**Second, and the binding one — the signals available cannot reach 97%.** The evidence is in
this round:

- The strongest positive signal in the data is **57% precise**. The best single rule
  (`creator_page`) contributes 67 of 149.
- Out of sample, agreement is **63.3%**, not 75%.
- Two of his own criteria are **not separable by anything currently measured**: dialogue
  (identical speech shares, opposite verdicts) and row 48's layout (invisible to OCR text).

**What would actually be needed**, in order of expected return:

1. **A speech-versus-music read**, not speech presence — `clip_speech.classify()`'s audio
   class, to settle the case his own rule cannot.
2. **A layout read** on the drawn text block — position, background, whether it sits above
   the video. This is the only route to row 48's format and probably to "is there text above
   the video in the middle", which is his own definition of a meme page.
3. **A face-recurrence read** — his creator signal is *"the same face across videos"*, and
   `creator_page` currently infers that from the bio, which is why 4 pages he wants still trip
   it.
4. **~300 more labels** — to make 90% and 97% distinguishable at all, and to let the positive
   signal be placed beside `format_share` instead of over it.

**A realistic target on the current signals is the high 70s to low 80s.** I would not promise
97% on anything measured here, and saying otherwise would be the fourth time.
