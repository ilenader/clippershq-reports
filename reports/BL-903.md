**`tools/claim.py` was itself mid-edit** — BL-889 declared exactly that file six minutes into my wait. The tool crashed, printed nothing, and my `grep -c` counted zero matches as *zero rounds*. Eleven claim files were sitting on disk at that moment.

Had I taken it at face value, I would have started a 40-minute measurement on a tree with eleven rounds running — the precise failure BL-891 exists to prevent, arriving through the back door.

**The lesson generalises: a stillness check must not depend on a tool that another round can break.** I switched to counting `.claims/*.json` files directly, which needs nothing but the filesystem. Every count after 21:43 in this report uses that method.

This is also the third instance of the same shape recorded in three days — `run_all.py` committed mid-edit (BL-871), `clip_library.py` edited under two concurrent rounds (BL-863), and now `claim.py`. In a tree with a dozen live rounds, **any tool you measure with is also a target.**

## 4. Why I stopped rather than measuring anyway

An hour of polling produced no movement toward the condition — the claim count oscillated 8→13→12 with new rounds arriving faster than old ones cleared, and `quiet` never left zero. There is no trend to wait out.

BL-884 already ran the five suites under exactly these conditions and got two clean of five, with three reds that turned out to be a real broken invariant other rounds were fixing mid-window. Running them again now would produce another result that cannot separate a defect from a half-edited import from genuine flakiness. **A measurement that cannot distinguish its hypotheses is not worth 40 minutes**, and a green obtained this way would not be the genuine green you are after — it would be a coincidence.

### What would actually be needed

- **zero** claim files in `.claims/` (currently 12)
- source hash unchanged **10 continuous minutes** (best achieved: ~6, with the library moving throughout)
- **no live `clip_library/` walk** — the hard one; it has been running for 6+ hours under BL-849/BL-867

Only the owner can call that window. A round cannot force it by waiting, and should not force it by asking other rounds to stop.
