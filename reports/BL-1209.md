# BL-1209 — 71 of the 130 links he pasted were refused by an opinion, and the opinion is gone

**He had already decided.** Every link in this system's paste path arrived because he looked
at a clip and chose it. The path then applied its own judgement to that choice and refused
**71 of 130 links — 54.6%** — on grounds that had nothing to do with whether the video could
be made.

His words: *"if I pasted the link, that means it's a good clip — edit it, no questions
asked."*

Selection is the machine's job when the **machine** picks. It is nobody's job when **he**
picks. This round removes every judgement gate from the pasted-link path, keeps the three
refusals that are physically necessary, and pins the distinction so the automatic path
cannot be collapsed into it later.

Every number here came from **`.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, Python 3.11.9**. Bare `python` is
3.13 and lacks the packages. **Vendor receipt: 25 billable calls, $0.0150** against a $0.50
cap.

### The speech term was refusing clips it had not measured

Of the 52 speech refusals, **9 were on audio that never decoded at all.** `speech_frac` was
`None` — ~35% of Instagram clips carry xHE-AAC that ffmpeg 8.0 cannot read (MEMEBOT-052) —
and the gate's own doctrine turns "could not measure" into a refusal. On his last batch that
was **three of seven**: the system told him three of his picks were too talky without ever
having heard them.

That is not a wrong threshold. It is a refusal with no measurement behind it.

## 2. What now refuses, and nothing else does

Three families, all in `paste_batch.PHYSICAL_REFUSALS`. Each is a thing that **cannot be
made**, not a thing we would rather not make, and each says so in his own language:

| reason | what he is told |
|---|---|
| `could_not_resolve` | Instagram did not return the post — deleted, private, an ad, or a bad link. |
| `not_a_video_post` | the post is a photo or a carousel; there is no video to edit. |
| `could_not_retrieve` | the post resolved but its video would not download. |
| `render_failed` | the edit ran and did not produce a healthy video. |
| `no_burned_in_text` | no burned-in text was found. The edit covers your line and re-types it; with no line there is nothing to read and nothing to re-type. |
| `unreadable_text` | we could not read your line confidently. Refused rather than drawn with different words — a wrong line is worse than no video. |
| `below_the_8s_floor` | shorter than the 8.0-second floor. Nothing can add footage that was never filmed. |
| `over_the_20s_ceiling` | longer than the 20.0-second ceiling with no trim configured to cut it, so the file would ship over length. |

**`no_burned_in_text` is the renderer's own precondition, not a preference.**
`clip_pipeline.render_one` takes a `clip_label_text.SourceLine` and refuses anything else by
type (`RewrittenLabel`). With no text band there is no line to read, so there is no argument
to be had about it.

**The ceiling is asymmetric with the floor, and that is edit.py's doing.** It *trims* to
20.0s (MEMEBOT-086: trim the tail, do not gate), so a 46-second clip he pastes is **made and
cut**, not refused. `over_the_20s_ceiling` can only fire when no ceiling trim is configured
at all — then nothing would cut it and the file would ship over length. A test asserts both
halves.

### The one that was quietly removed as well: no caption card

`no_caption_card` refused **16 of 130** links because his line sat on moving picture rather
than a static panel. It never made a clip unmakeable — `measure_label_box` already returns a
**full-width strip at the height of his own text** (BL-1128), which covers his line
completely. What the static card bought was edges that land on a flat panel and therefore
show no seam.

So the strip is now **fitted to his ink** when no card is found, and the seam risk is **said
per clip**, in the results list and in the README:

> *your line sits on moving picture, not a static panel, so the white strip is fitted to the
> line itself and its edges may show a faint seam. Your line is still fully covered and
> re-typed. Rendered — you picked this clip.*

A visible cosmetic cost on a clip he chose beats a refusal he did not ask for. A degraded
output he was **not** told about would be the failure MEMEBOT-044 shipped twelve times, so
it is said, per pair, not once at the top.

## 3. The distinction, and the test that will not let it collapse

The knob is **who chose the clip** — not "is this a teaching batch", which is where BL-1142
drew the line and drew it in the wrong place.

```
picked_by="operator"   a pasted link. NO judgement gate. Speech and motion are measured
                       and reported and cannot stop a render.  ← the default
picked_by="machine"    discovery found it. Every gate applies, exactly as before.
```

**`operator` is the default**, because a pasted link is his pick by definition and a
no-judgement path that he has to remember to switch on is a path that is off.

The old `speech_mode` knob is **gone, not defaulted off** — a gate left standing with a
friendlier default is a gate that comes back. `tests/test_song_examples.py` asserts
`speech_mode`, `SPEECH_MODES`, `R_SPEECH` and `R_STATIC` no longer exist.

**The automatic path is untouched.** `clip_pipeline.gate` still refuses on the view floor,
duration, empty caption, blank cover frame, missing audio class and dialogue, and
`tests/test_paste_batch.py::TheAutomaticPathStillGates` measures it rather than asserting it
in prose:

- `test_the_SAME_clip_refuses_for_the_machine_and_renders_for_him` — one clip, one
  difference, both outcomes asserted.
- `test_clip_pipeline_gate_is_UNTOUCHED_and_still_judges` — calls `CP.gate` on a thin clip
  and asserts the view floor fires.
- `test_the_automatic_gate_still_refuses_a_dialogue_clip`.
- `test_paste_batch_never_calls_the_discovery_gate` — the other half: this path must not
  acquire the automatic path's gate either.
- `test_every_reason_the_module_can_emit_is_PHYSICAL` — `set(ALL_REASONS) ==
  set(PHYSICAL_REFUSALS)`. A later round that adds a reason fails here until somebody names
  which physical family it belongs to, which is the conversation that has to happen before a
  judgement returns.

`REMOVED_JUDGEMENTS` keeps each deleted gate **by name with its measurement**, so the next
reader finds the decision instead of rediscovering the argument.

## 5. What did not change

The edit is the shipped one, function for function — this is not a second renderer:

- **OCR** the burned-in line, **cover** it with the fitted white label, **re-type the same
  words character for character** in black. His line and ours are printed side by side in
  the README and flagged if they ever differ.
- **His song**, his marked hook. No matcher, no tiers, no bias, no rotation.
- **The drop placed from his own marked offsets** — `drop_placer` aligns the marked drop
  instant to the biggest change in the picture, inside the window that will actually ship.
- **No filters, no zoom, no rotation, no added canvas** — and no jitter: `_neutralise_transform`
  zeroes the random speed (0.93–1.08) and head trim (0.1–1.5s), because a head trim makes
  OURS and ORIGINAL start at different moments for a reason nothing on screen explains.
- **The join key survives.** `store_path` and `hook_id` still land on the ledger, so an
  operator-picked window can still earn rotation. He is bypassing the matcher, not the
  learning loop.

## 6. Money

**Charged on the ATTEMPT, never on success.** One `/v1/media/by/url` call per link, reserved
and written **before** the request is made. Retrieval fails often — deleted, private, an ad —
and a failed resolve costs exactly what a good one does. Charging only successes has rebuilt
the same hole twice here (BL-886, BL-876).

The estimate is shown **before** the run and the cap **binds before anything is spent**:

```
$ paste_batch --links ... --cap 0.003 --estimate-only
ok=False
REFUSED before spending anything: 12 links would cost $0.0072 and the cap is $0.0030.
Paste fewer links or raise the cap.
```

| | |
|---|---|
| estimate shown before the run | $0.0072 for 12 links |
| cap | $0.50 |
| **billed this round** | **25 calls, $0.0150** |
| rendition + audio downloads | CDN GETs, unbilled |

**From the vendor receipt, not from a count of my own.** `spend.json` went
**16.922486 → 16.937486**, a delta of exactly **$0.015000** = 25 calls at $0.0006.

Those 25 are three passes and I am naming all three rather than only the one that worked:
**12** for a `--dry-run` pass that proved the gates before any render time was spent, **12**
for the real batch, and **1 wasted** — a first attempt that died silently after its first
link. It left `_batch.json` stopped at `checking` with an empty log, and the one call it had
already made was **still on the ledger**, which is the charge-on-attempt design behaving
correctly: the money was recorded before the request, so a process that vanishes cannot make
a run look free. I re-ran it unbuffered and it completed. I did not find the cause of the
first death and am not claiming one.

## 8. Still broken, and whose

- **`edit.py` reports `Errors: 1` and nothing else** on a failed render — no reason at all.
  This path guesses the likeliest cause from the label's character count (`LABEL_CHARS_SUSPECT
  = 104`, measured) because that is the only fact available on this side of the boundary. The
  actual defect is in `memebot/scraper/edit.py`, which this round does not hold.
- **~35% of clips carry xHE-AAC that ffmpeg 8.0 cannot decode** (MEMEBOT-052). It is why
  speech is unmeasurable on a third of his picks and why some ORIGINAL halves ship silent.
  Not fixable from here.
- **BPM and danceability are unmeasurable on a fresh paste.** They need Essentia's MTG heads,
  which have no Windows wheel (BL-1134) and run only under WSL2 (BL-1135). The README says
  "not measured" rather than printing a blank that reads as "no rhythm".
