## 7. Recommended order of operations

1. **Suppress the 41 hard-dead + 16 no-route addresses before your next send.** 1.4% of the pool, most of your hard bounces.
2. **Send Tier 1 (100), then Tier 2 (500), 100/day.** Same deliverability throughout; declining reply value.
3. **Run the 2,868 IG DMs in parallel** — free, no reputation risk, and they reach accounts 10× larger than the email pool.
4. **Start writing `date_sent` / `bounced`.** Right now every send is invisible; without it no ranking can ever be validated against real outcomes, including this one.
5. **Do not send the 265 role inboxes** until 1–4 are exhausted.

## Method and limits

Read-only pass over 57,386 master rows; address classification re-derived from the address per BL-746 rather than trusting `email_source`; MX/A resolution on all 926 non-freemail domains via the local resolver (871 MX, 16 A-only, 39 dead); freemail domains assumed live without lookup.

**Not validated against outcomes** — there are none recorded. Weights are reasoned, not fitted; the ordering is only as good as the reasoning behind them, and the first version of it was wrong (§2). Reach is `deep_median_views` where present (3.8% of rows) and `avg_views_sampled` otherwise, which are not the same measurement. Follower band is assumed 1k–2M globally rather than per campaign. No send was made, no row was modified, and no scoring system was implemented in the pipeline.
