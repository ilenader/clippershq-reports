### The five columns are a shipped contract

`clippershq.exporters.check_operator_columns` raises unless the **data** columns are exactly
**EMAIL · HANDLE · RANK · WHAT THE JUDGE SAID · PROFILE LINK** — exact, not a superset, because

### And it has a reason field — which is the whole repair

**The old marks file had no reason field. That single absence is what made the 97 an
identity**: truth was the judge's own answer wherever he pressed GOOD. So this sheet does
**not** offer "was the judge right". It asks **his** verdict — WANT / REJECT / can't tell — and
puts a free-text box beside it. Per page he also gets a readable **560 px grid thumbnail**
(both text bands legible; the full 934 px capture opens on click), the drawn text the judge
read, its three C24 counts, and its written reason.

### Marks save safely, and the false green has a specific fix

He lost 50 marks once to a save that failed silently in green, and **that exact shape was
rebuilt and caught again recently: a mark made in the same millisecond as a download read as
already-downloaded**, because the code compared timestamps with `<=`. Timestamps are not unique
and a millisecond is not an ordering.

* Every mark carries a **strictly increasing integer `seq`** assigned at mark time. "Waiting"
  means `seq > downloadedThrough` — **an integer comparison that cannot tie. No timestamp takes
  part in the saved/waiting decision anywhere.**
* Every write is **read back and compared** before it counts.
* The **Download** button is present from the first paint, not on success.
* The counter reads **"N saved · M waiting (of 100 pages)"** at all times and turns red while
  anything is waiting.
* Any failure paints a **blocking white-on-red banner** telling him to download now — never a
  green tick. Closing the tab with unsaved marks prompts.

### Verified by re-reading the file on disk

The build **refuses to finish** unless the bytes it just wrote contain all of it
(`scratch/bl1296_sheet_verify.json`): **100 profile links** (`https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/`,

## 5. What his answer will mean — written before he marks

**This section was written into `scratch/bl1296_score.py` before the sheet was built and before
he has marked anything.** It is registered in advance precisely so the bands cannot be redrawn
around whatever comes back — which is the failure mode that produced every optimistic number
this project has published.

### First, the floor — and it is not a constant

**Rejecting every page scores `1 − (his want rate)`.** On his marked hundred that was **81%**,
because he wanted 19 of 100. **On these pages his want rate is unknown until he marks**, so 81%
is a reference point, not the answer:

* if he wants **30** of these, the floor is **70%** and 82% would be a good result;
* if he wants **8**, the floor is **92%** and 89% would be a *bad* one.

**Compute the floor from his marks first. Then read the agreement against it.** A number quoted
without its floor is not a measurement.

### The bands

| what comes back | what it means |
|---|---|
| **at or below the floor** | The shipped brief is not earning its keep on fresh supply. Rejecting everything would do as well or better. |
| **above the floor but < 4 points clear** | It still beats switching it off, but most of the edge the marked-set figures advertise does not survive on pages nobody curated. Those figures are optimistic and should be requoted. |
| **≥ 4 points clear of the floor, and ≥ 85% absolute** | The judge does on unseen pages roughly what it does on marked ones. |

**The comparison point, stated honestly:** v8 scores **89% on his marked hundred, Wilson
[81.4, 93.8] — 8 points clear of that set's 81% floor.** But that 89 is itself inflated,
because the marks file records the **judge's own verdict** plus his GOOD/BAD **on it**, so
agreement there is forced to `97 − broke + fixed`. **This round has no such prop.** His verdict
on the sheet is his own, captured independently, with a reason field beside it.

### And read the WANT class as directional

Agreement is the tight number. **WANT precision and recall will carry intervals 25–35 points
wide** at this n — on his marked hundred, with 19 true wants, they ran [50.0, 86.2] and
[56.7, 91.5]. Differences inside those intervals are not differences.

**Say the honest thing first: that interval contains 19%, so the want rate alone is not
