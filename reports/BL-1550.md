# BL-1550 — The two halves cannot be joined: they are different platforms. And a second label set cut the gate's recall claim by 18 points.

**Round:** BL-1550 · **Spent: $0.00** · no vendor call was made or attempted · every source
file byte-identical before and after · the workbook updated in place with **no row lost**.

---

## THE PARAGRAPH AT THE TOP

**The halves cannot be joined, and it is not a key problem.** `tiktok_handle` is populated
on **100% of both sides**, the normaliser was proved in both directions, and the join's
positive control re-found **1,257 of 1,257** known rows. It still matched **0 of 16,555**.
The reason: the address-bearing rows and the bio-bearing rows are **different platforms** —
**96.4%** of rows with an address and no bio are Instagram; **99.8%** of rows with a bio
and no address are TikTok. Even inside TikTok, where both halves exist, **0 of 269** meet.
There is no shared population to key. Every other candidate key — `secuid`,
`instagram`, `instagram_url`, `tiktok_url` — also returns **0**, and `display_name`'s 5
"matches" are collisions: **0 of 5 have agreeing handles and two of the five "names" are a
single character**. So **no additional leads became scoreable this round: still 13 of
13,178**, and the fix is named in section 3 — it is a change to what the Instagram funnel
*writes*, not a better join.

**The second thing is a correction to my own last round.** BL-1548 published recall
**97.56% [87.40, 99.57]** from 120 hand labels. A disjoint 120 drawn from the same
population scored **79.41% [63.20, 89.65]** — the point estimate fell **18 points**. On
the combined **240**, the honest figure is **precision 87.65% [78.74, 93.15], recall
94.67% [87.07, 97.91]**. One set of 120 could not have told you that, which is exactly why
a second one was worth $0.00 and an hour.

**And the second set found a real gap.** The vocabulary had `editor`, `edits`, `editing`,
`edited` — and **not the bare verb `edit`**. Two of its seven misses were *"i edit anything
and everything"* and *"WANNA EDIT LIKE ME?"*. Adding the bounded verb plus the
Portuguese/Spanish nouns gained **4 real editors at 0 false keeps**, and closed the one row
BL-1548 explicitly gave up on. **The gate now ships as `clippershq/editor_gate.py`** with
16 committed guard-rail tests — it had been living in a file whose own docstring called it
a PROTOTYPE.

**Where the gate can run: 748 of 2,387 = 31.34% [29.51, 33.23] are likely editors.**

## 1. THE JOIN — PROVED, AND STILL ZERO

`master_leads.csv`: **72,971 rows, 72 columns** (parsed, never line-counted).

| | has a bio | no bio |
|---|---:|---:|
| **named `email_source`** | 1,257 | **16,555** |
| **blank `email_source`** | **48,886** | 6,273 |

The two off-diagonal cells are the round: 16,555 rows have an address and no bio; 48,886
have a bio and no address.

**THE KEY WAS CHOSEN BY CENSUS, NOT BY ITS NAME.** All 72 columns were measured for fill
rate on each side separately, because *a column full on one side and empty on the other
cannot join anything*:

| column | address-side fill | bio-side fill | distinct (addr / bio) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **`tiktok_handle`** | **100.0%** | **100.0%** | 17,797 / 50,143 |
| `display_name` | 81.2% | 100.0% | 14,382 / 44,194 |
| `tiktok_url` | 24.5% | 100.0% | 4,342 / 50,130 |
| `secuid` | 9.2% | 99.9% | 1,645 / 50,074 |
| `instagram` | 96.6% | 4.2% | 17,203 / 2,125 |

**THE NORMALISER WAS PROVED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS BEFORE IT WAS USED** — 8 pairs that must
collapse (case, leading `@`, platform prefix `tt:`, trailing dot, zero-width joiner,
non-breaking space, fullwidth characters, a full profile URL) and 4 that must not
(`someone` vs `someone1`, `edit` vs `edits`, `some_one` vs `someone`, and an empty string
which must never become a key).

**THE RESULT:**

| | of 16,555 | |
|---|---:|---|
| matched, same platform (usable) | **0** | 0.00% [0.00, 0.02] |
| matched only ACROSS a platform boundary | 1 | 0.01% |
| ambiguous (>1 bio row per key) | 0 | — |
| no bio row with that handle | 16,554 | 99.99% |

**AND A ZERO IS EXACTLY WHAT A BROKEN READER RETURNS, SO IT WAS CONTROLLED:**

- **Positive:** rows that already hold *both* an address and a bio re-find themselves
  through the same key — **1,257 of 1,257 (100%)**. Inside TikTok alone, **1,019 of
  1,019**. The comparison works.
- **Negative:** a fabricated handle matches **0** rows.
- **Negative:** the largest bio bucket for any one key is **2** — the normaliser has not
  collapsed everyone into one bucket.
- **Negative:** **0** empty keys were used as keys.

**Every other candidate key was tried on its own terms and every one returns 0:**

| key | present addr / bio | matches |
|---|---|---:|
| `secuid` | 457 / 48,886 | **0** |
| `instagram` | 15,961 / 883 | **0** |

**The `display_name` 5 are refused, and shown to be refusable:** for all 5, the two rows'
handles **disagree**, and two of the five "names" are **one character long**. A name is
not an identifier. Matching on it would staple one person's bio onto another person's
address, and every number downstream would inherit it.

**THE ONE PLACE A JOIN COULD HAVE WORKED, AND DID NOT.** 269 TikTok rows have an address
and no bio; 48,804 TikTok rows have a bio and no address. **0 of 269 meet** [0.00, 1.41].
The two halves are disjoint account sets *even inside a single platform*.

## 3. WHAT THE TWO FUNNELS WOULD HAVE TO WRITE

This is the deliverable from a failed join, and it is a smaller fix than a better key:

1. **The Instagram funnel must store the bio it can already read for free.** 15,958 of the
   16,555 unscoreable rows are Instagram. BL-1545 measured that the Instagram bio is
   available on the **free profile page for 76.5%** of accounts that have one — no vendor
   call. The funnel that writes `instagram:business-field` and `instagram:bio-text` rows is
   already fetching a profile; it is discarding the bio. **That single change would make
   roughly 12,000 currently NOT-SCOREABLE leads scoreable at $0.00.** (0.765 × 15,958 ≈
   12,208 — an arithmetic projection from a separately measured rate, **not** a measured
   figure, and it deserves a direct check on a small sample before anyone leans on it.)
2. **Or write a shared stable id.** `secuid` is present on 99.9% of the bio side and
   **9.2%** of the address side. Both funnels already see it; only one records it.
3. **The Spotify routes cannot be fixed this way at all.** `spotify:none` (5,670) and
   `spotify:resolved-instagram` (4,960) carry no bio and no TikTok identity. They need a
   different answer, and this round does not have one.

## 6. THE VOCABULARY FIX THE SECOND SET BOUGHT

Of set 2's seven missed editors, two said it in the plainest possible English and the
vocabulary had no word for it:

> *"i edit anything and everything"* · *"WANNA EDIT LIKE ME?"*

The list had `editor`, `editors`, `edits`, `editing`, `edited` — **and not the bare verb
`edit`**. BL-1548 added the noun and the participles and missed the infinitive.

**⚠️ BARE `edit` IS THE MOST DANGEROUS TERM IN THIS VOCABULARY AND IS ONLY SAFE BOUNDED.**
The control plants eight trap words and reports what the unbounded form *would* do:

```
credit card accepted   bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
creditors only         bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
editorial team         bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
meditation and yoga    bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
accredited             bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
editorials             bounded: False   unbounded WOULD fire: True
```

Measured on **all 240** labels, never on the set that motivated the change:

| variant | precision | recall | newly kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| shipped before | 87.01% [77.72, 92.79] | 89.33% [80.34, 94.50] | — |
| + bare verb `edit` | 87.50% [78.50, 93.07] | 93.33% [85.32, 97.12] | 3 gained, **0 false** |
| + romance nouns | 87.18% [77.98, 92.88] | 90.67% [81.97, 95.41] | 1 gained, **0 false** |
| **+ both (shipped)** | **87.65% [78.74, 93.15]** | **94.67% [87.07, 97.91]** | **4 gained, 0 false** |

**⚠️ THE INTERVALS OVERLAP.** This is an improvement in the point estimate with the
direction of both changes known — **not an established gain**. It ships because the
mechanism is named (a missing infinitive), the four rows it fixes are shown, and nothing
was lost; not because an interval moved.

**One of the four is BL-1548's own acknowledged miss** — *"i try and edit some stuff on
FCP12"*, the row that round said it was leaving. It is now caught.

**Still refused, and still measured:** requiring business-intent language buys 2 points of
precision and costs **51 points of recall**. A test now pins that refusal so it cannot be
re-added quietly.

## 8. WHAT YOU GOT WRONG

**I published 97.56% recall last round and it did not replicate.** A disjoint sample from
the same population gave 79.41%. The number was not fabricated and the method was sound —
it was a sample of 120 reported as if it were a property of the gate. The correction is in
section 5 and in the shipped module's docstring. **The lesson is not "measure more"; it is
that I stated a single-sample figure without the humility its width demanded**, even while
printing the interval next to it.

**I shipped a word-bounded handle rule into `clippershq/` and it was a regression.** It
looked like consistency with the bio rule. Measured, it lost **four real editors and bought
exactly zero precision** (5 false positives either way). **A handle is not prose** — nobody
writes "creditors" in a username to mean creditors. I found this only because I re-scored
the module instead of copying the prototype's numbers into its docstring. Had I trusted the
inherited figures, the file would have shipped claiming 97.56% while scoring 87.80%.

**Then I "tidied" the list and cost three more false positives.** Adding `amv` and a bare
`.ae` "for consistency" moved precision 88.89% → 83.33% for zero recall. **Adding a
plausible term to a measured list silently un-measures the list.** Both facts are now
pinned by name in the test.

**I put two REAL creator handles into shipped source as test fixtures.** While writing the
guard-rail test I needed examples of compound handles — `<word>edit` with no separator —
and I reached for two I had *just read in the bios I was adjudicating*. They went into
`clippershq/editor_gate.py`, the test, and a scratch instrument. **The leak scan caught all
five occurrences before the commit**, and they are now synthetic strings that were checked
against both corpora *before* use. This is the same defect as the fabricated fixture that
turned out to be a real row, arriving from the opposite direction: **a fixture that looks
illustrative is still a person if you took it from the data.** The test now says so above
the list.

**My first site census over-matched by 10×.** Probing for `editor`/`edits` returned 238
constants across 106 files, most of them config keys. The honest count with distinctive
tool names is 22. I caught it only because the output was classified by *where* each hit
sat rather than counted.

**And a control of mine asserted the opposite of what the measurement decided.** After the
substring rule was restored, my boundary control still demanded that `creditunion` must not
fire — the behaviour that had just been measured and rejected. A control that a *correct*
tool fails is worse than no control: it either stops a clean round or teaches you to ignore
the alarm. It now states the cost instead, and the cost is measured on all 72,971 real
handles: **11 of them** misfire this way.

**Smaller, and all self-inflicted:** a backtick inside a `python -c` string ran command
substitution and silently ate text out of a docstring; two `SyntaxError`s from mixing
implicit string concatenation with `+` in a regex (the version that silently "works" is the
one that drops a boundary); and my first backup reported **3 rows** for a store holding
**3,270**, because the payload is a dict under `pages` and I only looked for a list. A
sixth control now plants a 3,270-entry wrapped store and requires the reader to report
3,270.

**What I did not do:** I did not fetch a single Instagram bio, which is the change that
would actually unlock section 3's ~12,000 rows. That is network work with a real footprint
and this round was budgeted at $0.00 and no calls. It is the obvious next round, and the
projection in section 3 is arithmetic, not a measurement.

## 9. SAFETY

- **$0.00.** No vendor call was made or attempted. Every input was already on disk.
- **Backups first**, sha256-verified, all paths from one round constant: 8 stores,
  0 absent. **All six corruption controls fire on planted damage**, including the
  index-qualified one — on a duplicated pair, deleting one leaves the natural-key set
  **provably identical** and only the position-carrying digest moves.
- **Every source byte-identical after reading**, confirmed by sha256 on `master_leads.csv`
  and all four run files.
- **No lead data in this report.** The leak scan is built from **both** corpora — the lead
  store's 73,112 handles and 12,691 addresses *and* this round's four run files — because a
  scan built from one is structurally blind to the other.
- **The public bar does NOT inherit the "already at HEAD" exclusion.** 23 of the 30 longest
  lead-store handles are already committed at HEAD in earlier rounds' scratch dumps; an
  exclusion that skips them would be blind to exactly the handles worth catching. The
  public bar tests against the vocabulary of the 395 already-published reports instead.
- **Every detector proven on a planted control before the scan runs**, and the synthetic
  fixtures are themselves checked against both corpora — an invented "obviously fake" local
  part at a common provider once turned out to be a real row.
- **No address is ever truncated to redact it** — `local[:2]` is the whole local part on
  145 real rows. Matched values are counted by position and never printed.
- **Zero C0 control bytes asserted before writing, not after** — and the shipped module has
  its own test for them, because a word-boundary escape routed through a shell heredoc
  became two literal `0x08` bytes in a source file one round ago.
- The bios quoted in sections 5 and 6 are craft vocabulary with every handle removed.
