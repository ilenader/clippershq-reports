# BL-1127 — the render rebuilt to the operator's spec

**THE FOLDER IS `outputs_bl1127_operator/`.** Fifteen pairs, `NN_handle__ORIGINAL.mp4`
directly above `NN_handle__OURS.mp4`, with a `README.md` naming per pair the permalink, the
song, the phrase that fired, the hook window and every change made. 109 MB, deliberately
outside `scratch/` so nothing garbage-collects it, and deliberately outside git so the
history does not carry the bytes.

The operator rejected the last two batches on sight and restated the spec. This is that
spec and nothing else — no substitutions, no improvements, nothing he did not ask for.

---

## What the seven points became

### 1. The video is the source, full frame, untouched

**There is no canvas any more.** Both previous attempts kept a 1080×1920 canvas and argued
about how to fill it. `scale_width: 864` put a 36.0% white ring on **every** video —
`1 − (864·1536)/(1080·1920)` — including the 92.6% of sources already 9:16, and that ring is
the white he photographed. `scale_width: 1080` plus a blurred cover-cropped backdrop
(BL-1100) is better and still **adds** canvas on every source that is not 9:16; blurred
letterboxing is letterboxing.

The only geometry satisfying both of his sentences at once — *"if the source is 1080×1920 it
fills the canvas edge to edge"* **and** *"no letterboxing of any kind added by us"* — is the
source's own frame, passed through. `video.full_frame: true` short-circuits
`build_filter_chain` before any canvas key is read.

Picture share is now **100% by construction, on every aspect ratio**, rather than by
measurement on one. The fifteen sources ship at five different sizes: 720×1280, 940×1670,
1078×1234, 1080×1920 and **1082×970** — a landscape source that under any canvas-based
template was 60%+ dead space.

| measured on the finished files | result |
|---|---|
| output frame size == source frame size | **15 / 15** |
| added canvas, our chain re-run with `canvas.color: magenta` | **0.0000%** on 14/15, 0.0716% on one |
| the same sources through the OLD composition, same counter | **36.0% – 67.7%** |

That last row is the point. `full_frame` never reads `canvas.color`, so a magenta count of
0.0 proves nothing on its own — **an instrument that cannot fire is not evidence.** The
control column runs the same source through the pre-BL-1127 composition and must read ~36%,
or the measurement is reported BROKEN rather than as a pass. The one clip reading 0.0716% is
magenta-coloured **picture**, not canvas: its frame size still equals its source's exactly,
and there is no `scale`, `pad` or `overlay` in its chain to produce canvas with.

### 2. No chipped or rounded corners

The chip is `rotate={deg}*PI/180:c={canvas_color}:ow=iw:oh=ih`. `rotate` fills the void
triangles it opens with the canvas colour — **white** — and at the configured ±0.8° that is
a wedge in a corner. `rotation_deg` is pinned to `0.0` and the `rotate` filter is refused by
name in the chain builder.

Measured from frames: the four 40×40 corner patches of each output agree with the matching
source frame to a maximum of **0.842 / 255**, and none is a flat near-white wedge. Corners
the label reaches into are **excluded and reported as excluded**, never silently passed.

### 3. The white label covers only the source's own text

The band's **rows** come from `clip_text_signal.text_bands` — the Sobel-x / Otsu /
wide-short-close detector, `bands > 0 AND top_band <= TOP_BAND_MAX`. Its **columns** come
from the first and last inked column inside those rows, which is the side-padding primitive
the brief names.

Both halves were needed, and I only found out why by looking at the frames:

* `text_bands` returns **connected components**, and its shape filters (`MIN_W_FRAC 0.18`,
  `MIN_ASPECT 3.0`, a density window) drop the short words at the ends of a line. Two of the
  first fifteen renders shipped a 637 px strip on a 1082 px frame and read
  **`Me [OUR STRIP] ex tries [OUR STRIP] him:`** — the source's own words legible on both
  sides of our label. A sticker over the middle of a sentence is worse than no cover at all.
* the unfiltered union of every qualifying band made a **490 px slab on a 1670 px frame**,
  running from an infographic's title to the first rows of its table and swallowing the
  crest and photograph between. 29% of frame height is not a strip. Bands are clustered by
  vertical gap now and the topmost cluster wins.

Final widths run **60% – 100%** of the frame; 100% only where the source's own line really
does span it, which is his own carve-out. Our text is black, centred, shrunk to fit the
measured box **on both axes** — the type gives way, the box never grows by a pixel — and
ellipsised at a 14 px floor rather than allowed to overflow.

**No measurement means no strip and no text.** There is no default band and no fallback,
because a white band across empty picture is the defect he refused.

| measured inside each label, from frames | across 15 |
|---|---|
| the strip is white | ≥ **77.1%** of the box |
| our black ink is on it | ≥ **1.5%** of the box |
| the SOURCE had ink under it (Sobel-x energy) | ≥ **49.8** |
| white band added anywhere outside the box | **0 px**, rows and columns |

### 4. No filters

`brightness`, `saturation`, `gamma`, `hue` and `sharpening` pinned to identity;
`vignette`, `colorbalance` and `frame_noise` disabled. **And** every one of them refused by
name in `build_filter_chain`, with the refusal printed — `eq`, `hue`, `vignette`,
`colorbalance`, `unsharp`, `noise`, `gblur`, `scale`, `crop`, `pad`, `rotate`, `overlay`.

Both halves, because `config.yaml` is shared by ~10 rounds and one of them will re-widen a
range. A guard in the config alone is a guard someone else's sweep can quietly undo.

`transform.enabled` **stays true** — the same block carries the trims, the speed, the audio
chain, the encoder roll and the ambient bed, and turning the master switch off would ship
silent videos.

### 5. No zoom, no rotation

Both pinned and both refused by name. The trade was already established and the operator has
accepted it: at full bleed the anti-fingerprint zoom crops 5–20% of every edge and it ate
the source's headline on **10 of 10** — *"Temple explains…"* rendering as *"ɔmple
explains…"*.

**The fourteen anti-fingerprint dimensions that survive**, none of which touches a pixel of
the picture:

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 head trim | 5 audio volume dB | 9 encoder preset | 13 audio treatment route |
| 2 tail trim | 6 EQ band 1 (f/g/Q) | 10 encoder CRF | 14 hook window (5–6 per song) |
| 3 speed (setpts+atempo) | 7 EQ band 2 (f/g/Q) | 11 encoder keyint | |
| 4 pitch shift cents | 8 EQ band 3 (f/g/Q) | 12 bed start offset + gain | |

Asserted, not assumed: a new suite rolls the **shipped** config 40 times and checks the
excursion at the frame's top corner is **exactly zero**, that no picture filter is emitted at
all, and that the surviving dimensions still vary.

### 6. Dialogue clips skipped entirely

Gated in `clip_pipeline.gate_detail` with the reason **"dialogue clip — its own subtitles
collide with our label, and its speech is already buried by its own music"**. Digit-free,
because `run_batch` normalises numbers out of a reason so reasons group in the decision log,
and a reason carrying a number fragments into one bucket per clip. **76** of the clips with
local media were excluded by it. None was removed from the library.

At SELECTION and not at the renderer, for the reason the three gate terms above it exist: a
clip refused downstream has already paid for a re-fetch and ~40 s of encoding.

His reason is visual as well as audio, and this round made the visual half unavoidable: the
label covers the source's own text band, and a dialogue clip's subtitles **move with the
dialogue**, so there is no one band to cover. BL-1124 measured the audio half — 10 of 12
dialogue clips sit under 6 dB of speech-to-bed contrast **in the source**, median 2.0 dB, two
negative. The clip's own music already buries its dialogue. Nothing we do to the bed recovers
that, which is why this is a clip type to exclude rather than a level to tune.

### 7. Never force a song match

**His example reproduces, with one correction to the id.** He named `sng_0002`; the row is
`sng_0003`, and everything else in his description is exact:

```
clip   3950292323383892012_227282247
       "A young boy ... A woman is handing him a note, and he is questioning
        its authenticity. The text overlay suggests he notices it's fake"
song   sng_0003  — the SUMMER / FOOTBALL song
fired  track_title:Mother Nature -> mood:warm
```

**`track_title` is the reposter's own background audio.** It says what the poster laid over
the clip, never what happens in it, so *"Mother Nature → warm"* is a fact about a production
music library and not about a boy holding a forged banknote. **Six of the ten renders in the
batch he rejected were matched that way**, and three more put the female-empowerment anthem
on a police sergeant rerouting traffic, a firefighter in a psych evaluation and a
bomb-disposal soldier being questioned — all on *"Hope of the Heart"*.

`song_library.CONTENT_EVIDENCE_TIERS` now names the tiers whose firing phrase describes
**this clip's picture**, and there is exactly one: `VISION_RULE`, which reads the clip's own
scene description against a named strong/weak phrase with `requires_any` / `excludes_any` /
`outcome_contradicts_any` guards on top. `FRANCHISE_MOOD` fails identically (a franchise says
which *film*, so a quiet dialogue scene and a decapitation route to the same song),
`GENRE_MOOD` and `VALENCE_MOOD` are one word about a whole title, and `FALLBACK` is a house
set — forcing by definition.

Two further forcing paths were closed:

* a **parked** plan had `song_path` empty, fell out of the `isfile` loop and landed on the
  generic *"song_library returned no existing file"* — so the LRU corpus tier then forced a
  song onto a clip the matcher had just refused. The park reason is carried out now.
* when the matcher itself decided nothing fits, **the LRU tier does not run at all**. It
  still runs for what it was built for — no store, an unreadable store, a store of
  placeholder paths — because *"the matcher never got to decide"* is a different fact from
  *"it decided nothing fits"*.

**61 clips matched nothing and got no video**, each listed in the folder README with the tier
that was refused and the phrase it would have fired on. **`sng_0002` got zero clips in this
batch.** That gap is the correct output.

The term defaults off in `song_library` and is passed by `pick_song` (the render path) **and**
`servable_by` (which orders what the render path is offered), so a gate that promises what
the renderer refuses cannot open up here.

| added canvas on magenta | 0.0000% (one at 0.0716%, picture) |
| magenta instrument control fires | 36.0 – 67.7% |

| no chipped corner | max corner MAE **0.842 / 255** |

Alignment is **searched**, not computed: the render trims up to 1.5 s off the head and runs
at 0.93–1.08×, neither value is on the record, and a search that finds no match **errors**
rather than reporting "different". `picture_unaltered` also carries a **motion floor** — the
frame-to-frame change of the source at that moment — because no comparison against a sampled
frame can beat it, and without it a fast pan reads as a colour grade.

### The instruments that had to be fixed before they could be trusted

Three of them reported a **working deliverable as broken**, which is the failure direction
that matters:

* the corner check compared **our white strip** against the source's picture wherever the
  label reached a corner, and read 31.3 / 255 on a correctly drawn label.
* the white-band check was **absolute**, so three sources that are meme cards on white were
  reported as us adding white — while their frame-vs-source error was 0.16, 0.30 and
  2.98 / 255. It is differential now: what spec 1 forbids is white we **add**.
* the motion floor, above.

And the guards named in the brief held. Every `filter_complex` probe carries
`setpts=PTS-STARTPTS`, because `-ss` before `-i` does not rebase timestamps and the earlier
overlay probe emitted bare magenta and read 100% dead canvas on 4 good renders.
`output_dir` is the directory the source was staged into, because it **is** the clips root
and pointing it at a sibling made `--only` match zero videos at `rc=0`.

## Two defects found by reading the frames, not by any exit code

**A caption reduced to nothing, and a video shipped with no label at all.** `edit.main()`
capped `--override-text` at 120 characters by calling `caption_hook` on **raw** text.
`strip_emoji`'s own comment already said the punctuation strip has to *follow* it, because
stripping `🎥🎬: The Legend of Hei II` is what exposes the leading colon. On raw text that
colon **is** the first sentence boundary:

```
'🎥🎬: Shriek If You Know What I Did Last Friday the 13th (2000) ...'   200 chars
  -> caption_hook -> '🎥🎬…'  -> strip_emoji -> '…'  -> junk strip -> ''
```

One render of fifteen shipped with no strip and no text, at **exit 0**. The cleaning now
precedes the cut, with a word-boundary fallback when no sentence fits inside the budget.

**And it can no longer happen silently.** A measured box with no text now **raises**:
`apply_template` logs `edit:filter-build-fail` and reports the clip as failed, rather than
shipping a blank strip over the source's hook or a video with no text and no explanation.

**A previous round's render selected as a source.** The first media walk picked
`scratch/bl1126_work/…/final/white_frame/<clip_id>.mp4` — a finished BL-1126 render, because
outputs are deliberately named for their clip_id and it was the biggest file with that stem.
Rendering it would have produced a video of a video, with last round's 36% white ring baked
into a frame we then measured as untouched and called full-bleed. Caught by extracting the
frame. Selection now excludes any mp4 under a render output directory.

## Off-brief, stated

**The source's own black bars are left alone**, and nine of the fifteen have them — visible
on 01, 02, 05, 06, 10, 11, 13, 15. `detect_content_crop` used to strip them and it is now off
on this template, because removing them is a change to the source's geometry and *"untouched"*
rules that out. This is the one place where the honest reading of the spec produces something
the operator may not want, so it is named at the top of the folder README as a question for
him rather than decided here.

**One label still covers picture as well as text.** Clip 03 is an infographic whose header
band contains a crest and a photograph between its title and its subtitle; its strip is 23%
of frame height. The clustering brought it down from 29% and no further rule fixes it without
guessing which parts of a graphic are "text".

**I used the detector's shipped threshold, not the brief's number.** The brief cites
`top_band <= 0.35` at P=91.3% / R=95.5%; `clip_text_signal.TOP_BAND_MAX` ships **0.40**, and
its own docstring records why — 0.35 produced four false negatives on MEMEBOT-120's 42 hand
labels, the highest true negative sits at 0.415, and 0.40 is in the clean gap. I read the
constant rather than hardcoding a number beside it.

**`servable_by` and `pick_song` agree; the dashboard's `renderable` count was not touched.**
It calls the matcher directly with the looser default, so it will overstate how much of the
library can now be rendered. That is a dashboard round's fix, not this one's, and it is
recorded rather than quietly left.
