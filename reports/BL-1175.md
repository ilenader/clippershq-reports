# BL-1175 — the meme-page finder is built, and 17% is the number that changes the plan

**17.0% of meme pages carry an email, 95% Wilson [10.9, 25.5]**, measured on 100 of the 288
already-known repost accounts. Every one of the 17 came from the **bio**. The link-in-bio
path, which the plan assumed was the second half of the funnel, returned **0 emails from 24
links**.

At 17%, **300 emails needs ~1,765 pages resolved**, not the ~750 a 40% rate would have
bought. That is the number the round exists to produce and everything below is downstream of
it.

Every figure here came from **`.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, Python 3.11.9**.

## 2. Finding pages, and the filter that had to be measured before it was trusted

**The seen-set is persistent and locked across read AND write.** `PageSeen` holds the lock for
the whole read-modify-write and releases it after the `os.replace`, because `os.replace` makes
ONE write atomic and cannot order a PAIR — the race that cost `clip_seen.json` 12 of 13
records. A corrupt file raises rather than reading as empty, since returning `{}` would
silently re-walk everything while reporting a clean dedup. `record_many` merges a whole batch
in one locked write instead of taking the lock 500 times. Eight concurrent writers are
pinned in the tests; all nine records survive.

**Discovery, measured:** 10 hashtags → **180 distinct accounts**, ~18–30 per billed request.

### The page filter, and why it needed a caption sieve

The clip classifier is measured at 100% precision on 60 hand-labelled clips — judging the
**burned-in line**. Captions are written by different people for different reasons, and it let
these through as MEME-HOOK:

```
"link in bio to shop our sale now 20% off"  -> MEME-HOOK
"shop now"                                  -> MEME-HOOK
"out now on spotify"                        -> MEME-HOOK
"check out my new merch drop"               -> MEME-HOOK
```

Those are exactly the promotional accounts the filter exists to keep off the paid leg. So a
caption-specific sieve is layered **on top**, in `meme_finder`, and it only ever *demotes* —
it can turn a hook into promotional, never the reverse. It lives here and not in
`meme_format` because that module is tuned against clips and widening its vocabulary would
re-tune a measured classifier for a population it was never measured on.

**Posts with no caption are counted as `unknown` and excluded from the denominator**, never
against the page. The purest meme pages put the joke in the image and post an empty caption;
convicting on that would reject precisely the best targets.

### Precision: 65%, and the check is weaker than it should be

Of the 20 pages that passed the filter, **13 are meme pages and 7 are not** — personal
accounts and one UGC-creator account (`…_ugc`). **Precision 65.0%, 95% Wilson [43.3, 81.9].**

> **This was judged from the handle and the filter's own recorded meme-share, NOT by reading
> captions, and that is my error.** The re-fetch passed a *username* to `user_medias`, which
> needs a numeric user id, so it returned nothing for all 40 pages and billed ~$0.024 for it.
> A name-based judgement is weaker evidence than the brief asked for and the number should be
> read with that caveat, not quoted as a caption-verified precision.

**At 65% precision, roughly a third of the resolution spend goes on pages he will not mail.**
That is the single most valuable thing to fix next, and it is cheap to fix: the filter has the
captions in hand at judgement time and simply needs a stricter share threshold or a
personal-account signal.

## 3. Getting the email

Bio first, because the profile call has already been paid for; then the link-in-bio hop, which
is free HTTP. **No new caller was added to the quadratic regex.** `bio_parser._EMAIL_RE` is
still ~1.95s at 32k and ~4x per doubling, and `enrich_links` holds its own copy; both live
paths are capped, so this module reaches email text only through `ig_discovery.enrich_profile`
and `enrich_links.enrich_link` and never passes raw scraped text to the extractor. A new
uncapped caller inherits a freeze that arrives as a silent GIL-holding stall with no
traceback.

No email-finder vendor was bought. That category is name + company domain → permute, and
Hunter's own documentation says it cannot work with personal addresses. Meme pages are on
gmail.

## 5. The arithmetic he asked for

The chain, each link measured today:

```
pages found  --(filter passes 28.2%)-->  pages resolved  --(email 17.0%)-->  emails
```

| for | pages to resolve | pages to find | billed requests | dollars | hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **300 emails** | **1,765** | **6,259** | ~8,300 | **~$4.98** | **~5.1 h** |
| **250 emails** | **1,471** | **5,216** | ~6,900 | **~$4.15** | **~4.3 h** |

*Requests = one per page filtered plus one per page resolved, plus ~1 per 18 accounts
discovered. Dollars are that count × a config price constant — a price assumption, not an
invoice.*

**The binding constraint is time, and it is Instagram's rate limiter, not money.** ~$5 buys
300 emails; the same 300 cost five hours of wall clock. For Spotify the equivalent wall was
MusicBrainz at 1 request/second; here it is the per-request pacing on the IG client. Measured
tonight: 101 billed requests in 231 seconds ≈ **0.44 requests/second**.

**Two things would move it more than spending more:**
1. **Precision, not volume.** At 65%, ~35% of resolution spend is wasted. Lifting the filter
   to 90% cuts the pages needed for 300 emails from 6,259 to ~4,500.
2. **The 288 already in hand.** At 17%, resolving all 288 yields ~49 emails for ~$0.17 and
   twelve minutes — and 245 of them are existing leads that would go from DM-only to mailable.
   That is the cheapest 49 emails available and it needs no discovery at all.

## 6. The run

`--funnel meme_pages --target 40 --cap 0.60`, detached, incremental.

| | |
|---|---:|
| accounts discovered (10 hashtags) | **180** |
| already walked (seen-set + checkpoint) | 109 |
| judged | **71** |
| **passed the filter** | **20 (28.2%)** |
| rejected | 51 |
| **emails resolved** | **1** |
| billed attempts | **101 → $0.0606** |
| elapsed | 231 s |

**One email from 20 passed pages is 5%, against the 17% measured on known repost accounts**,
and the sample is far too small to treat as a second estimate — 1 of 20 has a 95% interval of
[0.9, 23.6], which contains 17%. The honest reading is that these two populations may differ
(hashtag-discovered pages are smaller and newer than the 288 already-catalogued ones) and that
one run of 20 cannot tell.

Every page was checkpointed as it was judged, flushed **and** fsynced per line, through the
same `run_resume.Checkpoint` the Spotify funnel uses.

> **The first run of this funnel produced 111 rows of nothing, and exited 0.** Its parser read
> `user_medias` items as bare media when they are `{"media": {...}}` wrappers, so every page
> was judged on twelve empty captions and rejected as *"no post carried a caption to judge"* —
> 117 billed requests, a clean exit, a plausible export, not one real verdict. The hashtag
> parser had the identical bug an hour earlier: `response.sections[].layout_content.medias[]`,
> three levels deeper than I guessed. **Both were already parsed correctly elsewhere in the
> codebase** (`ig_discovery._section_medias` and `ig_discovery._g`), and both now call that
> code instead of a second implementation. Those 111 verdicts were deleted, not kept: a void
> judgement in a seen-set would suppress 111 pages forever.

### Still broken, and whose

- **A found email cannot reach an existing master row.** `crossdedup.reconcile` merges the
  provenance tag (`repost_finder+meme_finder`) and leaves `email` empty — verified directly.
  So resolving the 245 known handle-only pages would update their *tag* and not their
  *address*. **This is the single biggest blocker to this funnel's value** and it is a change
  to shared merge semantics that affects all five funnels, so it is reported rather than
  quietly altered.
- **Filter precision is 65%**, and the check behind that number is name-based because my
  caption re-fetch used the wrong key. Both are mine.
- **`meme_format` is unchanged and still clip-tuned.** The caption sieve compensates in one
  caller; a second caller judging captions would need it too.
- **Two `memebot/` suite failures**: environmental, pre-existing.
