## The consistency number, first, because it decides everything

**No. Format is not all-or-nothing per account, and the free field is the one that fails.**

| signal | one-clip label accuracy | constant predictor | **lift** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `audio_type` (FREE, no audio) | 87.6% | **91.3%** | **−3.7 pt** |
| music label from `speech_frac` (~1.6 s CPU) | 78.8% | 61.9% | **+16.9 pt** |
| *BL-694 caption templating, for contrast* | *~98%* | *—* | *— all-or-nothing* |

Both have statistically real per-account structure (permutation null, p < 0.0001 each). But
**`audio_type` — the free discriminator BL-702 proposed — is worse than a constant** at the
account level. 81% of clips are `original_sounds`, so a single draw carries almost no
information about the page; "always guess original" beats it.

`speech_frac` does carry real signal, but 78.8% is nowhere near caption templating's ~98%.
**This is a measurement, not a free one-post label.**

### How many clips you actually need

Majority vote of *k* sampled clips against the account's own full-sample majority, 4000
resamples per *k*:

| k | accuracy | lift over constant |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 78.7% | +16.8 pt |
| 2 | 80.3% | +18.4 pt |
| 3 | 84.8% | +22.9 pt |
| 5 | 88.1% | +26.2 pt |
| 8 | **92.2%** | +30.3 pt |

A smooth curve with no plateau. That is the signature of **estimating a proportion**, not of
detecting a class. If it were all-or-nothing, k=1 would already be ~98% and k=3 would add
nothing.

## Why — the mechanism, and the most useful thing here

**Individual clips are unambiguous. Accounts are heterogeneous mixtures of them.**

Clip-level `speech_frac` is strongly bimodal — only **24 of 173 clips (14%)** land in the
0.30–0.60 middle:

```
0.0-0.1  47  ############################################################
0.1-0.2   7  ########
0.2-0.3   5  ######
0.3-0.4   9  ###########
0.4-0.5   4  #####
0.5-0.6  11  ##############
0.6-0.7  15  ###################
0.7-0.8  10  ############
0.8-0.9  22  ############################
0.9-1.0  43  ######################################################
```

38/173 clips scored exactly 0.000 (VAD found no speech at all); 32/173 scored ≥0.95. The
0.30 threshold sits in a genuine trough, so it is not slicing a continuum.

But **within-account** spread is huge:

| | |
|---|---|
| median within-account `speech_frac` IQR | **0.55** |
| accounts with IQR > 0.5 (wildly mixed catalogue) | **12 / 21** |
| accounts with IQR < 0.1 (tight, single-format) | **4 / 21** |

So the reason one post does not label a page is not measurement noise — each clip is read
cleanly. **It is that most pages genuinely post both kinds.** A page is a *mix ratio*, and
you cannot estimate a ratio from one draw.

That also explains why the discovery tag fails below: it is trying to assign a single label
to an object that does not have one.

BL-702 suspected this. Quantified:

| predictor | accuracy |
|---|---|
| discovery family → its own majority format (leave-one-out) | **38%** |
| constant ("always clip-first") | **62%** |

Every family spans nearly the whole range:

## Does format predict anything worth discovering on? Mostly no

Spearman ρ of account music share against each field, n=21 (|ρ| < 0.41 is not significant):

| field | ρ | edit-first median | clip-first median | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **median play_count** | **−0.06** | 4,809 | 4,971 | **nothing** |
| **median days between posts** | **+0.06** | 0.9 | 0.8 | **nothing** |
| caption templating rate | **+0.60** | 0.10 | 0.00 | significant, stable |
| median caption length | **+0.53** | 400 | 270 | significant, stable |
| reels returned | −0.35 | 12 | 12 | n.s. |

**The headline for lead quality: format does not predict reach or cadence.** Edit-first and
clip-first pages have statistically indistinguishable view counts (4,809 vs 4,971 median) and
post at the same rate (~0.85 days between posts). **Format is not a lead-quality signal, and
should not be used to rank or gate leads.**

Both caption correlations survive a jackknife (templating ρ range +0.55…+0.68; length
+0.47…+0.59), so neither is carried by a single account. But read the raw rates before
leaning on ρ: **17 of 21 accounts template at 0.00**. The correlation is really the statement
*"3 of 8 edit-first pages template heavily (0.25, 0.83, 1.00); 0 of 13 clip-first pages do."*
That is a real asymmetry on a sparse signal, not a smooth relationship.

**This sits in tension with BL-694**, which found templating on a *clip* page
(`clipcapture.tv`, 34/36 `Genre:` fields). BL-694 had n=3 accounts; this has n=21. The honest
reading is that templating is a page-owner habit that both formats can adopt, and BL-694's
association with clip pages was probably an artefact of its three-account sample.

**`follower_count` could not be tested.** It is absent from the `/v2/user/clips` payload
(0/273 clips) and would need a second paid call per account — $0.015 more, over the $0.02
budget.

## Method

- **Selection** (`scratch/acctformat_select.py`, free): 25 accounts drawn from
  `scratch/bl701/discovered.json` (317 accounts), stratified across BL-702's four families,
  seeded and sorted so the sample is reproducible.
- **Fetch** (`acctformat_fetch.py`, **the only paid step**): one call per account to
  `/v2/user/clips` — `ig_api.medias_endpoint` is already set to it — returning a page of that
  account's own reels. **25 calls, 273 reels, median 12 per account.** Metered via
  `record_aux_spend` as `probe:acctformat_percount`.
- **Audio** (`acctformat_audio.py`, free): up to 8 `original_sounds` clips per account
  downloaded straight from the CDN — **173 clips, 65.2 MB, 107 s** — then `speech_frac` from
  whisper-tiny VAD (`vad_filter=True`, int8, 6 threads), **718 s ASR**. Thresholds unchanged
  from BL-700 (<0.30 music/non-speech). BL-700's chroma/HPSS/peak features are **not** used,
  per its own finding that they failed to separate music from dialogue.
- **Scoring**: accounts need ≥8 reels and ≥4 measured clips → **21 of 25 scored**
  (`lzuu._edit4` 3 reels, `oggy_x_anime_` 2 reels, and two more fell short).
- Consistency is scored against a **permutation null** (4000 shuffles preserving each
  account's clip count), because purity on a two-class label is bounded below by 0.5 and a
  raw mean means nothing on its own.

Artifacts: `scratch/acctformat/` — `accounts.json`, `catalogues.json` (273 reels),
`speech.json` (173 measured), `consistency.json`, `accounts_scored.json`, `audio/`.

## Limits

- **Every music rate is a LOWER BOUND**, for BL-702's reason: `licensed_music` clips expose no
  downloadable asset, so `speech_frac` is only ever measured on `original_sounds` — the subset
  *least* likely to be music. Licensed clips are counted as music by declaration.
- **The singing confound is real and still unquantified.** 90/173 clips scored
  speech-dominant. Spot-checking their transcripts, most are clearly genuine dialogue
  (`"My favorite animal is the sea turtle"`, `"I'm not your wife. Stay calm."`), but several
  non-English ones are plausibly lyrics. 38/173 (22%) returned an empty transcript, which is
  the VAD correctly finding no speech. The direction of this bias is known — it *understates*
  music — but its size is not.
- **n=21 accounts.** Every correlation here has wide error bars; |ρ| below ~0.41 is noise.
  The family-level rows (n=4–7) are directional only.
- **8 clips per account, one page deep.** `/v2/user/clips` returns recent posts, so a page
  that changed format six months ago reads as its current format. Within-account consistency
  is therefore measured over a *short window* and is, if anything, **overstated**.
- **The 25 accounts came from one discovery pass** on 12 hashtags. Pages that never surface on
  those tags are unrepresented.
- Format classification uses a **50% threshold** on a continuous mix ratio. Accounts near the
  line (`torresmo_bh` 38%, `tokiocristal` 67%) are not meaningfully "a format" at all.

## What this changes

1. **Do not add a per-account format field at discovery time from one clip.** It would be
   wrong for roughly a fifth of pages using `speech_frac`, and *worse than a constant* using
   `audio_type`. If the field is wanted, it needs ~5–8 clips per account and should be stored
   as a **ratio with an n**, not a label.
2. **Drop the discovery tag as a format proxy.** 38% against a 62% constant. BL-702's
   per-family music shares describe tags, not accounts.
3. **Do not rank or gate leads on format.** No relationship to views (ρ = −0.06) or posting
   cadence (ρ = +0.06).
4. **The useful reframe: format is a clip-level property.** Clips are cleanly bimodal —
   14% ambiguous — so if the pipeline needs to know whether *a given clip* is music-first,
   that is answerable at ~1.6 s of CPU with no paid call. It is the *page-level* summary that
   does not exist.
